# V2.17 "Dog Days": contract and as-built notes

Goal (owner-approved, the proposal's "New features to keep players"): give players a reason to come back every day and every week.
v2.16 "First Week" made the first ten minutes guided; v2.17 adds a small daily wish, a Bond track to aim for, a diary and a sticker book to fill,
and letters in the morning mailbox. Nothing bad ever happens: no streaks, nothing lost on a missed day, no paid items.

Code: `game/src/48_dogdays.js` + `game/css/49_dogdays.css` (tag `dd`, test hook `window.__paw.dd`, `ddOn()`), with small hooks in
`00_core.js` (`v217Fields`, the two new `CLOTHES` rows, `ddBondSeen` in `addBond`, `ddUnlocks` in `unlocksFor`, the `stickers` act in `doAct`),
`47_firstweek.js` (the More tray gets Stickers, the v2.16 wish hands over to the daily wish), `07_treasure_journal.js` (Diary and Stickers tabs),
`35_photo.js` (the new frames), `25_guide.js` (Gerald's card stays off the wish chip). Art: `dogs/pawart_dogs.js` (the crown and the collar),
`world/pawart_world_e.js` (their item icons). Under the test harness everything needs `prefs.ddTest` (the older suites never see it). Suite: `v217_dog_days`.

## 1. The daily wish
- One wish a day for each dog (`S.dd.wish.w[dogId]`), seeded by the date and the dog (`seeded(hashId(day + '|' + id))`), picked from what the
  player can do at their Bond and place (`ddWishOpts`): ball, pets, a bath, a nap, a trick, a photo, always; a walk on a route the pack has unlocked
  (`ROUTES[k].bond <= topBond()`, not in Tokyo); a food in the bag (the dog's favourite first); a toy with a yard game; a trip to an unlocked town place;
  the Splash Pool from its Bond; the veggie patch from Bond 2; wearing something when the wardrobe has clothes.
- A crayon thought bubble under the place chip (`#ddWish`, yard and town places, hidden during ball play, photo mode, training and on slim HUDs)
  with the dog's head: "Today I wish for a sniff around Sunny Park". A tap explains it.
- Made true by the matching action (`trackAct`, the same events the missions use): a walk on that route, that food, that toy, that place...
  It gives: double the Bond the action gave (`ddBondSeen` records every `addBond`; the gain of that dog in the 4 s around the act is given again,
  at least `addBond(2)`), 10 coins (at most 20 wish coins a day, `DD_COIN_DAY`), and a sticker when one is due (one toast names all three).
- The v2.16 "see you tomorrow" wish (`S.fw.wish`, walk / ball / pet / snack) becomes that dog's daily wish the next day (`fw: 1`); it keeps its
  +10 Happiness, and the v2.16 handler stands down (`ddFwTook`) so nothing is paid twice.

## 2. The Bond track
- Tap the HUD Bond meter (or its "Next" line; `role=button`, keyboard too): "Mochi's Bond track", Bond 1 at the top to Bond 10, the dog's rung marked.
- Each rung lists the real gates, read from the tables at display time (`ddTrackRows`): tricks (`TRICKS`, the signature trick at 10), walk routes
  (`ROUTES`), places (`PLACES`, Tokyo `TK_PLACES` and the Station), the Boutique and toy shop (`TOYS`, `CLOTHES`, not festival, edition or reward
  items), the Gene Sniffer (`twTools`), houses, beds and home furniture (`HOUSES`, `BEDS`, `HM_DECOR`), dog spots (`SPOT_RULES`), playdates
  (`welfare.minBond`), the veggie patch and the Bond 2 party (Bond 2), the Best Friends badge (10).
- New gifts, given once when any dog first reaches the level (`S.dd.bt`; an old save already there gets them on its first v2.17 day):
  Bond 3, 5 and 7: photo frames (hearts, stars, flowers; `S.dd.fr`, photo mode `dd<frame>`); Bond 10: the **Crayon Crown** (head) and the
  **Golden Collar** (neck), `reward: true` (never sold), drawn in the crude crayon style with every fill on its own outline (`HAT2`, `NECK2`;
  the collar's tag is tucked away when the dog lies down). The level-up card lists the gift ("Gift: ...").

## 3. The dog diary (Journal > Diary)
- A page for every game day with something in it (1 game day = 24 real minutes), written by the active dog. The save keeps summaries only
  (`S.dd.today`, `S.dd.diary`, the last 60 pages: about 17 KB when every page is full, less on quieter days): routes, places, foods, tricks, a flag letter per kind of fun, the wish, the pack.
- The text is made at display time from templates (so it follows the language): an opener and a closer in the dog's voice (six voices from the
  breed personality trait: grand, bouncy, foodie, cuddly, sleepy, chatty), up to six lines of the day, the pack. A small doodle of up to three of
  the game's own icons. Flip with Back and Next; it opens on today's page ("Today so far").

## 4. The sticker book (Journal > Stickers, phones: More > Stickers)
- 12 pages of 6 stickers (`DD_PAGES`, `ddStickerDefs`): wishes come true (1-50), wish kinds, around town, out and about, the bigger town
  (Training Ground, Splash Pool, run tracks, Mt. Fuji Lookout), Tokyo (the station stamps count), festivals (leaf pile, parade, sweater contest,
  snowdog, snowdrift, New Year fortune), four seasons (+ Mt. Fuji, a photo), the Training Ground (runs, three rosettes, the gentle course, two dogs),
  the Splash Pool (the life vest, paddles, the sprinkler, floating fetch, two dogs), calendar days (the 3/7/12/18/24-day gifts, 30 paws) and a year
  of paws (paws in 2 to 12 months). Stickers are the game's own icons, items and Tokyo station stamps.
- A full page gives its gift at once: even pages an outfit (the next one not owned of Bow Tie, Rain Hat, Cowboy Hat, Pyjamas, Tutu, Chef Hat,
  Red Bandana, Sailor Collar, Yellow Raincoat, Polka-dot Raincoat: all Bond 1-2), odd pages and a full wardrobe a sticker photo frame.
- Counters: `S.dd.cnt`, places `S.dd.pv`, months `S.dd.mo`; old saves get their Tokyo stamps, pool vest, agility rosettes and calendar months counted.

## 5. Letters
- At most one Dog Days letter a day, on the first visit of the day (`S.dd.lt`): Gerald's town news once a week (8 short kind notes in turn,
  one a care tip from the vet), else, for players who adopted puppies out (`S.rehomed`), a short letter from a puppy's family every 3 days or so
  (6 templates in turn). The v2.1 postcards and gifts are unchanged. No selling words (checked in the suite).

## 6. Save fields (additive, `v217Fields`, S.v stays 1)
`S.dd = { wish, wn, wk, today, diary, st, pg, fr, bt, pv, cnt, mo, lt }` (full comment in `00_core.js`). A save from before v2.17 loads with
empty fields; junk is repaired; the diary is cut to its last 60 pages. The phone More tray shows a "new!" tag on Stickers until it is opened.

## 7. Economy
Walks stay the main income (about 115 route coins a day in the `v215_tracks` sim). Wishes pay 10 coins, at most 20 a day (the sim checks it);
the Bond track, the stickers and the letters give items and frames only.
