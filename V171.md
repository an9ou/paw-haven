- Each plot holds 3 drops.

## Part B: trick training mini-games (PLAY lane: game/src/15_trick_training.js and its css; it may add game/src/15b_trick_games.js after 15 in ORDER.txt)

Replace the click-to-mark training loop with **lure-training mini-games**. Real trainers lure a dog into a position with a treat, then fade the lure into a hand signal. The training side panel stays: trick chips, Focus, progress, the Train and Show off tabs. Nothing ever covers the dog.

### The core mini-game: Treat Lure (mouse follows the track)
1. Pick a trick, then press **Start**. A pencil-dotted **track** appears in the scene, starting at the dog's nose. It is drawn in the doodle style, with a start dot and an end star.
2. Grab the treat cursor at the nose and **drag it along the track**. The dog's head and body follow the treat: the dog leans, looks up, lowers or turns as you go, using poses and CSS transforms. Reaching the end star completes the pose: the dog does the trick, gets the treat, and hearts appear.
3. **Scoring:**
   - **Accuracy** is the share of the path inside the track tolerance, about 28 px each side.
   - **Pace:** going too fast makes the dog lose the treat ("Too fast! Noodle lost the scent."). Too slow is fine.
   - **Great** (accuracy ≥ 85%): +35% progress.
   - **Good** (≥ 60%): +20%.
   - **Missed:** 0, with no penalty and a funny line.
   - Let go of the treat early and you can retry from the last point, once.
4. **Fade the lure:** at 60% or more progress, the treat disappears. You trace the same track as a **hand signal** (a hand cursor with a fainter track), which gives ×1.3 progress, as in real training. At 100% the trick is **Learned**. A typical trick takes about 3–5 attempts.

### Tracks (logical, based on real lure-training motions)

| Trick | Track |
|---|---|
| Sit | from the nose, up and back over the head (a short arc). The dog's rear lowers into `sit`. |
| Lie Down | straight down from the nose to the floor, then slightly forward along the floor. Ends in `down`. |
| Paw | tap the dog's front paw 2× (2 tap targets), then hold an open palm still for 1 s. Ends in `paw`. |
| Roll Over | from `down`, a ¾ circle over the shoulder. Ends in `rollover`. |
| Spin | a full circle around the dog at body height. The dog walks the circle as a CSS spin. |
| Play Dead | a "finger gun" zigzag, then hold still for 1 s. The dog flops into `playdead`. |
| Bow | down to the floor between the front paws, then hold 1 s. Ends in `bow`. |
| Dance | drag the treat up high, then wiggle left and right 4× in rhythm. Ends in `dance`. |
| Speak | a **rhythm** mini-game instead of a track: the dog's ears perk on a beat, and you tap **Speak!** on 3 beats in time. The dog barks back with the breed voice. Accuracy comes from timing within ±120 ms (Great) or ±220 ms (Good). |
| Signature (Bond 10) | a 3-part combo of that breed's tracks. |

### Feel and logic
- **Focus:** each attempt costs 15 Focus, so a session holds about 6 attempts. It refills over time, as now.
- **Personality changes the track tolerance and progress:**

  | Dog | Tolerance | Other |
  |---|---|---|
  | Sunny (eager) | ×1.2 | |
  | Pepper (gentle) | ×1.1 | no "lost the treat" |
  | Mochi (stubborn) | ×0.9 | ×1.25 progress |
  | Biscuit and Dumpling (food-driven) | ×1.15 with the treat, ×0.9 in hand-signal mode | |
  | Peanut | | small dog, the track is drawn lower |
  | Rocket | | tall dog, the track is drawn higher; finds Lie Down easy and Sit hard (×0.85 tolerance) |
  | Bagel (nose-led) | | the treat track has a gentle "scent wobble", but ×1.2 tolerance |
  | Frost and Noodle | | use the breed modifiers already in v1.6 |

- **Treats:** +1 Hunger per treat, up to +15 per session, as before.
- **Feedback:** big readable feedback ("Great!", "Good!", "Oops") in Caveat, and the dog's reactions with barks.
- **Show off** stays, but each trick is performed by a quick hand-signal trace (a short version of the track, no treat). Combos and coins stay as they are. Mastered tricks get a wider tolerance.
- **The old "Good!" click marker and its timing loop are removed.** Keep the old stage data (progress %) so saves keep their progress.
- **Desktop mouse first.** Keyboard players get a fallback: arrow keys move the treat along the track.
