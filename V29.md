# V29: v2.9 "All Together" (Pack play & walks, Photo mode, Polish)

The owner picked these three on 16 Oct 2026. v2.8.4 made petting work for the whole pack. v2.9 finishes the job: **no switching dogs is needed for anything**, adds a **photo mode** players (and the owner's Instagram) can use, and clears the **open TODO list**.

This is the owner-level contract. The Coordinator adds the technical tables (data model, cross-lane API, art names, lane files, merge order) in section 5 before starting the lanes, the way `V27.md` and `V28.md` do.

Standing rules apply: nothing bad ever happens, walks stay the main income (~100 coins a day), save changes are additive (`migrate()`), every old save loads, 44 px taps and 15 px text on phones, the `copy` suite's words rules. No itch.io work (paused). No site deploy.

## 1. Pack play & walks (lane PACK)

The "active dog" stays as the leader (the HUD chips still switch it), but nothing needs a switch any more. Every tray that acts on dogs gets the v2.7 chip row (`crChipsHTML`, ticked = joins in), the same as Feed and Care.

- **Play tray.** The chip row on top. Ticked dogs join every game:
  - **Fetch:** the leader runs for the ball, the other ticked dogs chase along behind it (a simple run-along pose, no new mini-game). Each ticked dog gets the play effects and its own Bond once per session; the daily care bonus counts once.
  - **Toy play, Squeaky Duck, Tug:** every ticked dog gets the effects. Tug: the others pile on the far end of the rope (a pose, not new physics).
  - **Tricks** stay one dog at a time (training is per dog), but the Tricks card opens with a **dog picker** inside the tray. Training a dog never changes the leader.
  - **Playdates** are unchanged.
- **Walks.** Every ticked dog that is allowed out goes on the walk: the existing rules keep puppies under 3 months and nursing mums at home, with the kind line they already have. A ticked dog with very low Energy stays home for a nap, with a kind line, never a fail.
  - The leader leads; the others follow in a line on the route and in the runner.
  - Each walker gets the walk's stats and Bond. **Coins do not multiply**: a pack walk pays like one walk (a small "pack bonus" of at most +10% is allowed if the Coordinator wants one). Treasure finds stay one roll per walk.
- **Wardrobe.** Opens with a dog picker (the heads of every dog present). Dressing a dog never changes the leader.
- **Tap a dog anywhere** (v2.8.4) keeps petting. A long press (about 0.6 s) on a pack dog opens a small card: "Make leader", "Dress", "Train", for players who do want to switch.
- With one dog, or under the harness pin (`prefs.crOne`), everything is v2.8.4 exactly.

## 2. Photo mode (lane PHOTO)

- A **camera button** in the menu (and in the phone "..." sheet) in the yard, the house and every town place.
- Photo mode hides the HUD and trays and shows the scene with a **crayon postcard frame**: the place name, the date (Japan date), and the names of the dogs in the shot. A row of choices sets the frame colour and one of about 8 short preset captions ("Best day ever", "Nap champions", the season's line). No free text, so the copy rules hold.
- **Save:** renders the framed scene to a PNG (about 1080 px on the long side; one square 1080×1080 option for Instagram).
  - Phones use the Web Share sheet with the file when the browser offers it, so it can go straight to the camera roll or Instagram.
  - Otherwise the PNG downloads.
  - Where neither works (for example the claude.ai artifact sandbox), the picture opens full screen with "press and hold to save".
- The crayon fonts must be in the image (embed them, don't rely on page fonts).
- Photos are not kept in the save (save size). The journal counts "Photos taken". One daily mission: "Take a photo of your pack".
- A short line from Gerald the first time ("Say cheese. Or kibble.").
