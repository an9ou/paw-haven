# Paw Haven v1.5B: the phone version (portrait, touch)

Goal: the whole game plays well in a phone browser in portrait (360–430 px wide, 640–932 px tall). Landscape phones and tablets also work. **The laptop version (≥1024 px wide) must look and play exactly as it does now.** Use a breakpoint and a separate layout mode; do not reshuffle the desktop.

## Layout (game)
- **Mode switch:** on `matchMedia('(max-width: 820px)')` and on portrait aspect, set `html[data-layout="phone"]`. Recompute on resize and orientation change.
- **Phone stage, top to bottom:**
  1. **Compact HUD (about 56 px):** the active dog chip with a mood dot, its name and ♂/♀, and coins. Stats collapse into 4 small ring meters in a second row, about 40 px high, which you can tap to expand to the full meters. Settings and mute go into a ⋯ menu.
  2. **Scene viewport:** fills the remaining height above the action bar. The scene SVG uses `xMidYMid slice` with the focus on the dog area. Pick a viewBox crop that centres on x≈430 (where the dog stands), so the dog, the bowl and the right decor zone stay visible. Pan a little if the dog moves.
  3. **Action bar:** 7 icon buttons at least 48 px tall, with labels under the icons in small type, plus the safe-area inset at the bottom.
- **Popups:** become bottom sheets, full width, up to 85% of the screen height, with a drag handle. Swipe down or tap the dimmed backdrop to close.
  - Shop cards, the Journal and the Wardrobe grids reflow to 2 columns.
  - The purchase window becomes a sheet with big − / + buttons.
- **Text:** at least 15 px. Tap targets at least 44 px.
- **Map:** fits the width and can be panned or pinch-zoomed (simple drag pan is enough). Tapping a place opens a small confirm chip ("Go to Sunny Park") instead of a hover tooltip.
- **Hover-only interactions need tap equivalents:**
  - Petting is rub or drag on the dog with touch; a tap is a single pat.
  - The map uses tap-to-confirm.
  - Tooltips appear on long-press.
- **No horizontal page scroll. Respect safe areas.**

## Modules (touch and portrait)
- **`walkrun.js`:**
  - **Controls:** large on-screen Jump (hold for a long jump) and Duck buttons in the bottom corners. Tapping the right half of the screen jumps; swiping down ducks. The Dig button is big when it shows.
  - **Portrait:** the runner view keeps a landscape-shaped play strip, letterboxed and scaled to the width, with the HUD above and the controls below. The tutorial cards stack vertically.
  - Keep the timing fair at the narrower view. Obstacles must still give at least 1.8 s of warning, so zoom the camera out if needed.
- **`toys.js`:** every interaction works with touch (pointer events, `touch-action: none` on the play area). Throws work by flicking. The layout scales to portrait, with the play area on top and the header compact.
- **`garden.js` and `kitchen.js`:** touch drag for seeds and ingredients, with tap-to-select then tap-target as a fallback. Portrait layout:
  - Garden: plots in a 2×3 grid.
  - Kitchen: the pantry becomes a scrollable strip under the pot.

  The game agent owns these edits, keeping them minimal and changing only CSS/layout or input.
- **Audio:** no change, beyond starting after the first touch.
