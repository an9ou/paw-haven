# V2.18 "Fun Fair": contract and as-built notes

Goal (owner-approved): more ways to earn coins and new minigames, while walks stay the main income. Nothing bad ever happens: a slower or
clumsier round only earns less (a pinecone bounces off, Grandpa Joe delivers a missed paper, an empty box says "keep sniffing", Mrs. Plum shows the
card again). No selling dogs (the stall takes veggies and snacks only), nothing toxic (oat and pumpkin biscuits, plain treats).

Code: `game/src/49_funfair.js` (the switch, the caps table, the save, the place buttons and game cards, the game frame, the result card, the
stickers, the Saturday stall, the photo contest, the thank-you letter) + `game/src/49b_ff_games.js` (the eight games, `FF_DEF`) + `game/css/50_funfair.css`
(tag `ff`, test hook `window.__paw.ff`, `ffOn()`). Small hooks: `00_core.js` (mode `fair` in `go()`, `ffUnlocks` in `unlocksFor`, `v218Fields`),
`35_photo.js` (the contest button in photo mode), `48_dogdays.js` (two sticker pages through `ffStickerAdd`), `25_guide.js` (Gerald's Fun Fair card).
Art: world E (`PawArt.ffArt(name, o)`, names in `PawArt.FF_NAMES`: canopy, trunk, pinecone, bubble, kite, box, judgecard, stall, paper, door, biscuit,
tin, cloud, leaf, sponge, jug, towel; and the sticker items in `PawArt.V218_ITEMS`). Under the harness everything needs `prefs.ffTest`.
Suite: `v218_fun_fair`; the economy is in `v215_tracks`; the fit audit has an `ff` step.

## 1. The games (each 1 to 2 minutes, one finger on phones, mouse or keys on desktop)

| Game | Place | Bond | Play | Round pays | Day cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Treat Catch | Sunny Park | 1 | slide / mouse / arrows under the big tree; treats in the jar, pinecones bounce off; 60 s | 1 per 3 treats, at most 10 | 20 |
| Bubble Pop | Splash Pool | 3 | tap where the dog jumps (arrows + Space); golden bubbles 3; 60 s | 1 per 4 points, at most 8 | 15 |
| Sniff & Seek | Home Yard | 2 | a toy under 3 to 5 crayon boxes, a shuffle, the dog's nose lingers at the right box; tap or 1-5; 6 rounds | 2 first try, 1 second, at most 10 | 15 |
| Dog Show Pose | Town Square | 4 | Mrs. Plum's pose card with the dog's learned tricks (Stand tall and Wag fill in), remember, tap in order (or 1-6); a wrong trick shows the card again; 5 cards | 2 a clean card, 1 with a peek, at most 10 | 20 |
| Kite Day | Mt. Fuji Lookout | 5 | hold to lift (Space), let go to drift; clouds 1, leaves of the season 2; 60 s | 1 per 5 points, at most 8 | 15 |
| Newspaper round | Town Square, with Grandpa Joe | 2 | a fixed street of five doors; tap (Space) by a door | 3 a paper | 15 |
| Bakery helper | Pupuccino Café, with Baker Bea | 2 | sort oat and pumpkin biscuits into four shape tins before the crayon line runs out (60 s); a wrong tin is a short pause | 1 per 2 sorted | 15 |
| Neighbour dog wash | Home Yard, with the Tanakas | 3 | scrub (rub), rinse (hold), towel (rub) the visiting dog in the tub; 75 s; once a day | 10 for a full wash, less for part | 10 |

Every round: +3 Bond (with the BOOST, about 9 points), +5 Happiness, a little Energy (3 to 6), a count for the stickers. A round needs its Energy + 5.
Done mid-round keeps what was earned. A capped game still plays and still gives Bond.

## 2. The frame (smooth on every screen, the v2.16.1 approach)
Mode `fair`: `#view` holds a header (title, score, a crayon time line, Done, 44 px) and the play area. The field is 1000 units wide on desktop and
560 on phones (so sprites stay big enough to tap), and as tall as the view's shape. One `requestAnimationFrame` loop with time deltas (clamped to 50 ms);
sprites are divs moved with `translate3d` only; the dog is drawn through `dogArtSafe` once per pose and facing (a short rest delay so a jittery finger
does not redraw it). The scene behind is the place's own pencil art under a paper wash. On phones the bar, the place chip, the look buttons, Gerald's
card and the wish chip rest during a game (like trick training); the "next unlock" line rests on every layout.

## 3. The Saturday market stall (Market Street)
Saturdays only (local date). Pick up to six things: garden crops (Pip's price for the star, +1) and cooked dishes (3 + 2 a star); neighbours take them
home, at most 40 coins a Saturday. "Dogs are family, never stall goods." Text never pairs selling words with dogs (checked by `copy` and the suite).

## 4. The weekly photo contest
Photo mode shows "Enter in the photo contest (n/3)" until three photos are in for the week (Monday to Sunday). The entry keeps a summary only (place,
dog in the middle, dressed, dogs). The third entry is judged at once by Ms. Patel with kind rules: the dog in the middle 4 (else 1), an outfit 3 (else 1),
a place none of the other two shows 3 (else 1): up to 10 a photo, at most 30 coins a week, and a ribbon for everyone.

## 5. Stickers (the v2.17 book)
Two pages while ffOn (`DD_PAGES` gains `fair` and `ribbons`): Fun Fair (Treat Catch, Bubble Pop, Sniff & Seek, Kite Day, all three jobs, the stall)
and Ribbons (blue, red, gold show ribbons from the dog show, 5 shows, a photo contest ribbon, 3 contests). Without ffOn the book stays 72.

## 6. The neighbour's letter
A full or part wash sends a thank-you postcard from the Tanakas through the mailbox (`mailPush`, once a day).

## 7. Economy (V215.md 7; the sim in `v215_tracks`)
All caps in one table, `FF_CAPS` (real coins, no BOOST). At every cap the eight games give 125 a day, the stall about 5.7 and the contest about 4.3:
about 135 a day for all of v2.18 together, below walks (about 230 real coins a day in the sim). The sim sums every source (garden 60, the Square goal
30, missions 45, wishes 20, the calendar with the daily gifts about 8, ball play 40, agility 40, each v2.18 source) and checks walks are the largest
single source and that v2.18 together stays below walks; the old checks (no-Energy sources together below walks) are unchanged.

## 8. Save (additive, `v218Fields`, S.v stays 1)
`S.ff = { day, c: { game: coins today }, n: { game: rounds }, best: { game: best }, rib: { b, r, g }, mk: { day, coins, n }, pc: { wk, e, judged, won, rib }, wash: { day, n } }`.
Junk is repaired, bad counts dropped, at most three photo entries.
