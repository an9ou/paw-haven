## 1. Save (additive, `S.v` stays 1)

`v228Fields(s)` from `freshState()` and `migrate()` (00_core.js), one line each:

```
S.st = {
  stars: { <dogId>: n },        // the most Bond stars each dog has ever had (never goes down)
  ks: { 5: iso, 10: iso },      // the Bond star keepsakes, given once
  s: { <story>: { n, d, at, dn, fin, ks } }, // per story: letters arrived (0-5), requests done (0-5), date of the last letter,
                                // date of the last request done, the closing letter sent, the keepsake given (iso)
  lt: '',                       // local day of the last story letter (one story letter a day at most)
  pc: 0                         // postcards made (a count only)
}
```

Old saves: on load each dog at Bond 10 gets its stars computed once (`stars[id] = max(stored, computed)`); an old save already
at 5 or 10 stars gets its keepsakes on its first quiet tick. Nothing is ever taken away.

## 2. Bond stars

- After Bond 10 the Bond points keep counting (they always did). A star per `ST_STAR = 5000` Bond points past the Bond 10 line
  (`BOND_TH[9]`): `stars = floor((pts - BOND_TH[9]) / 5000)`, shown as `max(that, S.st.stars[id])`.
- A new star: one gold toast ("{name} earned a Bond star!").
- Shown on the Bond track (a stars block under the ladder at Bond 10, with the next star and the two keepsakes) and on the dog's
  journal profile card (a star badge next to the name), and the HUD Bond meter title.
- Keepsakes, once per save, when any dog first reaches the count:
  - 5 stars: **Framed Paw Print**, a house wall decoration (`HM_DECOR`, `reward: true`, `v228: true`, prop `stpawframe` drawn by
    `56_stories.js` through `PawArt.prop`), put out in the house like the Trophy Shelf.
  - 10 stars: **Star Collar**, a neck item (`CLOTHES`, `reward: true`, `v228: true`): a blue crayon collar with a gold star tag
    (`NECK2['Star Collar']` in `dogs/pawart_dogs.js`, the wardrobe icon in `ITEMS_V217` of `world/pawart_world_e.js`, the Golden Collar's style).

## 3. Neighbour stories

Four townsfolk, five steps each (`ST_STORIES`). Each step is one mailbox letter (id `st_<story>_<step>`, kind `news`) with one small,
kind request. The request is done only by doing it (the act bus, `on('act')`):

| kind | done by |
| --- | --- |
| `go` | travelling to the place (`travel`, `place`) |
| `walk` | any finished walk |
| `photo` | a photo taken at the place (`photo` while `S.place` is it) |
| `plant`, `water`, `harvest` | the garden acts |
| `bath`, `pet`, `trick` | those acts |

Pacing (no deadline, nothing is ever lost):
- One story letter a day at most (`S.st.lt`), only in a quiet yard (like the Dog Days letters).
- The next step's letter comes at least `ST_GAP = 3` local days after the last request was done.
- A step's letter waits (never skips) while its request cannot be done yet (a place not open, no garden).
- Story 1 starts once any dog is at Bond 2. Each later story starts when the one before has 2 requests done, and its own gate is open.
- The 5th request done: the keepsake (a photo frame for photo mode, `S.dd.fr` id `k_<story>`) and the story's page in the journal's
  Stories tab. The closing letter comes on a later day.

The stories:
1. `plum`: Mrs. Plum next door learns to knit a dog sweater (walk, Market Street, deliver to the café, a bath, a photo at Sunny Park). Keepsake: the wool frame.
2. `rosa`: Rosa from the café adopts Comet, a shy retired greyhound, from the Shelter (no money, only a promise) (café, a walk, a photo at the café, a trick, Sunny Park). Keepsake: the café wall frame.
3. `pip`: Pip the farmer grows a carrot for the town show (plant, water, harvest, deliver to the Town Square, a photo at the Town Square). Gate: the veggie patch. Keepsake: the ribbon frame.
4. `post`: the mail carrier makes friends with dogs, and with the left shoe (a pet, a trick, the Vet Clinic, a walk, a photo in the yard). Keepsake: the stamp frame.

Where the player sees them:
- the letters in the mailbox;
- the Monday help-wanted board (the Week tab, `24d_week.js`): an open story request shows as a pinned note under the five board
  notes ("Neighbour stories"); it does not count for the board;
- the journal's **Stories** tab: one card per started story (five dots, the open request, the letters so far to reread),
  and a finished story's page with its keepsake.

## 4. Share postcard

A postcard button on the yard (desktop: the top bar icon after the camera; phones: the "..." menu), on the dog diary page and in
My Dog Card. It reuses the v2.19 Share poster path (`50_friends.js`: `PawArt.FRIENDS.poster()` backdrop and `posterBox`, `frPosterLine`,
`frQRSVG`, `phLoad`/`phDataURL`, the share sheet or a download). Only game words go out: a headline per screen, the dog's crayon drawing
(no name: names are typed), the friend name and code and the QR when the player has a friend code (else the doodledoggo.app link),
the Dog Card name (picked words) for the Dog Card postcard. No typed text leaves the device.
