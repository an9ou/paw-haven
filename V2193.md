# v2.19.3 "Puppy start" (contract)

Owner, 5 Nov 2026 (choice window): "The players should have a puppy at the dog shelter instead of a dog, they can enjoy watching the puppy grow up."
A small update on v2.19.2. Suite: `v2193_puppy`. Save stays `v: 1`; everything is additive.

## 1. New players: the starter is a 3-month puppy

- Every starter adopted at the shelter arrives at **3 months** (`lifeStage` puppy): the first dog (`freshState`, 00_core.js) and any later
  starter (`mode === 'adopt'` / `kind === 'starter'` in 30_shelter.js: `srStarterLook`, `srRoomList`, `srAdoptFlow`; the shelter list's
  starter cards in 13_more_dogs.js `shelterCard` draw the puppy too). One helper: `starterMonths()` / `PUP_START = 3` (10_sex_age_genes.js).
- Shelter residents and rescues keep their own random ages. `addDog`'s default (`spec.months || 10`) is unchanged: only test hooks reach it.
- Age model unchanged: 1 real day = 1 dog month, so the puppy is a puppy for 3 days (3, 4, 5 months), young from day 4, adult from day 10.
- The meet dog's speech bubble in the playroom sits lower over a puppy (`srSayUp`).

## 2. Old players: their starters get their puppy months back, once

`pupStartFields(s)` (00_core.js), called in `migrate()` right after `linkDogs` (so a dog with no `born` is left as it is). Self-contained,
so the v2.22 Town levels migration merges beside it. Runs once: `S.pupStart = 1` marks it done; a new save has it from `freshState`.

- A **starter** is a dog with no `rescue` record and no `parents` (adopted as a 10-month starter).
- New age m' = max(m - 7, start of lifeStage(m)): born moves (m - m') days later. Stage starts: young 6, adult 12, senior 84. A puppy is left.
- **Dogs that have had puppies keep their age**: `litters > 0`, `lastLitter`, expecting (`preg`), nursing (a litter's `mum`), or the parent
  of any dog in `S.dogs`, the nursery (`S.litters`), `S.npcLitters`, the rehomed list or the family tree. The welfare rules on age stay true.
- A female's `seasonSeed` moves with the birthday, so her season phase stays. The family tree record (`S.tree[id].born`) follows.
- `d.pupBday` = the last birthday the dog had already reached (12, 24, ...); `birthdayCheck` skips a birthday at or below it, so the shift
  never throws a party, and a year the dog already celebrated is not celebrated again.

| Before | Stage | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10, 11 | young | 6 |
| 13 | adult | 12 |
| 30 | adult | 23 |
| 88 | senior | 84 |
| 100 | senior | 93 |
| any, has had puppies / parent / expecting / nursing | | unchanged |
| shelter resident or rescue, born pup (parents), no birthday | | unchanged |

Other things keyed on `born` stay sane: Dog Cards and the friends snapshot read the current age; Growing up (24f_grow.js, behind
`MS2_LIVE`) shows the current stage's moments and a stage is never gone back to; the diary and the calendar don't read the age.

## 3. No dead end for a puppy in its first days

- Puppy's first day with Gerald (47_firstweek.js): pet, feed, ball and tag work at 3 months; the walk step already says "try the Sunny
  Park", which a puppy walks at 0.7 length (`walkScale`). The walk's Bond is not scaled, so Bond 2 still lands on the walk home.
- The daily mission (24_missions.js): `msCanWalk(k)` (some dog in the pack can walk route k, `walkBlock`) gates `walk_river`, `new_trail`
  and, for Missions v2, `ms2FreshRoutes` (`walk_fresh`). With a grown-up dog in the pack they come back.
- The daily wish (48_dogdays.js `ddWishOpts`): a walk wish only names routes that dog can walk (a puppy: the Sunny Park).
- Checked and fine at 3 months: the pool and agility (blocked under 3 months only), the Fun Fair (under 3), trips to Tokyo / Japan
  (`staysHome`, under 3), the Training Ground and tricks (under 2), the welcome calendar, the Bond 2 party (its route unlock is a list,
  and the route carousel says "Not for {name} yet" with the kind puppy line).
