# Merch part 7 review: the legal page drafts (25 Nov 2026)

One combined review (text against the contract and the code, legal sanity at research level, layout, the suite) of commit e237298:
`site/merch/terms/`, `shipping/`, `seller/`, `tokushoho/`, `legal.css`, `merch/test_legal.js`, `docs/design/merch/PART7.md`.
Written by the MERCH session from the reviewer's report. Research, not legal advice.

**Verdict.** The four drafts are well built and behave on screen: noindex, the draft banner, no inline script, nothing off the origin, no
console errors, the layout fits at 360, 390 and 1280. The tone is plain and kind and no word rule is broken. They are not yet true to the
code: delivery times come from the page's stand-in catalog, not from the Worker's; the Japanese notice misstates the order limit; the UK/EU
row offers a zone whose countries are all shut; the delay notice timing and the liability and 30-day wording need work. The suite is green
(304/304) but checks against the fixture and only matches a few phrases, so it lets several of these through.

"Where": **P7** = fixed in the part 7 fix batch (pages, PART7.md, `merch/test_legal.js`). **W** = needs the Worker or the shop page, so it
goes into the part 6 fix batch (those files were being changed by part 6 during this review).
