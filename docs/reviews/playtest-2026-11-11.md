## Findings (for the Game Manager)

1. **Regular players hoard from day 3.** Buying two things a day, a regular player still has 1,835 coins on day 7; everything open at
   town level 5-6 costs 45-180, and the first 1,200+ item opens at level 13 (about day 22). In the regular shops, days 3-20 have no mid-size
   goal; the only one is the kind shop's coin looks (1,500, live since v2.24), which sit in a separate sheet (the script does not open it).
   Making those looks easy to find, and a first town project (Fountain 5,000), would fill this gap (see `docs/design/balance-2026-11-11.md`).
2. **Two hours a day progresses like 45 minutes.** Keen reaches town level 7 on day 6-7, regular level 6: the 150 XP daily cap flattens
   it. Keen also reaches Bond 10 on day 6 (regular Bond 8-9 on day 7), so the Bond track is a first-week goal, as the economy review said.
   Keen spends every coin daily on small items, which is fine for week 1 but runs out once the cheap shop is owned (sim: day 54).
3. **Keen: "walk did not start" on days 2 and 3** (once each, late in a long session, after naps). The route Start was pressed and the
   game stayed on the route screen with no toast the script could read. Check by hand: a long session, 6+ walks, energy and hunger low.
4. **Many daily missions need a minigame or a trip** (agility rosettes x4, Splash Pool float fetch, Fun Fair, Mt. Fuji Lookout, a trick
   in the Square, Tokyo, cook a new dish, photo contest). With one free swap the swap often lands on another such task. A light player
   who only feeds, pets and walks finishes the mission 3 days in 7, so the stamp card fills slowly for exactly the players it should keep.
   Worth checking the story weighting for new players (levels 1-5).
5. **Quiet mail for light players.** After the welcome letters on day 1 (Halloween Market, leaf festival, a neighbour's parcel) the next
   letter came on day 7 (Gerald's news, Harvest Market). Days 2-6 had only a small purchase as "new". A daily small letter or the
   "While you were out" page (improvement plan release A) would help.

Also seen: no stuck windows and no console errors in 21 player-days; the second feed of a session sometimes found no food tray (the dog
was still eating: a script timing issue, not a game bug); the route carousel always opens on the place's route (Sunny Park), so light
players never try another route unless they swipe.
