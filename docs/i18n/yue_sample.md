# Cantonese voice sample (for the owner to approve)

About 40 real lines from the game, so you can hear the Cantonese voice before the whole game is translated. Each row: the English, today's Traditional Chinese (written for Taiwan), and the new Hong Kong Cantonese. "Mochi" stands for your dog's name, and DOG-XXXXX for a friend code. The first column is the line's name in the game files, so you can point at a row. The full rules are in `yue.md`.

## Five choices to confirm

1. **Puppy = 狗BB** everywhere (the way Hong Kong people say it), not 小狗.
2. **Play = 玩耍** on the bar, simple as you asked. 一齊玩 is warmer but longer.
3. **Hong Kong names for breeds and food**: 唐狗 (the shelter mutt), 金毛尋回犬, 比高, 芝娃娃, 哥基, 八哥犬, 松鼠狗; 提子, 朱古力, 甘筍, 番薯, 士多啤梨 (the food-safety lessons use the words kids see in shops).
4. **Hong Kong jokes**: the Chihuahua is 八號風球, the Husky sings 大戲, the Corgi is a 方包, Mrs. Plum is 布冧太太.
5. **The website** in the same spoken Cantonese, the privacy page included.
