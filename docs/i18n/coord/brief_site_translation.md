## v2.14 site translation brief (phase 1b), shared by the five translator lanes
Read docs/i18n/coord/brief_common.md first (rules, contract). Setup: `git fetch origin integration/v2.14 && git checkout -B lane/v214-tr-<NAME> origin/integration/v2.14`.
Task: translate the doodledoggo.app site into your two languages: write `site/i18n/<code>.json` (codes exactly: ja, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ko, es, pt-BR, fr, de, th, id) with the same keys as `site/i18n/en.json` (about 716 keys, ~4,000 English words: all pages, meta/OG, alt/aria, the 35 dog profiles, playroom barks, 404). Read `_notes` in en.json (length limits, placeholders, what not to translate: URLs, the katakana dog names) and `_meta` (set `complete: true` in yours when every key is done).
- Follow `docs/i18n/<code>.md` (register, tone, punctuation, word rules) and `game/i18n/glossary/<code>.json` (breed names and terms must match exactly). Keep every placeholder and ICU plural/select exactly (`{count}` etc.; translate inside plural branches). Keep the jokes landing in the language: cozy, kind, simple, playful, the crude crayon dogs are the joke. Two-sentence care lessons stay two sentences. Never "breed" as a verb, "stud", "litter price", "sell" for dogs, "made in Japan".
- The privacy page: translate faithfully and add (one key if en.json has a fitting slot, else in the intro line) that the English version is the one that counts if the two differ; ask nothing, just do it in the same register.
- Write the translation with your own language skill, carefully, section by section (don't machine-dump; read your output back once per section). Do not edit en.json, templates, or any tool; if a key can't be translated well because of the English structure (e.g. word order baked into the template), note it in your report.
- Check: `node tools/site_i18n.js --report` (0 missing, 0 broken, 0 word-rule hits for your languages), `node game/test_i18n_node.js` (ALL OK), `node tools/site_i18n.js --pseudo` not needed. Then build the fonts for CJK/Thai languages: `pip install --user fonttools brotli` once, then `node tools/i18n_fonts.js --lang <code> --title-keys "$(node tools/site_i18n.js --title-keys)"` (see docs/i18n/FONTS.md for the exact flags; skip for Latin languages) and `node game/run_tests.js site` (must pass). Look at your two languages' home and dogs pages once at phone width (Playwright screenshot to your scratchpad) for overflow, broken line breaks and missing glyphs (tofu); fix what you find.
- Commit (`site/i18n/<code>.json` and your generated `site/fonts/i18n/<code>*` files only), push `lane/v214-tr-<NAME>`, report in 15 lines at most: keys done, test results, what needs a native check (also list them as `TODO(native)` lines appended to `docs/i18n/<code>.md` under a "Site" heading).
