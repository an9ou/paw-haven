Hello, and thank you! Doodle Doggo is a cozy browser game: you adopt a crayon-drawn dog, drawn ugly-cute on purpose, then feed it, play, go for walks and grow a garden. Nothing bad ever happens in the game.

The words are spoken Hong Kong Cantonese on purpose, and kids read them too, so they should be simple and kind. The dog jokes are meant to be silly. Please fix anything that sounds wrong, Taiwanese or Mandarin, too formal, or rude.
