- Nav: ホーム / 犬たち / マップ / 作者より. "About Us" is 作者より (the page is the maker's own letter); わたしたちについて was too long for the phone.
- Map page title and h1: おでかけマップ; `map.h1_gloss` keeps `{chizu}` (地図 chizu).
- About Us is written as the maker's letter in warm です/ます, first person わたし (neutral: the maker's gender is not stated; changed from ぼく by the site lead).
- Hachikō: 忠犬ハチ公.
