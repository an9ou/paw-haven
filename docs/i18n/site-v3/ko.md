# Site v3, Korean (ko): translator notes

99 new keys in `site/i18n/ko.json` (nav, map page, About Us, itch.io box). Place names are the glossary terms.

Choices:
- Nav: 홈 / 강아지들 / 지도 / 소개. The nav links have 12–16 px side padding, so a bare 홈 stays a 44 px tap (the footer keeps 홈으로).
- No particle after a placeholder: `map.shot_alt` is `Doodle Doggo 속 {place}`, `about.mail` ends with `: {mail}`.
- About Us in warm 해요체, first person 저 / 저희. "We don't live on our own yet" is 아직 독립하지 않았어요. Donations: 후원하실 수도 있어요 (optional). No 분양 anywhere.
- Zoomies on the beach became 모래 위 달리기 (owner: simple words). Hachikō: 충견 하치코.
