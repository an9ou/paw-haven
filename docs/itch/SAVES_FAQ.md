# Saves: questions and answers

**Where is my game saved?**
In your browser, on the device you play on. It saves by itself while you play, so there is no Save button.

**I played on itch.io and now I opened doodledoggo.app. Where is my dog?**
The itch.io game and doodledoggo.app are two different places, and browsers keep their saves apart. Your itch.io dog stays on itch.io. To bring it over, use a save code (next question).

**What is a save code, and how do I use it?**
It is a long piece of text that holds your whole game. Open **Settings**, press **Export save code**, and copy it. Keep it somewhere safe, like a note on your phone. To load it, open the game anywhere, go to Settings, press **Import save code**, paste it and confirm. That is also how you move your game to a new phone or computer. Export one now and then as a backup.

**My save disappeared. What happened?**
The most common cause is **Safari**. Safari clears the stored data of websites and of games shown inside a web page (like on itch.io) if you do not visit for a while, and also when you clear history or use private browsing. Other browsers can do the same if you clear site data or play in a private window. To stay safe: play in a normal (not private) window, do not clear site data, and export a save code from time to time.

**Is there a login?**
Not yet on itch.io, but it is coming. You will be able to make a free account with an email and your own password (no emails are ever sent). Your save will then be kept online and you can play the same game on any device. Guest play will always stay free and complete.

**Will my current save work when the login arrives?**
Yes. Your dogs will not be lost. You will be able to make an account and your save will carry over. If you want to be extra safe before that, export a save code first.

**Can two devices share one save right now?**
Not automatically. Until accounts arrive, move your game with a save code. When you import a code, it replaces the game on that device, so export from the device that has the newest game.

**Can I share my save code with a friend?**
It will load your whole game on their device, as a copy. It is fine to do, but treat it like a private backup.
