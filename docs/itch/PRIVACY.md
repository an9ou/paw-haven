# Doodle Doggo privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026. Written to go live together with the Cloudflare login. A game by an9ou.

**In short:** Doodle Doggo has no ads and no tracking. If you make an account, we keep your email, a scrambled version of your password and your game save. If you use Friends, we keep the list in that section. Nothing else.

## What we collect

- **If you play as a guest:** your game is saved in your browser, on your device. If you are online, a guest copy is also kept with the login service so it isn't lost, with no email or name attached. Friends, if you turn them on, are kept as the Friends section says.
- **If you make an account:** we store
  - your **email address**, only so that you can log in. We never send emails, not even to say hello.
  - a **hashed password**. A hash is a scrambled version of your password that cannot be turned back into the password. We cannot read your password, and nobody else can either.
  - your **game save**, so you can play the same game on any device.
  - the **dates** of your account and your latest save.

We do not collect your name, your age, your location, your contacts or anything else.

## What we do not do

- No ads.
- No tracking, no analytics and no cookies from advertisers.
- We never sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
- We never email you.

## Friends

You can use Friends as a guest or with an account. It starts when you pick a friend name.

- **What friends can see:** your friend name of two words from a list, your dogs' looks, and your yard and decorations as you last saved them.
- **No free text.** Notes are preset kind lines only. Nothing you type is sent.
- **No search.** A friend is added only by a code or an invite link.
- **What we store:** your friends list, blocks, your yard snapshot, and pets, notes, gifts and walks. Pets, notes, gifts and walks are kept for 30 days.
- **Remove and block** are quiet. The other player is not told.
- **Leave Friends** in Settings deletes it all: your code, your friends list, the blocks you made, your yard snapshot, and the pets, notes, gifts and walks. A block someone else made stays.

## Where it is kept

Account data and saves are stored with Cloudflare, who run the login service. They keep the data only so the game can load and save it. Where you play (doodledoggo.app, GitHub Pages or itch.io) the game runs in your browser. itch.io has its own privacy policy for its own site.

## Children

Doodle Doggo is a gentle game for everyone. If you are young, please ask a grown-up before making an account. You never need an account: guest play is complete.

## Your choices

- **Play as a guest** at any time, with no account.
- **Export a save code** in Settings whenever you like, to keep your own copy.
- **Delete your account and everything we hold:** contact the owner at **[CONTACT EMAIL: to be filled in by the owner]** from the email address you used to sign up, and say you want your account deleted. We delete the account, the password hash, the saves, any Dog Card and your Friends data (your code, friends list, blocks, yard snapshot, and pets, notes, gifts and walks), and tell you when it is done. Deleting your account does not remove a save code you exported or the guest save on your own device. You can clear those yourself.
