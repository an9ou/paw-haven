# Doodle Doggo privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026. Written to go live together with the Cloudflare login. A game by an9ou.

**In short:** Doodle Doggo has no ads and no tracking. If you make an account, we keep your email, a scrambled version of your password and your game save, and nothing else.

## What we collect

- **If you play as a guest:** nothing leaves your device. Your game is saved in your browser, on your device only. We never see it.
- **If you make an account:** we store
  - your **email address**, only so that you can log in. We never send emails, not even to say hello.
  - a **hashed password**. A hash is a scrambled version of your password that cannot be turned back into the password. We cannot read your password, and nobody else can either.
  - your **game save**, so you can play the same game on any device.
  - the **dates** of your account and your latest save.

We do not collect your name, your age, your location, your contacts or anything else.

## What we do not do

- No ads.
- No tracking, no analytics and no cookies from advertisers.
- We never sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
- We never email you.

## Where it is kept

Account data and saves are stored with Cloudflare, who run the login service. They keep the data only so the game can load and save it. Where you play (doodledoggo.app, GitHub Pages or itch.io) the game runs in your browser. itch.io has its own privacy policy for its own site.

## Children

Doodle Doggo is a gentle game for everyone. If you are young, please ask a grown-up before making an account. You never need an account: guest play is complete.

## Your choices

- **Play as a guest** at any time, with no account.
- **Export a save code** in Settings whenever you like, to keep your own copy.
- **Delete your account and everything we hold:** contact the owner at **[CONTACT EMAIL: to be filled in by the owner]** from the email address you used to sign up, and say you want your account deleted. We delete the account, the password hash and the saves, and tell you when it is done. Deleting your account does not remove a save code you exported or the guest save on your own device. You can clear those yourself.
- **Ask what we hold** about you in the same way.
