# How cozy games keep players: suggestions for Doodle Doggo (4 Nov 2026)

Asked by the owner on 4 Nov. These are suggestions only; nothing is decided. Missions v2 (`docs/design/missions-v2.md`) already covers dailies, progress badges and the new-player notebook, so it is not repeated here.

## What cozy games do (the patterns that work)

| Pattern | Seen in | Why it works | Doodle Doggo today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Something happens while you are away | cat-collecting idle games, self-care pet apps, idle bunny games | Coming back is a reward, never a punishment | Hunger and needs only; no gift or story waits for you |
| Rare visitors you attract | cat-collecting games (toys and food bring rare cats) | A reason to check in, and a collection with visible gaps | Rehomed visitors at a few places, not in your own yard |
| Tomorrow hooks | farming and island-life games (crops, buildings, a parcel next day) | The player leaves with a reason to return | Garden only |
| Long shared goals | town-building games (bridges, community projects) | Big coin sinks that feel good | Not built (economy review option 3) |
| Neighbours with small stories | island-life and farming games | Players come back for people, not coins | Gerald, puppy families' letters |
| Gentle streaks | language apps (streak freeze), self-care pets | Habit without guilt | No streaks (good); no "days played" count |
| Welcome back after a break | most live cozy games | Brings lapsed players back kindly | None |
| Seasons that come back each year | island-life games (seasonal fish, bugs, events) | Calendar moments without FOMO | Festivals and markets (good) |
| Self-expression to share | decorating and dress-up games | Players stay to make it "theirs" and show it | Yard, outfits, photo contest, Friends (v2.19) |

What cozy games avoid: punishing absence (villagers leaving, dead crops, broken streaks), timers that demand attention, paid speed-ups, and FOMO items that never return. Our "nothing bad ever happens" rule already matches the best of them.

## Suggestions (best value first)

1. **"While you were out" (small, high value).** When the player comes back after 4+ hours, the dog greets them, then a page from the dog's diary says what it did: napped in the sun, found a sock, watched a bird. Sometimes a small gift (a sticker, a toy, a treat; coins rare and capped). One page a JST day. Returning always feels good.
2. **Yard visitors (medium, the strongest loop).** Put toys, bowls and decorations in the yard; neighbourhood dogs (the 35 breeds, named, with coats) visit while you are away, play, and sometimes leave a thank-you gift. Rare visitors need a certain toy, season or weather. A Visitors Book with silhouettes for the ones not met yet. It gives yard items a purpose and is a natural coin sink.
3. **Town projects (medium, the economy fix).** The town saves up together for a Fountain, Bandstand, Sakura Avenue, Lighthouse (the economy review's option 3): players drop coins in, each project takes days, and then it is built in the town for good. Solves "nothing left to buy" and gives a "come back tomorrow" moment.
4. **Days-played calendar that never resets (small).** Count days played, not days in a row: gifts at 7, 30, 100, 365 days. A missed day costs nothing.
5. **Welcome back (small).** After 7+ days away: Gerald's letter ("we missed you"), a care package, and a short "what's new" page. No catch-up grind.
6. **Tomorrow hooks (small, mostly text).** End each session with one gentle "tomorrow" line: the seed sprouts tomorrow, a parcel comes tomorrow, the Saturday stall opens in 2 days. The toast after the last daily task is a good place.
7. **Neighbour stories (medium, writing heavy).** 4-6 townsfolk with 5-step story arcs over weeks, told through letters and small requests (ties into the Missions v2 weekly board).
8. **Share hooks (small).** A "postcard" button on the yard, the diary and Dog Cards, using the Share poster from v2.19. Friends visiting each other is the best long-term retention we have.

## Measuring it

Keep reading the retention dashboard (D1, D7, D30) before and after each change, one change at a time where possible, so we know which idea worked.

## Suggested order

Missions v2 (being built) -> 1 "While you were out" + 4 days-played + 5 welcome back (one small release) -> 2 yard visitors -> 3 town projects -> 7 neighbour stories.
