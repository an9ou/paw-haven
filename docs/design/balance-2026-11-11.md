## Kind shop coin prices (v2.24.2): fine

Looks 1,500 = 1.6 days of a regular player's net; tools 6,000 / 6,000 / 10,000 = 6.4 / 6.4 / 10.7 days. All inside 1-120 days. Buying
everything cheapest first, a regular player gets the last look on day 34 and the Robo Scoop on day 71; a light player on days 83 and 172.
They push "everything owned" from day 55 to 67 (regular) and 120 to 162 (light). The Supporter Pack (+20% walk coins) adds about 13% to a
regular player's income (931 to 1,052 net a day).
