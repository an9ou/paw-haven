"My dog" merch page: the phone flow at 390 px

Design prototype for doodledoggo.app/merch. Prices: PROVIDERS.md suggestions (not final). Placeholders are marked with dashed yellow tags.

1. Landing

One line, three things with crayon dogs on them, "Use my dog", and for visitors with no dog: Play first and four ready-made famous breeds. FAQ below.

2. Pick your dog

The player's dogs as cards (head, name, breed), favourite first; puppy and outfit tags. Also designed: the itch.io link state and the no-dogs state.

3. Pick a thing

Six equal tiles, the chosen dog already on each, the delivered price, "made near you" from the country.

4. Make it yours

A big honest preview (and the flat print file), pose, background, name (20 letters), colour, size, how many; the price bar stays in reach.

5. Check and pay

The item and options, where it goes, delivery time, total, our promise, "ask a grown-up", then Stripe's own page (prototype stops at a hand-off sheet).

6. Thank you

Order received, where it is now (4 stages, tracking link), a small in-game thank-you gift idea, the order recap.
