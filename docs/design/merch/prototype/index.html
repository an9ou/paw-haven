      Questions

      How do I bring my dog from the game?

      Open Doodle Doggo, tap More, then Put my dog on merch. This page opens with your dogs on it. Playing on doodledoggo.app? Your dogs are already here when you open Merch in the same browser.

      Where is it made, and how long does it take?

      Each order is printed just for you by our print partner: in the USA for US orders and in the UK for UK orders (anywhere else: the nearest one). Printing takes 2 to 5 working days, then about 3 to 4 days in the post in the USA and about 2 in the UK. You get a tracking link by email.

      Can I send it back?

      Because it is printed just for you, with your own dog on it, we can't take it back if you change your mind. But if it arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, tell us within 30 days with a photo and we reprint it or refund you, whichever you like.

      Which size should I get?

      The tees, sweatshirts and hoodies are unisex adult sizes. Lay a top you like flat, measure from armpit to armpit and compare with the size guide. Between two sizes? Pick the bigger one.

      Is my dog's picture private?

      Your dog's picture is drawn in your browser and sent only to the print shop, to print your order. We never show it anywhere, and we delete the print file 90 days after it arrives. placeholder period Your dog's name is printed only if you turn it on.

      Why does my dog look like that?

      It's meant to. The wobbly crayon lines are the joke, and the printer keeps every one of them.

      Does it change anything in my game?

      No. Your dog and your game stay exactly as they are. Nothing you buy here makes a dog happier or faster: they already love you.
