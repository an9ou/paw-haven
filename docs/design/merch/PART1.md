# Part 1: print art ready for real products (24 Nov 2026; review fixes the same day)

PawPrint makes the final print files for the launch products, the **tee and the mug**, as PRINT_FILES.md decided. Nothing is live. The review (`docs/reviews/MERCH_PART1_review.md`) items 1-11, 14, 15 and 17 are fixed below; 12 (game art), 13 (patch corners) and 16 (the order server) are not part 1's.

**No stickers at launch (owner, 24 Nov 2026).** The sticker sheet is switched off: `PRODUCTS.stickers.launch` is `false`. `printHash` and `printFromHash` refuse it (the print page shows `ERR product off`) unless a test passes `testOnly`. Its layout code and tests stay as they were, with one fix shared with the garments: a sticker no longer cuts art that reaches past its art box. No sticker samples for the owner.

## What was built

- **One table, `PawPrint.PRODUCTS`** (`merch/pawprint_core.js`): for `tee`, `mug` (`launch: true`) and `stickers` (`launch: false`) the file size, 300 dpi, inches, the Printful product, placement and technique (tee 71 `front_large` `dtg`; mug 19 `default` `sublimation`; sheet 505 `default` `digital`), the layout numbers, the poses (stand, sit, happy) and the colours. The merch page preview, the print page and the tests all read it.
- **`PawPrint.print(recipe, { product, colour, pose, name })`** returns the whole print SVG at exactly the product's size. Caller's slips throw: a bad recipe, product, colour or pose; a name string that `nameOk` refuses (`name: word|letters|empty|long`) or that cannot print at least 87 px tall on that product (`name: long`); art whose ground shadow is not found exactly once (`shadow`). `name: true` (or left out) prints the recipe's name, `false` none. `print.last` holds the numbers (scale `k`, `artMM`, `dogIn`, the real drawn box `dogBox`, the `nest` box, `box`, `patch`/`patchIn`, the name's size and box).
- **The whole drawing, never cut.** The game draws each dog in a 240 x 200 art box but some drawings reach past it: an Akita's sitting tail (5.5 units), sparkle stars over a Witch Hat (3.8), hearts. The print measures each drawing's real box (strokes included; `PawPrint.printArt`), nests the art with that box plus 4 units, and places it by that box. The scale stays the tee's (a puppy still prints smaller than an adult). If a drawing would leave the safe print area, it moves first, and only if that is not enough that one dog shrinks just enough.
- **Tee**: 4500 x 5400, transparent. 12.5 px per art unit (the art box would print 10 x 8.33 in). The drawing is centred on the tee, its top 1 in (300 px) below the top of the print area, and its name goes under it (gap 90 px, letters at most 1.3 in, one line in a 9.6 in band). Safe area: 0.5 in in from every edge of the file. No ground shadow.
- **`minDogIn`** (`PRODUCTS.tee.minDogIn`, inches, **5** for now, 0 = off; the owner chooses, `samples/puppy_size_choice.png`): a dog whose drawing would print narrower is scaled up to it. With 5: newborns and many puppies print 5 in wide, adults keep the art-box scale (Beagle: newborn 2.8 to 5.0 in, puppy 4.1 to 5.0 in, adult 5.9 in). Scaling up makes the crayon lines thicker in step, as in the game.
- **Tee colours** (`PawPrint.TEE_COLOURS`, made in both the US and the UK): White #FFFFFF, Soft Cream #E7D4C0, Athletic Heather #CECECC, Pink #FDBFC7, Heather Prism Ice Blue #C3E2E3, Navy #212642, Black #0C0C0C, each with US, UK and "both" sizes. Every colour but white has `patch: true`.
- **The paper patch**: a cream card with a wobbly crayon edge that hugs the drawing and its name with an even 0.4 in margin (a newborn's patch is about 6 x 6 in, an adult Lab's 10.5 x 8.5 in). It always holds the whole drawing and the name (`patchIn`, tested over 720 tees).
- **Mug**: 2700 x 1050. The same dog on both faces (centred at 2.25 in and 6.75 in), facing the back of the mug, the name above each dog, a yard strip along the bottom. Dog and name stay 0.25 in from top and bottom and 0.5 in from both ends (the handle). The dog fills its face by its real drawn box; its art box is never under 80 mm (smallest over 40 breeds, 3 ages, 3 poses, dressed and plain: 80 mm). The name has a 3.5 in band per face, as tall as its line needs at 87 px and at most 190 px; a long name tilts less (2 degrees down to 1). The mug is white: no background colour, so no band edge shows on the glaze.
- **Sticker sheet (switched off, code kept as is)**: 1749 x 2481, transparent. Nine stickers, as DESIGN 9: stand, sit, happy (one scale), a big head, a small head on a round badge, a name label ("good dog!" when the name is off), a heart, a paw, a bone. Each sticker is the art over a white backing 0.06 in wide (PRINT_FILES 4). Sparkle stars are left off the sheet; the happy pose's hearts get a white bridge to the dog.
- **Line weight**: no stroke under 0.5 mm in any file (`minLine`), counted through every transform. **The name too**: it is filled outlines, so its thinnest line is the font's. Caveat Bold's strokes are at least **0.068 em** (`FONT_THIN_EM`: the 2nd percentile of the local stroke thickness over the ink of every allowed letter; the test measures it from the outlines: 2nd 0.068, 5th 0.073, median 0.090 em). So letters are never under 0.5 mm / 0.068 = **87 px (0.29 in)** at 300 dpi (`NAME_MIN_PX`, `PRODUCTS.*.nameMin`). A name never shrinks under that: the mug's name band is wider (3.5 in, was 3 in) and grows with the name; a name that still cannot fit is refused for that product. Twenty W's (no space to break at) fit the tee (199 px) but not the mug: `nameOk(s, { product: 'mug' })` gives `{ ok: false, why: 'long', say: 'A little shorter, please', max: 16 }` (`max`: how many of its letters would fit). Two lines were tried and dropped: two lines at 87 px need about 200 px of the 900 px face, which would push the dog under 80 mm. On XS-M tees Printful shrinks the whole file x0.767, so lines and letters print at 77% there (0.38 mm); the rule is on the file.
- **Names** (`merch/names.js`, no dependencies; also inside PawPrint): `nameClean(s)` and `nameOk(s)`. At most 20 characters, trimmed, one line, only what the font draws (Latin with accents, digits, space and `' - . & !`), and a letter only when the font also has its other case (so Ă, ă, ÿ, Ÿ are all refused; ı pairs with I). A refusal is `{ ok: false, why: 'empty' | 'long' | 'letters' | 'word', name, say }`, never a throw, so the page can say why kindly.
- **The kind-word list** (ROT13 in `names.js`): English plus a short list for the game's other Latin-script languages (es, pt-BR, fr, de, id). Matched on a normalised copy (accents off, leet digits, marks as spaces), per word: as typed (so "xxx" is caught), with runs of 2+ letters squeezed ("Fuuck", "Sshhiitt"), runs of 3+ squeezed to 2, and said aloud (ph to f, v to u: "Phuck", "Fvck"). SUB words are matched inside single words only, WORD words as whole words; names spelt in single letters ("f u c k", "A.S.S") are joined. Words that are also everyday romaji or names ("shi"+"t" in Toshitaka, Yoshito, Ashita; Thai "Porn") are WORD-only, and a small allow list keeps Scunthorpe, Penistone, Atwater, Therapist, Pussycat, Nazira and "Moby Dick". The tests hold both lists (48 that must be refused, 49 that must pass) and every dog name the game itself gives (150).
- **`PawPrint.ART_VER`** (`pp1-` + a hash by `tools/merch_build.js` of the art, genes, font, name rules, core, the print page `merch/print/v1/*` and the build wrapper) is in every print SVG as `data-pawprint` and a comment. Store it with each order.
- **The print page** `merch/print/v1/` (`index.html`, `print.js`). The build copies it with `pawprint.js` to `merch/dist/print/v1/` **and** `merch/dist/print/<ART_VER>/`, so once the site keeps the old folders an order is redrawn by the build it was previewed on. Link: `#p=<one dog>&prod=tee|mug&col=<colour>&pose=stand|sit|happy&n=0|1&av=<ART_VER>`, **every key required**, any order (`PawPrint.printHash(recipe, o)` writes them all; `n=1` only for a dog with a name). `printFromHash` refuses unknown, repeated or missing keys, a wrong colour or pose, two dogs, a bad dog, another build's `av` (`ERR art version`), a product not on sale, `n=1` with no name, and a name that cannot print (`ERR name`). The page then checks the viewport: `innerWidth x devicePixelRatio` and `innerHeight x devicePixelRatio` must be exactly the file's pixels, or it draws nothing (`ERR viewport ...`). Title: `READY tee-navy-stand-n1 4500x5400 pp1-xxxxxxxx` (product, colour, pose, name on/off, size, ART_VER) or `ERR ...`. Its CSP allows no requests but its own two scripts. Screenshot it with the viewport at the product size, or half of it at deviceScaleFactor 2 (the tee), and compare the title with the order before saving (`tools/merch_render.js part1` does).
- **The ground shadow** is removed by matching the art's markup, so it is counted: exactly one shadow found and removed and no faint ink ellipse left, or `print()` throws `shadow`. The tests check all 27,000 drawings and three changed arts (no shadow, a moved one, two).
- Part 4 (the print render) starts from the Browser Run + Durable Object path of RENDER_FREE.md section 8. The spike's `merch/print_worker.mjs` and its test are deleted (git keeps them): they drew the old layout, capped the size under the tee's and had no auth.

## Edges on coloured fabric

- The patch is drawn with `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`, and it is the only thing that touches the fabric: the drawing and the name lie inside it. Every soft crayon pass is composited onto cream. Measured on the Chromium render: alpha is only 0 or 255 in every navy and black tee file, and for every patched colour.
- On white tees (part 9 fix m6) the drawing and the name lie on a crisp-edged **white backing** (`PRODUCTS.tee.backing`: every shape in white, 2.4 px wider, as the sticker sheet's backing; `<g class="pp-back">`), so a white tee file too has only alpha 0 or 255, in Chromium and in the 12-band resvg fallback. White is not printed on a white tee, so it looks as before; the backing carries no ink, so the 0.5 mm line rule does not count it. The mug is sublimation, where soft edges are fine.
- Pixel post-processing (flatten alpha at 0.5) is only needed if a renderer ignores `crispEdges` (Chromium and resvg both honour it), or if we ever print on colour without the patch.

## How to run

1. `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node tools/merch_build.js` (PawPrint, ART_VER, the print page in `merch/dist/print/v1/` and `merch/dist/print/<ART_VER>/`).
2. `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node merch/test_pawprint.js`: 84 checks, about 2.5 minutes; the last 13 render the real print page in Chromium. Without Playwright it ends `SKIPPED 13 rendered checks` and exits 1, unless `MERCH_ALLOW_SKIP=1`. Among them: the real-bounds scan (40 breeds x 3 ages x 3 poses x every head and body item, plus dressed, sparkly and left-facing: 27,000 drawings, 189 reach past the art box, none cut), the pixel test that the Akita's tail and tall hats meet their drawn box and never the clip edge, line widths as Chromium itself computes them (independent of `weight()`), the font's stroke measured from its outlines, ART_VER changing with each hashed file, and every refusal of the page.
3. The game round trip: `node game/build.js && node game/harness.js merged && NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node merch/test_merch_game.js` (483/483 drawings equal the game's).
4. Samples: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node tools/merch_render.js part1 [out] [sheets]` renders the 8 sample dogs (`merch/sample_dogs.js`: the Akita sitting and a Dane in a Witch Hat among them) through this build's print page, saving each file only when the title is READY for exactly that file (else exit 1), full size to `merch/dist/part1/`, and writes the mock-ups in `samples/`: `tee_white.png`, `tee_dark.png` (navy and black), `mug.png`, `puppy_size_choice.png`. (PNGs are git-ignored: add them with `git add -f`.)
