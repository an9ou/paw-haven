# Doodle Doggo: brief introduction (24 Oct 2026, v2.14.2)

For bios, press, partner messages and "what is it?" replies. English only.

**One line**

A free cozy dog game where everything is lovingly sketched in pencil, except the dogs: they drew themselves in crayon.

**Short (about 60 words)**

Doodle Doggo is a free cozy dog game you play right in your browser, on phone or computer, no download. Adopt one of 35 wobbly crayon dogs, care for it, dress it up and walk a pencil-sketched, Japan-style town all the way to Tokyo. Grow a garden, raise a pack of four, and nothing bad ever happens. Now in 11 languages.

**Longer (about 120 words)**

Welcome to a little pencil-drawn town where every fence post and flower box is sketched with care. Then someone handed the dogs a crayon. They drew themselves, and they are very proud of the result.

In Doodle Doggo you adopt a wobbly crayon pup from 35 dog types, each with its own voice and jokes. Feed it, pet it, bathe it and dress it up. Take it on walks that turn into a little runner game, play ball in the yard, snap photos, and explore the map from your backyard to Mt. Fuji and a trip to Tokyo. Grow a dog-safe garden, cook treats, and grow your family up to four dogs. The seasons follow the real calendar.

Free, no download, no ads, in 11 languages. Play at https://doodledoggo.app or https://an9ou.itch.io/doodle-doggo.
