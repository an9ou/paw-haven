# Children's video series (owner, 3 Nov 2026)

Owner: "I want to create a series of children videos ... that allows parents to let their child watch." Seven series were suggested in chat; the owner picked two for pilots (choice window, 3 Nov 2026): **Dog manners** and **Bedtime Walk**. English first; RedNote (Chinese) cuts only after the owner approves the English ones.

## Rules for every kids' episode (what makes parents say yes)

- **Calm.** Slow camera moves, no fast cuts, no flashing, no sudden loud sounds; soft music at a low level (-20 LUFS or quieter under the voice); a clear goodbye ending.
- **No selling.** No "play now", "download", link or QR inside the episode, no prices, no coins talk. The logo may appear small on the last card; the game link goes only in the description, written for parents.
- **One small true thing per episode** (a dog-safety rule, a number, a colour). Dog facts and safety rules come from vets and dog-safety bodies (see "Sources" below) and are checked before the owner sees them.
- **Read-along subtitles**: big, simple words, one short line at a time, inside the safe area; text centred with `centre_text.js` (`centreCheck()`), dots after numbers ("1. ask first").
- **Narrator:** Noodle, the soft Kokoro voice (English `af_bella`; slower than the Reels, about 0.9x, short sentences, warm). Kids' words: no sarcasm, no "ugly", no scary words.
- **Design rules still hold:** nothing bad ever happens, no selling dogs, real welfare and food safety, no black or white dog as the subject of an episode (owner, 1 Nov 2026), colour fills inside the lines, the game's real art only (no game UI).
- **Formats:** YouTube long form 16:9 (1920 x 1080), 2–4 min, set **"Made for kids"** (COPPA: comments off, no personalised ads; that is expected and fine); plus a vertical 9:16 cut of 30–60 s for Shorts, and for Instagram/TikTok aimed at parents ("a calm 3-minute video for your little one"), since those apps are 13+. RedNote later (亲子 audience).
- **Each episode gets its own cover** (16:9 thumbnail for YouTube; 9:16 cover with the 3:4 middle for the vertical cut).

## Pilot 1: "How to say hello to a dog" (Dog manners, episode 1), about 2.5 min

Noodle shows each rule the right way, with a friendly dog (pick one at random, not black or white, not Noodle; the dog's grown-up is a game person, e.g. from the town families or Gerald, if the art has people; else the grown-up is only a hand on the lead and a voice). The child is "you": the player's crayon hand (the trick-signal hands, as in the cuddle Reel).

| Beat | Noodle says (draft) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| Hello | "Hi, friends! I'm Noodle. Today I'll show you how to say hello to a dog. Gently!" | Noodle in the yard, waving tail |
| 1. Ask first | "Before you pet a dog, ask the grown-up: may I say hello? If they say no, that's okay. We can wave instead!" | the dog on a lead with its grown-up; a wave |
| 2. Let the dog sniff | "Stand still and hold your hand low, like this. Let the dog come to you and sniff. Sniffing is how dogs say hello!" | the hand low and still, the dog sniffs (sniff pose) |
| 3. Gentle pets | "Pet softly on the side or the back. Slow and gentle. Not the face, not the tail." | the hand strokes the dog's side; hearts |
| 4. When the dog walks away | "If the dog walks away, it means: that's enough for now. Let it go. That's being a good friend." | the dog trots off happily |
| Also | "And when a dog is sleeping or eating, we let it be." | a dog asleep in its bed; a dog at its bowl |
| Recap | "Let's say it together: 1. ask first. 2. let it sniff. 3. gentle pets!" | three big numbered cards |
| Bye | "Bye-bye, friends! See you next time." | Noodle in his bed or waving |

Later episodes of the same series: "Be a tree" (a dog you don't know runs up: stand still, arms folded, look at your feet), "Dogs don't like hugs" (pet the side instead), "Quiet around sleeping dogs", "Reading a dog's tail and ears", "Gentle with puppies".

## Pilot 2: "Noodle's Bedtime Walk", about 3.5 min

A slow walk through the sketchbook town as day turns to night (the game's `dusk` and `night` times), counting things, saying goodnight to each place, ending with Noodle asleep in his bed. For winding down at bedtime.

| Beat | Noodle says (draft) | On screen |

Music: the game's own night music, very soft; ambience (crickets, waves) where the game has it. Voice slower and softer than pilot 1. No end-card text except the small logo.

## Sources to check the dog-manners rules against

Every rule in the script must match them; note the source next to each rule when the script is final.
