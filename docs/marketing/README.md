# Marketing: owner's standing rules

For every Marketing session (branch `marketing/launch`). Read these with `CLAUDE.md` before starting.

- **Captions are not sent in chat unless the owner asks (owner, 19 Oct 2026).** The owner usually posts later, so captions sent in chat get lost. Write every caption, alt text and hashtag set into the right doc in `docs/marketing/` and tell the owner which doc and section to find them in. When the owner asks for captions ("send me Noodle's caption"), paste them in chat.
- **Images and videos are sent with SendUserFile** when a piece is finished.
- **Every Reel gets a designed cover (owner, 23 Oct 2026).** Not just a frame from the video: a cover page made for it, with a short title, the art and the logo. Keep everything that matters inside the middle 1080 x 1440 (y 240 to 1680), which the profile grid shows. Send it with the video.
- **Ads only (owner, 23 Oct 2026).** From now on Marketing makes Reels and pictures for ads, nothing else (no devlogs, carousels or posts made only for the feed unless the owner asks).
- **A different dog each time (owner, 23 Oct 2026).** Do not always use Noodle: pick a dog at random from the 35 for each new ad (for example the Chihuahua), and avoid repeating the last one or two. Dogs used lately, newest first: Mochi the Shiba and Waffles the Corgi (Friends how-to preview, 7 Nov: Mochi is the dog of our real Friends account and its poster), the Chow Chow (snack ad 2 at the pier, 3 Nov), the Border Collie, the Great Dane and the Cane Corso (update posts, 25 Oct), the Westie (party Reel, 25 Oct), the Mastiff (11-languages post, 23 Oct), the Chihuahua (snack ad, 23 Oct).
- **Breed profiles in their real coats are fine (owner, 7 Nov 2026).** The "no black or white dog as the subject" rule (1 Nov) does not stop a breed's own profile in its real game coat: the owner OK'd Sprinkles the Dalmatian, Fondue the Bernese and Pavlova the Australian Shepherd (dog-36..38). For ads and Reels the rule still applies.
- **Colour stays inside the lines (owner, 25 Oct 2026).** Every colour fill must sit inside its outline, in game art and in anything we draw for ads (no fills drifting off the lines). Passed to the Game Manager for the existing and future game art. Already-made Reels are not re-rendered (owner: no need). **Fixed in the game in v2.15.2 "Inside the lines" (25 Oct 2026)**: all 35 dogs, puppies, outfits and town art; merge main before making anything new so it uses the fixed art. The site's dog art (`site/img/art/`) is redrawn too.
- **"3D" means depth with the real dogs (owner, 23 Oct 2026).** When an ad should feel 3D (like the cozy-game ads with 3D animals), keep the game's real crayon dog and build the depth around it: layers moving at different speeds, depth of field, soft round shadows, light rays and floating light, a slow camera move. Never redraw the dogs in 3D or shade them: the ad must show the dogs players get.
- **Fixing a video: re-render only the changed part (owner, 21 Oct 2026).** Never re-render a whole video for a fix to one part. Keep the frames of the last full render (copy its workdir), re-render only the frames that changed and encode again with the same music. The birthday Reel takes `FRAMES=a-b` for this (see `BIRTHDAY_DOG_REEL.md`); give any other video script the same option before fixing it. Re-render the whole video only when the change touches every frame (timing, length or something on screen the whole time).
- **English only, no Japanese (owner, 21 Oct 2026).** No Japanese text in images, videos or captions, and no Japanese hashtags, for everything made from now on. (Older captions in these docs still carry a Japanese line; drop it when you reuse one.) Japan-style scenery in the art is fine.
- **Words:** never "ugliest"; never "breed" as a verb; never "sell" for dogs; never "made in Japan" (say "Japan-style"); no 🇯🇵 flag. The build also rejects "nintendo", "pokémon" and "animal crossing".
- **The game is live:** say "play free on itch.io" (https://an9ou.itch.io/doodle-doggo), link in bio. Instagram is @doodledoggo.app.
- **Support link (24 Oct 2026, from the Game Manager):** the owner's Ko-fi page is https://ko-fi.com/doodledoggoapp (bio and first post: `docs/marketing/KOFI_INTRO.md` on main). Use it as the support link in future posts, alongside itch.io.
- **Site links (21 Oct 2026):** the game plays right on https://doodledoggo.app (say "play right on doodledoggo.app", never "tap Play at itch.io on our site"). The How to play page, https://doodledoggo.app/guide, is the link for "new here? start here" posts.
- **No Threads account (owner, 9 Nov 2026).** Don't write Threads captions or Threads versions; caption sets cover Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X and RedNote. `THREADS.md` and `brand/threads/` are old and unused.
- **The Noodle story videos never show game UI.**
- **Reports:** no progress updates; report when the whole task is done, and send the Game Manager a one-line done note.
- **Every video's end card (owner, 4 Nov 2026):** the game logo, the website (doodledoggo.app) and our Instagram, X and YouTube, each with its icon and handle. Instagram @doodledoggo.app, X @doodledoggoapp, YouTube @DoodleDoggoApp (owner, 4 Nov 2026). Shared card: `brand/kids/endcard.js`. This replaces the kids' rule of "the logo only" on the last card. RedNote still never shows a link, a domain or a QR.
- **Dogs never crowd each other (owner, 6 Nov 2026).** Dogs that share a shot never overlap or touch: leave clear space between them. Mark each dog's group `data-apart` and call `apartCheck()` (from `SAFE_JS` in `brand/instagram/launch/centre_text.js`, 28 px minimum gap) on every frame next to `centreCheck()` and `safeCheck()`, as `breeds_reel/build_breeds.js` does; the build stops if two dogs come too close.
- **Comment asks offer something back (owner, 6 Nov 2026).** When a Reel asks people to comment their dog, add what we give back, e.g. "comment your dog 👇 / we'll draw it for you ✏️", on screen, in the voice and in the captions.
- **Content approval is never assumed (owner, 7 Nov 2026).** The 5-minute rule does not approve content. Final cuts, RedNote versions and anything "ready to post" are made only after the owner explicitly approves the English piece; until then, wait (previews may be updated on request). The Friends launch finals and RedNote cut of 6 Nov were made without approval and the owner has changes.
- **RedNote end cards may carry a QR code to the game website (owner, 7 Nov 2026).** RedNote Reels can end on the RedNote end card plus a QR code that opens https://doodledoggo.app (made with the game's own QR code module). Still no clickable link in RedNote captions; the search line "浏览器搜 Doodle Doggo App 就能玩" stays.
