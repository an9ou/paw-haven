## Episode 1: How to say hello to a dog (Dog manners)

**YouTube title**

How to Say Hello to a Dog 🐶 Dog Manners for Kids with Noodle

**YouTube description**

We made this 1½-minute video for kids aged about 3 to 7 who can't wait to pet every dog they see. Noodle, our crayon dachshund, shows four gentle steps with his friend Bagel the Beagle:

1. Ask first. Ask the dog's grown-up: "May I say hello?"
2. Stand still. Let the dog come to you and sniff.
3. Pet softly, on the shoulder or the side, then stop after three strokes and see if the dog wants more.
4. Let it go. If the dog walks away, that's enough for now.

And when a dog is sleeping or eating, we let it be.

For grown-ups: we checked every step against dog-bite-prevention advice for children from the AVMA, Dogs Trust, the Royal Kennel Club and Ireland's HSE. It's a start, and it doesn't replace you, so please stay close whenever your child meets a dog, even one they know well.

There are big read-along words on screen. We kept out ads, shouting and fast cuts.

Noodle and Bagel come from Doodle Doggo, the free cozy dog game I draw like a pencil sketchbook (it's for grown-ups and older kids): https://doodledoggo.app

## Episode 2: Noodle's Bedtime Walk

**YouTube title**

Noodle's Bedtime Walk 🌙 A Calm Goodnight Story for Kids
