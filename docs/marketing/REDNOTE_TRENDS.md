# RedNote: what's working and what to post next (30 Oct 2026)

Owner, 30 Oct 2026: on RedNote the food-safety Reel (狗狗能吃吗, 7 foods dogs can't eat + safe swaps) did very well; the trick-or-treat Reel, the freestyle dance Reel and the hello poster were fair. Done with the `ig-viral` method (look at what works, find why, turn it into posts we can make), adapted to RedNote.

## How sure this is

- **Our own data:** 1 strong post against 3 fair ones, with no numbers yet. That is a direction, not proof.
- **Outside data:** web research only. RedNote hides notes and their view counts from search engines, so the skill's "outlier multiple" (a note's views divided by that account's usual views) couldn't be measured. The sources are platform figures and marketing articles (listed at the bottom), and they agree with each other but aren't independent.
- **To make it solid:** after each post, add its numbers to the table at the end (views, likes, 收藏, comments, new followers). After 6–8 posts, `ig-audit` can say what works from our own evidence.

## Why the food-safety Reel won

1. **It answers a question people type.** RedNote works like a search engine for everyday life (the platform claims 800 million searches a day). "狗狗能吃…吗" is the question every new owner searches. "第4扇门" and a dancing dog match no search at all.
2. **It's worth saving.** A list of 7 foods with safe swaps is 干货 (useful content) that people save and come back to. The cover even said "先收藏". On RedNote, saves keep a note showing up in search long after posting.
3. **Its audience is dog owners, not gamers.** Every dog owner is the audience, and the game shows up as a nice extra ("在这个游戏里，有毒的食物做不出来"). The story, dance and hello posts only interest people who already care about the game.
4. **It fits the place:** pet health and care is a strong, growing RedNote topic (see sources), and Halloween is a small festival in mainland China.

What the outside research adds:
- game makers find **picture posts** (图文) do well on RedNote even when simple, and cost less than video;
- **life-related, personal posts** (a developer's story, everyday dog life) beat pure game showcases, in one indie developer's case by "dozens or even hundreds of times";
- the topic **#我在小红书做游戏** has 18.5 billion views, and indie teams that post at least once a month can join RedNote's **独立游戏开发者联盟** (Indie Game Developers Alliance), which offers guides and traffic boosts;
- **city dog walks** (城市小狗漫游, pet raincoats, pet-friendly places) are a rising topic;
- pet owners are most active **18:00–23:00**.

## What to post next

The main idea: make **狗狗能吃吗 a numbered series** (系列, 第2期, 第3期…). Each one is useful for dog owners and is all the game's own reviewed text. The game has 24 safe-food tips (`data.food.*.tip`) and 14 unsafe-food lessons (`data.people_fb.*`, `data.extra_unsafe.*`) in zh-Hans, so the series can run for months without writing new food facts. One pillar of useful posts, one of personal posts.

| # | Title (draft, 20 characters max) | Format | Why now / why it should work | The game's text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 冬天的烤红薯，狗狗能吃吗？ | 图文, 5–6 cards | Roast sweet potato season; a real search question. Answer: yes, cooked and plain, never raw; pumpkin the same way. | `sweet_potato_chews` "一定要煮熟、原味，不吃生的。生红薯很难消化。", `pumpkin_puree`, `sweet_potato_coins` |

| 3 | 包饺子的时候，别让狗狗靠近 | short video + cover | A Chinese kitchen moment, before 冬至 (21 Dec): onion and garlic, cooked or raw, and rising raw dough. | `people_fb.onion`, `people_fb.garlic`, `extra_unsafe.raw_dough` |
| 4 | 我把35只狗都画丑了，是故意的 | 图文, owner's voice | A developer's story under #我在小红书做游戏, using the /about story. Personal, not a sales pitch. **The owner writes or approves the wording.** | site `/about` |
| 5 | 下雨天遛狗，游戏里也会湿透 | short video | City dog walks and pet raincoats are trending; v2.18 wet dogs and raincoats. Light and cute, no welfare claims. | v2.18 rain (`wet: true`) |
| 6 | 评论区报品种，画成蜡笔丑狗 | 图文 + replies | Comments drive reach on RedNote; the ugly-cute look is the joke. Reply with the game's crayon version of their breed (35 breeds). | `make_dogs.js` |

How often: about 3 a week, posted 18:00–23:00. Alternate a useful post (1, 2, 3) with a personal or fun post (4, 5, 6).

A test worth running: post #1 as a picture post and #3 as a video, then compare saves. If picture posts save as well, they're the cheaper default for the series.

## What to do less of on RedNote

- **Dance Reels and Western-holiday stories** (trick-or-treat): keep them on Instagram, Shorts and X, where they fit.
- **Pure game showcase or hello posts:** fold the game into a useful or personal post instead.

## Rules this plan keeps

- Food facts only from the game's reviewed zh-Hans text: no doses, no first aid beyond "不小心吃了？马上联系兽医。". 韭菜 (Chinese chives) isn't in the game's text (it says 韭葱 and 细香葱), so a dumpling post leaves it out unless the game text is reviewed and extended first.
- No link on RedNote: "搜索 Doodle Doggo".
- Nothing bad ever happens: a useful post always ends on the safe swap, never on harm.
- English first only applies to Reels made for every platform. RedNote-only posts are made in zh-Hans and need the owner's go-ahead before they're made.
