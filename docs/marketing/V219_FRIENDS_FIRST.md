## What's new for players

- **Friends:** every player gets a friend name made of two Dog Card words, picked on a name wheel (e.g. "Sunny Biscuit"), and a friend code (`DOG-XXXXX`). Share it as the code, a QR on your card, or an invite link `doodledoggo.app/f/<code>`.
- **Visit a friend's yard** (read-only): see their dogs and decor, pet their dogs (+1 Bond each side), leave a paw-print note from a preset list ("Your dog is so fluffy!", "Woof! (That means hello.)" …).
- **One gift a day** to a friend (treats, seeds or decor from your bag), arriving as a letter in their mailbox.
- **Take a friend's dog on your walk**; they get a walk letter. You don't need to be online together.
- Up to +20 coins a day from Friends; walks stay the main income.
- **Safe by design:** no typed text ever leaves the device, no chat, no search, no directory (you can only add someone by their code or link). Remove or block any time; a block hides you both ways and the other side is never told. Privacy page updated 7 Nov (`doodledoggo.app/privacy`).
- In all 11 languages.

Links on the day: https://an9ou.itch.io/doodle-doggo, https://doodledoggo.app, https://an9ou.github.io/paw-haven/.

## The angle

Friends is the first feature that **turns viewers into players who bring players**. So every post asks for one thing: **drop your friend code in the comments** (or send the post to a friend). Comments are what Instagram and RedNote reward most, and a code in the comments is a real invite. This is safe by the game's own design: sharing your code is the consent, and nothing else about you is shown.

The cozy, safe side is the second message, for parents and careful players: "no chat, no typing, no strangers can find you".
