## 4. Every time you post

- [ ] Upload the image (1080 x 1350). Check the preview crop in the grid view before you hit Share.
- [ ] Paste the caption. Check the Japanese line shows (no broken characters).
- [ ] Add the **alt text** (Advanced settings, Write alt text).
- [ ] Hashtags: 5, from the pools in `PROMOTION_PLAN.md`.
- [ ] Leave "Also share to Facebook" off unless you want it.
- [ ] After posting: stay for the first 30 to 60 minutes and answer every comment.
- [ ] Share the post to your Story with a link sticker.
- [ ] Do **not** tag accounts that did not ask to be tagged.

## 5. Replies (the part that grows the account)

- [ ] Reply to **every** comment, within 24 hours (ideally in the first hour).
- [ ] Ask a small question back ("Which dog would you pick?"). Questions bring more comments.
- [ ] Heart every comment, reply to the ones that say more than an emoji.
- [ ] Reply in the language the person used (English or Japanese).
- [ ] Bug reports: thank them, ask for device and what happened, and write it in `TODO.md` or tell the dev team.
- [ ] Rude or spam comments: delete and block. You never owe a reply.
- [ ] DMs from other devs asking to "promote" you for money: ignore.
- [ ] Never ask for a follow back or a like.
- Ready-made replies are at the end of `INSTAGRAM_FIRST_POSTS.md`.

## 6. Weekly (Fridays, 20 minutes)

- [ ] Write 4 numbers: reach, shares/saves, profile visits and link taps, new followers.
- [ ] Note the best post and why.
- [ ] Plan next week's 3 posts on Sunday.
