Briefing from the Game Manager, 31 Oct 2026. v2.18.4 is live on itch.io, doodledoggo.app/play and GitHub Pages. The Winter Market is on the town map from 1 to 26 December, every year. The items are in the game now, but players can only buy them from 1 Dec. No new breeds, so no new Instagram dog profiles. The design and item list are in `docs/design/winter-market.md`; the release notes are in the v2.18.4 row of `CHANGELOG.md`.

## What's new for players

- **The market:** five dressed crayon stalls (decorations, clothes, homes, toys and a treats cart).
- **10 winter costumes**, for all 35 breeds, adults and puppies:
  - Father Christmas Suit
  - Little Elf Outfit
  - Gingerbread Pup Suit
  - Snowman Suit (the carrot sits on the hat, never on the nose)
  - Polar Bear Hood
  - Penguin Suit
  - Puffer Jacket
  - Nutcracker Jacket & Hat
  - Snowflake Wings
  - Jingle Bell Collar

  The Puffer Jacket and the Polar Bear Hood keep a dog warm in the snow.
- **5 yard decorations:** Felt Fir Tree, Yard Snowman, Candy-Cane Fairy Lights, Present Pile and Star Lantern. Both lights glow after dark.
- **Homes:** a Gingerbread Kennel (its windows glow at night) and 4 beds: Present-Box Bed, Little Sleigh Bed, Snowball Nest and Giant Mitten Bed.
- **5 toys:** Squeaky Snowman, Candy-Cane Rope, Plush Penguin, Snowball Ball and Present Puzzle Box.
- **3 dog-safe treats:**
  - Reindeer Crunch (驯鹿脆脆): carrot and apple;
  - Turkey & Cranberry Bites (火鸡蔓越莓小口): plain turkey;
  - Frozen Yoghurt Snowballs (酸奶雪球): plain yoghurt.
