## First post

**Hello from Doodle Doggo! 🐶☕**

My girlfriend loves dachshunds more than anything. Every time one walks past us, she stops and smiles. "One day, we'll have one of our own."

But one day isn't today. We don't have our own home yet, and a dog deserves space, time and someone there every day. So we wait.

I didn't want her to only wait, so I picked up a crayon and drew her a dog. It was wobbly and a little crooked, and she loved it anyway. Then it needed a yard, a bowl, a town to walk through and friends to play with. Little by little, that one crayon dog grew into **Doodle Doggo**.

**What you can do in the game:**
- Adopt one of 35 deliberately crude crayon dogs, each with its own personality
- Pet, feed, bathe and nap with them. They remember every belly rub
- Walk a sketchbook town under Mt. Fuji, find treasures and earn coins
- Grow carrots and pumpkins, and cook dog-safe snacks
- Puppy playdates with real coat genetics, and every puppy goes to a kind family
- Take the Shinkansen to Tokyo

It's for everyone who loves dogs but can't have one right now: a small apartment, a busy life, a landlord who says no. Here, nothing bad ever happens to your dogs.

The game is completely free, with no ads and no in-game purchases, and it plays in 11 languages. If it brightens your day, a coffee here helps me keep building: new dogs, new places, little surprises with every update. And maybe one day it brings us a little closer to a real dachshund of our own.

Thank you for playing. 💛
