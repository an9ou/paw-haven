# v2.16 "First Week" (25 Oct 2026): briefing for future ads

From the Game Manager's briefing. No ad made yet (ads only, a different random dog each time; recent dogs: the Westie, the Mastiff, the Chihuahua). Since v2.15.2 every colour fill stays inside the lines: merge main before making anything.

Live on https://doodledoggo.app and itch.io (build #2209159). Screenshots: `docs/release/v2.16/` on main (`guided_start_*`, `next_unlock_*`, `calendars_*`, `bond2_party_*`, `dock_c_*`).

**New for players**
- Puppy's first day: Gerald guides 5 steps (pet, feed, play ball, name tag, a short walk). Each gives Bond, so most players reach Bond 2 within minutes.
- Faster early Bond: Bond 2 at 60 points (was 100), Bond 3 at 180 (was 250). Bond never goes down.
- A "Next unlock" line under the Bond meter (e.g. "Bond 2: learns Paw + 8 new outfits").
- The Bond 2 party: confetti, a free Bow Tie, and the dog does Paw for the first time.
- A 7-day welcome calendar for new players: gifts on days 1, 3, 5 and 7 (day 7: a photo frame with your dog's name). The days don't need to be in a row.
- A monthly calendar for everyone: a seasonal crayon page, a paw stamp for every day you play; rewards at 3, 7, 12, 18 and 24 days (coins, a seasonal toy, a treat bag, the month's photo frame and outfit). Missing a day never costs anything.
- "See you tomorrow": the dog names a small wish for tomorrow.
- A simpler phone menu: 4 big buttons (Care, Walk, Map, More). Computers keep all 7.
- In all 11 languages. No new breeds.

**Ad angles (from the Game Manager)**
- "A monthly calendar where missing a day costs nothing."
- "Your first day with your crayon puppy" (the 5 guided steps ending in the Bond 2 party, the dog's first Paw and confetti).

**Next:** v2.17 "Dog Days" (daily dog wishes, a Bond reward ladder, a crayon dog diary, a sticker book, letters).
