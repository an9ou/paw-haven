# v2.17 "Dog Days" (26 Oct 2026): briefing for future posts

From the Game Manager's briefing; no post made yet (a different random dog each time; recent: the Border Collie, the Great Dane, the Cane Corso, the Westie). Live on https://doodledoggo.app and itch.io (build #2211792). Screenshots: `docs/release/v2.17/` on main (`wish_chip_*`, `bond_track_*`, `diary_*`, `stickers_*`, `letters_*`, `daily_gift_*`).

**New for players** (a reason to come back every day)
- Daily gift: a small gift every day you play (coins, food, a treat, a brush and more), one tap in the calendar. The 3/7/12/18/24-day specials stay; Halloween, Christmas and New Year's Day bring festive treats. Missing a day never costs anything.
- Daily wish: each dog has one small wish a day ("sniff the park", "a carrot", "play ball"); granting it gives double Bond, coins and a sticker.
- Bond reward ladder: tap the Bond meter to see what levels 1 to 10 unlock. New: photo frames at Bond 3, 5 and 7, and a **Crayon Crown** and **Golden Collar** at Bond 10 (crude crayon).
- Dog diary (Journal → Diary): a short page each day in the dog's own voice, six personality voices.
- Sticker book: 12 pages from wishes, places, festivals and calendars; full pages give outfits or frames.
- Letters: Gerald's weekly town news, and letters from the families who adopted your puppies.
- All 11 languages (in French, Spanish and Portuguese the dog calls you "my favourite person": no gendered word for the player). No new breeds.

**Post ideas (from the Game Manager):** "What does your dog wish for today?" · the Bond 10 Crayon Crown · diary pages in the dog's voice.

**Next:** rainy days (wet dogs in the rain, all 35 breeds), then v2.18 "Fun Fair" (minigames and town jobs).
