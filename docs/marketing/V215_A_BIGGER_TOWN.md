# v2.15 "A Bigger Town" (24 Oct 2026): briefing for future ads

From the Game Manager's briefing. No ad made yet (ads only, a different random dog each time; the last two were the Mastiff and the Chihuahua).

Live on itch.io (build 2.15), https://doodledoggo.app and the TapTap Android app (it loads the live site). Details: `CHANGELOG.md` v2.15 rows and `V215.md` section 18 on main.

**New for players**
- The town map is redrawn: winding roads, every building its own button, shops scattered around town with their own storefronts.
- New places: the **Training Ground** (agility, plus a gentler puppy and senior course) and the **Splash Pool** (a daily paddle, a free life vest, floating fetch and a Winter Dome). Three run tracks with treasures and gates.
- 3 new wearables and new item icons.
- Dogs roam more freely.
- Everything in 11 languages. No new breeds.

**Also new:** Ko-fi at https://ko-fi.com/doodledoggoapp (button on the site's About Us page). TapTap International is coming; key art: `brand/taptap/keyart-1920x1080.png` on main.
