# "Party time" ad Reel (deadpan party, 25 Oct 2026)

The owner shared a Reel (@rolandcristal, 23 Oct 2026: a 3D dachshund standing up in a party hat between pigeons in party hats, on a flat sky and flat grass, about 14 s of hard cuts, sudden close-ups and an odd chant) and asked for a similar one. Ours keeps the deadpan, low-effort-on-purpose look but uses the game's real art only. English only. Files are in `brand/instagram/launch/party_reel/`.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `doodle-doggo-party-reel.mp4` | 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, 13.7 s, with the game's own "play" music. |
| `party-cover.jpg` | The designed cover: "party time." on a cream sticker, the Westie and two pigeons in party hats, the logo. Everything sits inside the grid's middle 1080 x 1440. |
| `build_party.js` | Remake: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node brand/instagram/launch/party_reel/build_party.js <workdir>` (about 1 minute; `DOG=<key>` for another dog; `ONLY=stills` for key frames and the cover; `FRAMES=a-b` re-renders only those frames over a copied workdir). |

**Dog:** the **Westie**, picked at random (not the Mastiff, the Chihuahua, or the promo shots' Pomeranian, Corgi, Beagle and Samoyed), in the game's Party Hat, standing up in its real "beg" pose. **Pigeons:** the game's own crayon city pigeon from the Tokyo scenes (world D), each with a small paper party cone (the cone is the only thing drawn for this Reel).

**What happens**
- 0–1.4 s: a white void with two small pigeons, far apart, each pecking once. Two coos, no music.
- 1.4 s: a black flash, then the music starts.
- 1.6–10.7 s: flat sky and flat grass. The Westie stands up in its party hat between the pigeons and sways to the beat; the pigeons bob their heads. A hard cut every one or two beats: the ground jumps up and down, the dog flips sides, a third pigeon shows up, an extreme close-up of the feet, the face turning left and right, one pigeon staring, then everyone hops at once (with a small yip).
- 10.7–13.7 s: the end card on the same scene: "Doodle Doggo · a free cozy dog game · play right on doodledoggo.app", with a sparkle.
