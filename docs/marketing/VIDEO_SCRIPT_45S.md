# Doodle Doggo: 45-second promo video script

Written 17 Oct 2026. **Narrator: Noodle the dachshund**, speaking in a calm, proud, slightly deadpan voice (he thinks he is the star, because he is). About 105 spoken words, a relaxed pace that fits 45 seconds with room for the jokes to land.

Every shot is something that really exists in the game, so it can be recorded with the same tools as the 30-second Reel. The dog lines in quotes are real lines from the game.

**Format:** 9:16 (1080 x 1920) for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. The same script works at 16:9 for YouTube or the itch.io page.
**Music:** the game's own cozy lo-fi music (no licence risk). Keep it quiet under the voice, about 25%.
**Sound effects:** the game's own barks, the squeaky duck, a soft "pop" for each title card.

## Voice-over only (for the recording)

Read this straight through. A pause marked *(beat)* is about half a second.

> Everything in this town was drawn very carefully. *(beat)* Except me. I drew myself.
>
> Hi. I'm Noodle. Welcome to Doodle Doggo.
>
> Adopt a wobbly crayon pup. There are fourteen of us. We are all very proud.
>
> Feed us. Pet us. Bath us, if you must.
>
> Take us on walks. Dig up treasure. Visit Mt. Fuji. *(beat)* Somebody should dig a tunnel through that mountain. I volunteer.
>
> Grow a garden. Cook dog-safe treats. Have puppy playdates, with real coat genetics.
>
> Dress up for every season. The ugly sweater contest is judged by a duck.
>
> And nothing bad ever happens here. Worst case, you walk into a log. *(beat)* I meant to do that.
>
> Doodle Doggo. Coming soon. Follow, so you don't miss me.

## Notes

- **No voice? It still works.** Turn every voice line into an on-screen caption (white box, Caveat font, as in the Reel). Many people watch with the sound off, so put captions on the voiced version too.
- **The first 5 seconds matter most.** The pencil-then-crayon drawing is the hook: it shows the joke of the whole game (a careful world, a badly drawn dog) without any words.
- **Who reads it:** you can record it yourself on your phone in a quiet room, ask a friend, or use a text-to-speech voice. If you use text-to-speech, choose a calm voice, not an excited "advert" voice. The deadpan delivery is what makes the jokes funny.
- **Words rules:** checked. No "ugliest", no "breed" as a verb, no "sell" for dogs, no other game names.
- **Season shots:** Halloween ends 2 Nov and Snow Days start 1 Dec. If you post between those dates, the seasons shot still works, because it promises "every season", not a date.
- **For ads:** cut it to 15 seconds by keeping the hook (0:00 to 0:09), the Fuji joke (0:19 to 0:26) and the end card.
- **I can build it:** the same pipeline that made the 30-second Reel (`docs/marketing/REEL_AND_ADS.md`) can make this as a video with captions and the game's music. Only the voice has to be recorded separately.
