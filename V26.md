# Paw Haven v2.6: Halloween 2026 (desktop 1280×720 and phones)

Read `PIPELINE.md` (lanes, shared scope, tests), `V25.md` (the festival and season system this builds on, section 11 is as built), `PHONE.md` (phone minimums) and `CLAUDE.md` (design rules) first. **Where this file disagrees with older docs, this file wins.**

v2.6 adds:

- **Halloween 2026, 8 Oct – 2 Nov 2026 (23:59 JST)**, layered **on top of** the autumn season: the maple leaves, leaf piles and Harvest Stall stay, and Halloween dressing is added over them in the yard, the Square, Market Street and the walks. Later years keep the v2.5 window (24–31 Oct).
- **The Pumpkin Patch Pop-up**, a festive stall in the Square, open only during the 2026 event, selling 16 **2026-exclusive** items for coins (never money): 4 yard decorations, 4 dog-safe treats (each with a two-sentence lesson), 4 toys (each with a small yard play game), 4 clothes (every breed, pose and age).
- **Exclusive rule:** items bought during the event stay with the player forever and show a small **"2026"** tag. After 2 Nov the pop-up packs up and these exact items are never sold again. Nothing is ever taken away.

No new place, no new save version. **Not in v2.6** (v2.7 next): home/yard furniture placement, multiple beds, pack care. Don't start them.

## Standing rules (every lane)

- **Nothing bad ever happens.** Cute-spooky only: smiling pumpkins, friendly ghosts, felt bats. No skulls, blood, fangs that bite, spiders or anything that frightens. The pop-up closing is a kind, quiet thing.
- **No real money.** Coins only. No selling dogs.
- **Food safety.** Every treat is dog-safe as described. Its `tip` is the two-sentence lesson.
- **Words.** The copy suite rules: no semicolons, no emoji, no browser dialogs in player text. Never "breed" as a verb.
- **Look.** Pencil-sketchbook world, deliberately crude crayon dogs. New art matches the neighbouring existing art in line weight, palette and crudeness.
- **Byte-identical:** every existing dog render, and every scene and walk render with Halloween off, stays byte-identical to `v26-base` (= v2.5 `e622d9a`).
- **Shared scope.** Prefix new top-level names with your lane tag: HALLOWEEN `hw`, TOYS has its own module. Grep `game/src` for every new top-level name before committing.
- **Under the test harness** the season is pinned to `summer` and festivals to `off` (`test_lib.js`), so older suites see exactly the old game. A v2.6 suite passes `fest`, `season` and `date` itself.
- **Phone:** taps ≥ 44 px (yard props 48 px), text ≥ 15 px, no sideways scroll, nothing covers `#dogHit`, the HUD, the action bar or the place chips. Rules live under `html[data-layout="phone"]` / `isPhone()`.
- **Screenshots:** desktop 1280×720 and phone 390×844, the new thing next to existing neighbours, before and after, under `game/shots_v26/<lane>/` (committed on your lane branch; the coordinator removes them before the release).

## 1. Data model and core plumbing (coordinator, on `integration/v2.6` before the lanes start)

### Dates (core, `00_core.js`)

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `HW_ED` | `2026`, the edition sold this year |
| `HW_WIN` | `{ 2026: ['2026-10-08', '2026-11-02'] }`, Halloween windows by JST date for years that differ from the default |
| `jstISO()` | today's date in Japan, `'YYYY-MM-DD'` (`new Date(Date.now() + 9 * 3600e3).toISOString().slice(0, 10)`) |
| `festNow()` | `halloween` in `auto`: if `HW_WIN[year]` exists, `jstISO()` within it (inclusive), else JST month 10 and day ≥ 24 (the v2.5 rule). `leaf` is unchanged. Overrides (`ENV.fest`) unchanged. |
| `hwShopOpen()` | `festOn('halloween') && jstISO() >= HW_WIN[HW_ED][0] && jstISO() <= HW_WIN[HW_ED][1]`. A Dev panel override outside the dates never reopens the pop-up. |
| `envNow()` | gains `halloween: festOn('halloween')`. `sceneG` passes it to the art. |

Walk contexts (`18_mod_walk_toys.js`, both `ctx` objects that pass `season`) also pass `halloween: festOn('halloween')`. The classic walk gets it through `envNow()`.

### Save fields

| Field | Default | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| `S.hw` | `{ letter: null, seen: null }`: `letter` = `'2026'` once the pop-up letter is sent, `seen` = date the pop-up sheet was first opened | HALLOWEEN |

`migrate()` gets one additive line (`if (!s.hw) s.hw = { letter: null, seen: null }`), `freshState` mirrors it. Bought items go where items always go: `S.inv.food[n]`, `S.inv.toys`, `S.inv.clothes`, and decorations in `S.decor[name] = { got: ISO, out: true }`.

## 9. As built (coordinator notes after the merge)

- **Names.** The 2026 treat is the Pumpkin Patch Pupcake, because Pumpkin Pupcake is the v1.3 kitchen dish. HW ART draws it under that name.
- **Dates.** `festNow()` in `auto` asks `hwAuto()` (in `00_core.js`): inside `HW_WIN[year]` by `jstISO()` when that year has an entry, else JST 24 to 31 October. `hwShopOpen()` also checks the `HW_ED` window, so a Dev panel override outside the dates never reopens the pop-up.
- **Harness gate.** Under the test harness (`navigator.webdriver`) the pop-up opens only when a suite sets `prefs.hwTest`, because the real date sits inside the 2026 window. The occlusion hooks use `__paw.hw.allow(true)`.
- **The pop-up in the Square.** `#hwPopG` is drawn before `#pack`. On phones its spot also stays clear of the bowl box (`HW_BOWL`, `[195,452,110,110]`), which shows while a snack is fed in the Square.
- **Other shops.** The Harvest Stall's `fsStallItems` skips `ed` items. Kibble Corner's toy list skips `fest` items. A mutt's random favourite food and toy are never `ed` items. The Market Street `buy` mission ignores `shop: 'popup'` buys.
- **Scene dressing.** `mkEnv` sets `hw` only when `o.halloween === true`, so a missing flag stays byte-identical. The dressing draws from its own seeded stream (`k.hw`), the scene and strip cache keys gain `|hw`, and the scene root gets `data-halloween="1"`. The runner passes the flag to the strip art and adds felt bats to its far sky at dusk and night.
- **Dog art.** A new `BACK2` table draws the parts of a body item that sit behind the dog (the Vampire Cape's flare, the far bat wing), in the cape layer before the tail. Old outfits have no `BACK2` entry and draw nothing new.
- **Toys.** `v26Pay()` in `mods/toys.js` pays a reward only when it gives Happiness, Bond or coins, so an energy-only step shows no "+0 Happiness" toast. Fast players stay under 20 Happiness per game, and the Plush Ghost gives one Bond per game.
