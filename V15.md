# Paw Haven v1.5, part A: "More Dogs" update (laptop first, fast)

This builds on V13.md, V13b.md and V131.md. Part B (the phone version) follows once part A is published.

## Goals
1. **Multi-dog home.** Adopt a second dog, and later up to 4. Each dog has its own stats, Bond, outfit and Profile.
2. **Home capacity** comes from the dog-house tier.
3. **Shelter rescues** with real coat genetics, including merle and dilute dogs, as groundwork for v2 breeding.
4. **Coat and eye variety** in the dog art, driven by genes.
5. **Tidy-up:**

   - Merge the old tests into one test.
   - Stop toasts from overlapping the garden and kitchen top bar.

- **Second adoption** unlocks when ANY dog reaches Bond 5 AND capacity is at least 2. The shelter then offers "Adopt" on its dogs.
- If capacity is full, the shelter says: "Your home is full. A bigger dog house would fit another friend."
- Dogs are never given away or sold. No release feature.

## Shelter
- **Starters:** the 6 starter dogs stay as they are (Boy/Girl choice, starter genes from V2_GENES.md).
- **Rescues:** 2 new rescue dogs arrive each real day, seeded by date, so they stay stable for the day.
  - **Breed:** random, from the 6 breeds.
  - **Sex:** a random fixed sex. Realistic: a rescue IS a boy or a girl, with no choice.
  - **Age:** random, 8–36 dog months.
  - **Genes:** random genotype from breed-appropriate allele frequencies (`PawGenes.randomGenotype(breed, rng)`). Merle (M/m) appears in about 15% of corgi, dachshund and mutt rescues, and never M/M.
  - **Name:** a funny default name, which the player can edit.
  - **Card:** shows the dog in its real coat, with breed, sex, age, coat name, eye colour and personality, and a "Rescue" stamp.
- **Starter card after the first dog:** once you already have a dog, the starter cards say "Adopt a starter" and keep the Boy/Girl choice. Each dog key can only be adopted once as a starter, but rescues can repeat breeds.

## Genes module: `mods/genes.js` → `window.PawGenes` (designer)
- `PawGenes.LOCI` documents the loci exactly as in the v2 tab and V2_GENES.md.
- `PawGenes.phenotype(genes, breedKey)` returns `{ coatName, eyes, coat }`.
  - `coatName` is plain language, for example "Red with cream urajiro", "Blue merle", "Lilac", "Black & white piebald".
  - `eyes` is one of `'brown' | 'amber' | 'blue' | 'odd'`.
  - `coat` is the art palette, in the shape below.
- `PawGenes.randomGenotype(breedKey, rng)`
- `PawGenes.inherit(mumGenes, dadGenes, rng)` (used in v2; written now and unit-tested)
- `PawGenes.isDoubleMerle(a, b)` and `PawGenes.related(dogA, dogB, allDogs)` (v2 welfare checks; written now)
- `PawGenes.STARTER_GENES`, copied from V2_GENES.md.
- **Logic:** keep it loosely real.
  - e/e gives red, yellow or cream (each breed has its own red shade).
  - B_ gives black pigment, b/b gives liver/brown pigment with amber eyes.
  - d/d dilutes: black becomes blue, liver becomes lilac or isabella.
  - S/sp gives small white markings; sp/sp gives extensive white piebald.
  - M/m gives merle (marbled patches of diluted base); it only shows on black or liver pigment, and on e/e it is hidden ("cryptic merle").
  - Bl/Bl gives blue eyes (husky-type).
  - Merle may give a blue or odd eye at about 25%, seeded per dog.
- **Starter dogs:** the starter genotypes MUST produce each starter dog's CURRENT look. The default palette must match the current art colours exactly, so the existing dogs don't change.
- Plain IIFE, no DOM. Include a Node test: phenotypes for the 6 starters, inheritance ratios over 10,000 runs, the double-merle check, and the relatives check.

## Coat art contract: `PawArt.dog(key, {coat, ...})` (dog artist)
```
coat = {
  base:  '#hex',   // main fur colour
  light: '#hex',   // secondary/cream areas (muzzle, chest, urajiro, tan points)
  dark:  '#hex',   // ear tips / mask / saddle shade where the breed has it
  white: 0 | 0.25 | 0.6,   // white spotting: none / small markings (chest, toes, blaze) / piebald patches
  merle: false | true,     // marbled darker+lighter patches over base, crayon smudges
  eyes:  'brown' | 'amber' | 'blue' | 'odd'
}
```
- **No `coat`:** the dog draws EXACTLY as today. All existing calls must be byte-identical.
- **With `coat`:** recolour the breed's fills from the palette, keep the outlines, and keep every joke trait.
- **Patterns:**
  - Piebald patches are deterministic per dog. Seed them with `o.seed`, the dog's id hash, so each dog keeps its pattern.
  - Merle is crayon-smudge marbling, seeded the same way.
  - Odd eyes are one blue and one brown, with mismatched crayon circles, which fits the ugly style.
- `PawArt.dogHead(key, {coat, seed})` gets the same support.
- Keep the 4-leg check, the performance and the outfit fit.

## Game (integrator)

**Save model**
- `S.dogs = [dog, ...]` with `S.activeId`. Each dog holds:
  - `id`, `key`, `name`, `sex`, `born`, `genes`, `coat`, `eyes`, `parents`, `rescue`
  - `stats {hunger, happy, energy, clean}`, `bond`, `bondPts`
  - `outfit {head, eyes, neck, body, charm}`
  - potty needs, `litters`, `fixed`, `seasonSeed`, `adoptedAt`
- **Shared:** coins, inventory, owned clothes, houses, beds, the Journal and treasures, the garden, the fridge and the places unlocked.
- **Migration:** move `S.dog` plus the old per-dog fields (`S.stats`, `S.outfit` and so on) into `S.dogs[0]`. Keep a `D()` helper that returns the active dog, so existing code changes as little as possible.

**Scenes**
- Every hub place shows all your dogs.
  - The active dog stands in the dog area.
  - Up to 3 others stand in secondary spots (left back, right back, centre back), at scale 0.82, behind the active dog, with a gentle idle wander and occasional poses (sit, happy, sleep).
  - Click a dog to make it active. It gets a small bounce, and the HUD switches to that dog.
- **Dog interactions:** occasional joke moments between dogs ("Biscuit and Frost are having a staring contest."), and a happiness bonus when dogs share a place.

**HUD**
- A dog switcher at the top left: a portrait chip per dog (`dogHead` with coat), the name with ♂/♀, and a tiny mood dot (green, amber or red from the lowest stat). Click a chip to switch.
- The stat meters and Bond show the active dog.

**Actions**
- Feed, Play, Care, Wardrobe and the Profile act on the active dog.
- Add "Feed all" in the Feed popup at home, so each dog gets one portion if you own enough.
- **Walk:** takes the active dog. The others stay home in the yard or house, and greet the walker on return ("Pepper sniffs Mochi thoroughly. Report filed.").
- **Map travel:** all dogs travel together.
- **Decay and potty:** stats decay for every dog. Potty runs per dog, with up to 2 messes per place when 2 or more dogs live there.
- **Unlocks:** Bond-gated unlocks (routes, places, items) use the HIGHEST Bond among your dogs.

**Profile**
- The Journal Profile has a dog selector.
- It shows the coat name and eyes from the phenotype.
- A rescue shows "Rescued on <date>".
- The gene slots stay hidden ("?"), with the v2 vet test teaser.
