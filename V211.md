# V211: v2.11 draft "Tokyo Trip" (Japan places, new breeds) and a multiplayer plan

Status: **draft for the owner** (17 Oct 2026). Nothing here is built yet. It follows v2.10 "Free to Roam", so roaming dogs and ball play everywhere are assumed.

## 1. Tokyo Trip: famous places in Tokyo and Japan

**How you get there:** a new **Station** pin on the town map. You buy a train ticket for the **Shinkansen**: a short crayon ride, dogs at the window and Mt. Fuji going past. That opens a second map, **Japan**, with Tokyo in the middle and a few day trips around it.

- **Cost:** a ticket costs coins (about one day of walks for a day trip).
- **Unlocking:** places open by Bond level, like the Mt. Fuji Lookout, so the trip is something to work towards.
- **Dogs:** every dog comes along, the same way pack walks work today.

| Place | Why it fits Doodle Doggo | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| **Shibuya Crossing and the Hachikō statue** | Japan's most famous loyal dog. The perfect first stop. | Sit with Hachikō (a Bond bonus, a short kind story about loyalty), cross the scramble with the pack, take a photo. |
| **Yoyogi Park dog run** | Tokyo's real dog run. Lots of other dogs. | Ball play on a big lawn. NPC dogs drop by (or real players' dogs, section 3). |
| **Asakusa and Sensō-ji** | The giant red Kaminarimon lantern. A classic postcard. | Nakamise snack street (dog-safe treats only), an omikuji fortune for your dog, the photo spot under the lantern. |
| **Tokyo Tower** | A skyline landmark. Looks great at night. | A night view with Winter Lights. A souvenir bandana. |
| **Ueno Park** | Cherry blossoms in spring, ponds, autumn colours. | Season scenes (hanami picnic in spring, momiji in autumn). |
| **Odaiba seaside** | A beach in the city, with the Rainbow Bridge. | A sunset walk and ball play on the sand. |
| **Day trip: Kamakura** | The Great Buddha and a surfing beach. | A beach walk, and a giant Buddha photo spot. |
| **Day trip: Hakone onsen** | Hot springs and a dog-friendly ryokan. | A foot bath for the owner, a cosy towel nap for the dogs (a Bath and Nap bonus). |
| **Day trip: Kyoto Fushimi Inari** | Thousands of red torii gates. Fox shrine. | A torii tunnel walk (a short runner level). Foxes peek and the dogs are very curious. |

Real-world care, shown in the game's kind two-sentence style:
- Dogs stay on the lead in busy places.
- Some shrines and temples only allow dogs in carriers.
- Hot spring water is for people, not dogs.
- No feeding the deer or foxes.

That means **no Nara deer feeding**: dogs and deer are best kept apart.

**New items:**
- souvenir bandanas: Shibuya, Asakusa, Tokyo Tower;
- a Shinkansen plush toy;
- a lucky cat (maneki-neko) decoration;
- a stamp book (eki stamps, one per place) that unlocks a final photo frame.

**Money:** the trip is a coin sink with Bond rewards, so walks stay the main income.

## 3. Multiplayer: what is possible, and a safe way to start

### What it needs technically

The cloud already exists: logins and saves on the Cloudflare Worker and the D1 database.
- **Visiting other players' dogs** needs only small additions: a public, read-only "dog card" per player.
- **Live chat rooms** need a real-time server. Cloudflare **Durable Objects** with WebSockets can hold one room per place, with a small monthly cost at our size.

### Key risk: safety

A cozy dog game will attract kids and teens. Open text chat with strangers brings:
- spam;
- bullying;
- people sharing personal details;
- adults contacting minors.

That needs moderation, report and block tools, and an age rule, plus updates to the privacy page and the store pages. It is the biggest part of the work. It isn't a technical problem.

### Recommended plan, in three steps

1. **v2.11: "Dog Cards" (visit, no chat).**
   - Every logged-in player can publish a **dog card**: their dogs' names, breeds, coats, outfits and a photo-mode picture.
   - Other players see **visiting dogs** at the Dog Park and the Yoyogi dog run: real players' dogs drawn in the scene, roaming with yours.
   - Tap one to see its card and give it a **paw** (a like).
   - No free text anywhere, so nothing to moderate. It is opt-in, and only the player's chosen display name shows, never the email.
2. **v2.12: "Quick Chat".**
   - In the dog run and on cards, players talk with **preset phrases and stickers** only: "Cute dog!", "Want to play ball?", "Good boy!", a set of crayon stickers.
   - **Friends** by a friend code.
   - This is how many family games do chat: it is friendly, needs almost no moderation, and works across languages (the phrases can show in Japanese and English).
3. **Later, only if wanted: "Free Chat" for friends.**
   - Typed chat only between accepted friends, never with strangers.
   - It needs a word filter, report and block, a 13+ rule, rate limits, message logs kept for a short time for reports, and a moderation contact.
   - Bigger work and an ongoing responsibility, so decide after Quick Chat has run for a while.

### Costs

- **Dog Cards:** fits in today's free tiers.
- **Quick Chat rooms:** Durable Objects run about a few dollars a month at small scale, rising with players.
- **Free chat:** adds moderation time, which is the real cost.

## 4. Suggested order

- **v2.11 "Tokyo Trip":** the Station and the Japan map, the first 5 places (Shibuya and Hachikō, Yoyogi dog run, Asakusa, Tokyo Tower, Odaiba), 2 to 3 new breeds and Dog Cards.
- **v2.12:** the day trips (Kamakura, Hakone, Kyoto), the remaining breeds and Quick Chat with friends.
- **Later:** free chat for friends, if the owner wants it.
