# V221: v2.21 "Five New Friends" (working name; 5 new breeds, 40 in all)

Status: **non-art side built** (4 Nov 2026) on `claude/new-breeds` (from `add7691f`, the dev branch with Missions v2). The art is two ART lanes (section 5).
It launches **together with v2.20 "Little Helps"** on the owner's date, behind the same switch (section 7). Base for the art check: `origin/v2184-base`.

## 1. What the owner asked for

Five new breeds, keys 36 to 40, chosen by the owner: **Dalmatian, Bernese Mountain Dog, Australian Shepherd, Cavalier King Charles Spaniel, Jack Russell Terrier**.
Done the way v2.12 added its fourteen (`V212.md`, the template): append-only keys, every breed table, real coat genetics folded into the six loci, the
welfare rules, personality and size rows, Dog Card words and the Worker count, deliberately crude crayon art in every pose, age, outfit and face, every old
render byte-identical. New this time: **the gate** (section 7), so nothing shows before the owner's launch date.

## 3. Genetics, coats, litters, care notes (GENES lane, built)

Coats fold into the existing loci (B, D, E, S, M, Bl), as the older breeds do. **Only the Australian Shepherd carries M**, as M/m (`FREQ.M` 0.35);
M/M never exists: `randomGenotype` gives M/m at most, `isDoubleMerle` blocks every pair of two M carriers (seen or hidden on a yellow e/e coat), `predict`
drops M/M outcomes, and the Dog Card Worker refuses M/M. The relatives rule and the age, season and rest limits stay as they are.

## 6. Placeholders (built) and the art-measured numbers

- **Before the art lands** the game runs: `dogsList()` falls back to the `FALLBACK_DOGS` rows, and `artKey()` (`00_core.js`) draws a breed PawArt does
  not know as the neutral **mutt**. No other hook is needed; the ART lanes only add their `SP` rows and `KEYS`.
- **The placeholder never ships:** `tools/itch.js` refuses to pack while `MS2_LIVE` is on and a new breed has no `SP` row, and `v221_breeds` section 3 fails the moment `MS2_LIVE` is `true` while any of the five lacks its own `KEYS` entry and
  `SP` row (while it is off it prints a note). `__paw.v221.ownArt()` lists the new keys PawArt draws itself.

## 7. The gate (built): one switch launches v2.20 and v2.21

`v221On()` in `00_core.js` is `ms2On()` (`24b_missions2.js`: `MS2_LIVE`, the GitHub Pages copy's `window.__ddMs2Test` from `tools/pages.js`, or the dev

- **adoption and the shelter starters** (`dogsList()` leaves the five out; its memo knows the switch), the passing town dogs (they read `dogsList()`),

- **Dog Cards and Friends:** `dcBreeds()` is the live list (35), card dog names stop at 115 (`dcNameN`), so a card never carries a new breed or word and
