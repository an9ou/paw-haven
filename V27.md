# Paw Haven v2.7: Home & Pack (desktop 1280×720 and phones)

Read `PIPELINE.md` (lanes, shared scope, tests), `V26.md` (the last release, same shape as this file), `V25.md` (seasons, festivals, the v2.5 nap fit), `PHONE.md` (phone minimums) and `CLAUDE.md` (design rules) first. **Where this file disagrees with older docs, this file wins.**

v2.7 adds:

- **Home decorating.** 8 everyday decorations for the yard and 8 for the house, sold at Barkitecture on Market Street for coins (60 to 140, about a day's walk). A **placement mode** in the yard and the house: place, move and put away, by drag or by tap-to-select and tap-to-place, on desktop and phone. The yard decorations from v2.1 and v2.6 become movable too.
- **Furniture the dogs use.** Five big pieces for the house, sold in a new **Furniture corner** tab at Barkitecture: the Dog Play Tower, the Tunnel Sofa, the Ball Pit, the Sofa Ramp and the Window Seat. Dogs climb, sit, nap and play on them in idle time with their existing poses. The art has a back layer and a front layer, so a dog really is inside the tunnel or the ball pit.
- **A bed for every dog.** Several beds stand in the house at once. Every dog naps on its own bed, the whole curled-up dog on the cushion (the v2.5 fit). One new cheap bed, the Knitted Nest.
- **Pack care.** With two or more dogs, Feed (meals, snacks and water), Pet, Bath and Nap act on every dog present: one bowl each, showing the food, one bath each, everyone petted, everyone tucked in. A row of dog chips lets the player untick dogs.
- **The Shelter Playroom.** The shelter becomes a cozy dog-café playroom: shelter dogs of every breed and age wander, nap and play, the player pets them, gives belly rubs and tosses a toy. An "Adopt me" board on the wall lists every dog looking for a home: tap a dog, Meet it, Adopt it with the existing naming flow. The first-dog adoption of a new game happens here too.

No new place, no new save version.

**Not in v2.7:** new dog poses (every existing dog render stays byte-identical, no DOG ART lane), new walks, toys or treats, furniture in the yard, beds in the yard, rotating or resizing placed items, a second floor, pack walks, pack tricks or pack play, a Journal page for decorations, missions about decorating (the acts fire, the mission pool is unchanged), any change to Halloween 2026.

## Standing rules (every lane)

- **Nothing bad ever happens.** No fail states. A blocked action gets a short, kind, two-sentence explanation. Shelter dogs are always cared for: water bowls, cushions, toys. A dog nobody adopts today is still happy tomorrow.
- **No selling dogs, ever.** Adoption is free. Never "buy", "sell", "sold", "price" or "cost" near a dog, in code text or art. Decorations and furniture are bought with coins, never with money.
- **Real welfare and food safety.** Plants are dog-safe (sunflowers, snapdragons, the Boston fern). Puppies in the shelter are at least 3 months old. Seniors get the Sofa Ramp and soft spots. Nothing a dog eats changes in this release.
- **Look.** Detailed pencil-sketchbook world (style G: coloured-pencil washes drifting off two-stroke tapered outlines, light hatching, Caveat labels, washi tape), **deliberately crude crayon dogs**. New props stand next to the crayon box, the rocking chair and the beds and look drawn by the same hand.
- **Words.** The `copy` suite rules: no semicolons, no emoji, no browser dialogs in player text. Never "breed" as a verb, "stud", "litter price", "sell" for dogs. Plain short English, one idea per sentence, jokes welcome.
- **UI.** Every window is a centred popup (`openModal`), a bottom sheet on phones. Motion respects `[data-motion="off"]`: the game still works with still frames.
- **Phones.** iPhone 13 (390×844) and Pixel 7 (412×915): taps ≥ 44 px (yard and house props ≥ 48 px, `hmDecorHit` style), text ≥ 15 px (captions 13 px), no sideways scroll, nothing covers `#dogHit`, the dogs, the HUD, the action bar or the place chips. Drag is always optional (tap-to-select, tap-to-place). Rules sit under `html[data-layout="phone"]` / `isPhone()`.
- **Economy.** Walks stay the main income (about 100 a day). Decorations 60 to 140, furniture 200 to 360, the Knitted Nest 150. The playroom pays nothing: petting and toy tosses are for fun.
- **Save.** `S.v` stays 1. Every new field is optional and every reader defaults it. The coordinator added the defaults (`v27Fields` in `00_core.js`, section 1). Old single-dog and multi-dog saves must load and look exactly as before until the player changes something.
- **Shared scope.** Prefix new top-level names with your lane tag: HOME `ho` (CSS `.ho-`), PACK CARE `cr` (`.cr-`), SHELTER `sr` (`.sr-`). `sh` is taken (v2.4 SHOP), `pk` is taken (`pkLead`), `.pc-` is the postcards. Grep `game/src` and `game/css` for every new top-level name before committing. Cross-lane calls are guarded: `if (typeof fn === 'function') fn(...)`.
- **Byte-identity.** The base is the branch **`v27-base`** (= v2.6 main `3619e7e`). Every existing dog render (`node dogs/check_identical.js` defaults to `v27-base`), every existing scene, walk strip, prop, item, icon, bed and house render stays byte-identical. New names are new. world_a is not touched in this release.
- **Under the test harness** the season is pinned to `summer`, festivals to `off`, and **pack care to "active dog only"** (`prefs.crOne`, section 1), so the older suites see exactly the old game. v2.7 suites set what they need (`packCare: true`, `season`, `fest`, `date`).
- **Screenshots:** desktop 1280×720 and phone 390×844, the new thing next to existing neighbours, before and after, under `game/shots_v27/<lane>/` (PNG under 300 KB, at most 60 per lane, committed on your lane branch; the coordinator removes the folder before the release). Halloween 2026 is live now: take each yard and Square shot once with `fest: 'off'` and once with `season: 'autumn'`, `fest: 'halloween'`, so the new things are seen next to the Halloween dressing and the four 2026 decorations.

### Furniture use spots (cross-lane, in each furniture entry's `spots`)

A spot is the dog's **feet point** `x, y` and the **dog box width** `w` (the 264×220 dog box scaled to `w` wide), all in the prop's **viewBox units**, with the pose and facing. `front: true`: the prop's `<g class="pa-front">` is drawn over a dog on that spot. `via`: where the dog starts its walk (the ramp foot). The art may tune the numbers and publishes them as `PawArt.FURN_SPOTS[prop]` (same shape), which **wins over** the table when present.

World conversion (HOME): with the item at `[ax, ay, aw, ah]` and viewBox `[vw, vh]`, `k = aw / vw`; feet `fx = ax + x * k`, `fy = ay + y * k`; dog scale `s = w * k / 264`; the active dog goes there with `dogTo(fx - 430 * s, fy - 500 * s, s)`, a pack dog with `hoPackSpot` returning `[fx, fy, face, s]`. With `w` 200 and `k` 0.8, a dog on furniture is drawn at 0.61, between the back and front pack spots (0.53 and 0.76).
