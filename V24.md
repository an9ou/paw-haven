# Paw Haven v2.4: Shop Day (desktop 1280×720 and phones)

Read `PIPELINE.md` (lanes, shared scope, tests), `V21.md` (standing rules, the style of a lane brief), `PHONE.md` (phone minimums) and `CLAUDE.md` (design rules) first. **Where this file disagrees with older docs, this file wins.**

v2.4 adds:

- **32 new crayon-drawn items:** 6 houses, 12 outfits, 6 toys (each with its own yard play game), 8 dog-safe foods with safety tips. Most are sold in the Market Street shops; a few come from walk treasure, a letter, a care milestone and the mission stamps.
- **Daily missions:** 3 a day on a crayon checklist card, about 15 coins each, a crayon stamp for all 3, a special item at 7 stamps. Unfinished missions just disappear.
- **A game guide:** Gerald the duck walks new players through their first day. A Journal "How to play" tab keeps the guide for rereading. Existing saves get a letter, never the walkthrough.
- **The claude.ai artifact publishes again** as a multi-file artifact (coordinator only, section 11).

No new place, no new save version.

## Standing rules (every lane)

- **Nothing bad ever happens.** No fail states, no sickness, no running away. A missed mission vanishes quietly. A blocked action gets a short, kind, two-sentence explanation.
- **No selling dogs, ever.** Sparkle stays cosmetic. Reward items (Rocket Ship, Astronaut Helmet, Cozy Hoodie, Knit Scarf, Sun Hat, Squeaky Hedgehog) never appear in any shop.
- **Real welfare and food safety.** Every new food is safe for dogs and says so in two kind sentences. Nothing toxic is ever named as food.
- **Look.** Detailed pencil-sketchbook world (style G: coloured-pencil washes drifting off two-stroke tapered pencil outlines, light hatching, Caveat labels, washi tape) and **deliberately crude crayon dogs**. No new palettes, no generic icons, no plain web UI. New art must sit next to the old art in a screenshot and look like the same hand drew it.
- **Words.** Never "breed" as a verb, "stud", "litter price", "sell" (for dogs). The build rejects "nintendo", "pokémon" and "animal crossing". Plain short English, one idea per sentence, no semicolons in player text, no emoji in code, jokes welcome, tips two sentences at most. The `copy` suite checks this.
- **UI.** Every window is a centred popup (`openModal`), a bottom sheet on phones. Motion respects `[data-motion="off"]`.
- **Phones.** Every new screen and card passes the phone minimums on iPhone 13 (390×844) and Pixel 7 (412×915): 44×44 px taps, 15 px text (13 px captions), no sideways scroll, nothing covers the dog (`#dogHit`). Phone rules sit under `html[data-layout="phone"]` or `isPhone()`.
- **Economy.** Walks stay the main income (about 100 a day). Missions pay 45 a day at most. Shop prices for clothes, toys and foods stay under 100 (two toys at 130 and 150, like the Frisbee and the Puzzle Feeder today). Houses sit on the existing ladder (300 to 5,000).
- **Save.** `S.v` stays 1. Every new field is optional and every reader defaults it. The coordinator adds the defaults to `migrate()` and `freshState()` in `00_core.js` (section 1); lanes default their own fields on `game:ready` as well.
- **Shared scope.** Prefix new top-level names with your lane tag: `sh` SHOP & MISSIONS (missions use `ms`), `gd` GUIDE. Art lanes and TOYS have their own files. Only the cross-lane names in section 2 are unprefixed. Grep `game/src` for every new top-level name before committing.
- **Cross-lane calls** are always guarded: `if (typeof fn === 'function') fn(...)`.
- **Screenshots.** Art lanes check every new item in screenshots against the existing items at desktop 1280×720 and phone 390×844, at every breed, pose and age where it applies, and iterate until it matches. Save them under `game/shots_v24_<lane>/` (not committed) and list the paths in your report.

## 5. GUIDE lane (new `25_guide.js` + `28_guide.css`; `game/test_v24_guide.js`, `game/test_phone_v24_guide.js`)
