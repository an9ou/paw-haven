How to play

Doodle Doggo is a slow, cozy game. There is no way to lose and nothing bad ever happens. Here is everything you need for your first days.

Your first day

  1. Adopt a dog

    Start at the Shelter. Read each dog’s card, pick a boy or a girl and give them a name. Adopting is always free.

  2. Take care of them

    Four crayon meters show Hunger, Happiness, Energy and Clean. Feed, Play, Care and Nap fill them, and petting your dog always helps.

  3. Go for a walk

    Walks are where you earn most of your Paw Coins. Dash along the route, jump, collect treats and follow the Nose-o-meter to dig up treasure.

  4. Grow your Bond

    Everything you do together adds Bond. Each new Bond level opens a new part of the game.

  5. Explore the town

    Open the Map to visit the park, the beach, Market Street’s shops, the café and more. Every place has its own lines for every dog.

What Bond opens

  • Bond 2: the Garden and the Mt. Fuji Lookout.
  • Bond 3: the Kitchen and the Station, where the Shinkansen goes to Tokyo.
  • Higher Bond: more places your dog likes to hang out around home and town.

Handy tips

  • Gerald the duck knows everything. Follow his tour on your first day, or read his notes in the Journal later.
  • The clipboard in the yard has three daily missions. They are a quick way to earn coins and Bond.
  • Ball play works everywhere: drag the ball and let go to throw. Your whole pack races for it.
  • Time runs fast: one game hour is one real minute, and one real day is a month in your dog’s life.
  • The camera button makes a postcard of your pack to save or share.
  • Seasons follow the real calendar, with festivals in autumn, winter and the New Year.

Puppies and families

Grown-up dogs can have puppy playdates. Coats and looks are inherited like real dogs, and mixes get a body from one parent and a head from the other.

The game keeps the real welfare rules: no relatives, the right age, rest between litters. Puppies you don’t keep go to named families for free, and they send you postcards.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser, with no ads.

Where is my game saved?

In your browser. Make a free account in Settings to play the same dogs on another device.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The game has a phone layout. Use the Full screen button on the home page for the biggest view.

Can my dog get sick or run away?

Never. Meters only go low, your dog just gets a bit sleepy or hungry, and one cuddle starts to fix it.
