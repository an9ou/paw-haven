      Questions

      How do I bring my dog from the game?

      Playing on doodledoggo.app? Your dogs are already here when you open this page in the same browser. Playing somewhere else, like itch.io? We can't see those dogs from here yet, so pick one of the ready-made dogs for now.

      Where is it made, and how long does it take?

      Each order is printed just for you by our print partner, at a print shop near you where it can. Printing usually takes 2 to 5 working days, and we post every order within 7 working days of payment. Then the post: your basket shows how long it usually takes to reach you. You get a tracking link by email.

      How much is shipping?

      Choose where it's going under Ship to at the top: the shipping, and when shipping is free, show right there and again in your basket before you pay. Prices are in US dollars.

      Can I send it back?

      Because it is made just for you, with your own dog on it, it can't be returned if you change your mind. If it arrives damaged, misprinted or lost, tell us within 30 days and we reprint it or refund you, free. Changed your mind straight away? You can cancel within 1 hour of paying.

      Which size should I get?

      The tees are unisex adult sizes. Lay a tee you like flat, measure from armpit to armpit and compare with the size guide on the tee. Between two sizes? Pick the bigger one.

      Is my dog's picture private?

      Your dog is drawn here in your browser, from how it looks in the game. When you order, only how it looks goes to us and the print shop, to print your order, and we delete the picture we print about six months after it ships. Its name is printed only if you leave it on.

      Why does my dog look like that?

      It's meant to. The wobbly crayon lines are the joke, and the printer keeps every one of them.

      Does it change anything in my game?

      No. Your dog and your game stay exactly as they are. Nothing here makes a dog happier or faster: they already love you.
