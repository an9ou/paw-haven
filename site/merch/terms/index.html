What you're ordering

  • Tees are unisex adult sizes. The size guide is on the shop page, next to the sizes.

Your dog and its name

  • Your dog's picture is drawn from how your dog looks in the game. The wobbly crayon lines are on purpose: they are the joke, and we print every one of them.
  • The name is printed only if you leave it on. Our crayon letters can't draw every character, a name has to fit on the item, and we won't print some words. The shop page tells you when a name can't be printed, before you add it to your basket.

  • We may refuse to print a name. If we find a problem with a name after you've paid, we write to you and refund that item in full.

Prices and currency

  • Prices are in US dollars: a mug is $19.99 and a tee is $26.99. Bigger tee sizes cost a little more; the shop page shows the price when you pick the size.

If you choose that, Stripe converts the price at its own rate, which includes a small conversion fee, and you see the exact amount before you pay. You can always choose to pay in US dollars instead; your bank may then charge its own fee for a payment in another currency.
  • The price you pay is the one shown on the payment page when you pay. If our prices change while your basket is open, the shop page shows you the new total and asks again before anything is paid.

  • Depending on where you live, tax may be included in the price, added at checkout, or collected at your door.

Paying

  • You pay on Stripe's secure payment page, with a card or one of the other ways it shows you. We never see your card details.
  • You pay the whole order at once, when you order. Your card statement shows DOODLEDOGGO MERCH.
  • Stripe sends you a receipt, and we send you a "Thank you" email with a link to your order page.
  • There are no accounts to make and nothing to sign up for. We use your email only for this order's emails, never for news or offers.

How ordering works

  1. On the shop page you choose where it's going, your dog, the mug or tee, the pose, the name, the colour, the size and how many (up to 6 different things, and up to 3 of each).
  2. Your basket shows every item, the shipping and the total. You can change or remove anything.
  3. You tap Pay and go to Stripe's payment page. There you type the address and pay, and you confirm that you are 18 or over, that you agree to these terms, and that you understand the items can't be returned if you change your mind.
  4. When your payment goes through, our contract with you begins. We send the "Thank you" email with your order page.
  5. One hour later we start printing. Our print partner makes your order and posts it, and we email you a tracking link for each parcel.

If we can't take your order before you pay (for example, the shop has made all it can for the day), nothing is paid and nothing is made.

Cancelling within 1 hour

  • For 1 hour after you pay, your order page has a Cancel button and shows the time printing starts.
  • If you cancel in that hour, nothing is printed and we refund the whole order, shipping too.
  • Cancelling covers the whole order. To change one thing, cancel and order again with the change.
  • After the hour, printing has started, so the order can't be cancelled. If something then arrives damaged or wrong, we still make it right (see If something is wrong).

Changing your mind

Everything is printed just for you with your own dog, so we can't take it back if you change your mind. You can cancel the whole order for a full refund within 1 hour of paying, from your order page. If something arrives damaged or misprinted, or never arrives, email us within 30 days of delivery (or of the last day of the delivery time shown for your order) with a photo, and we'll reprint it or refund it, whichever you prefer. This doesn't affect your legal rights.

The 30 days is our own promise: write to us within 30 days and we'll put it right without fuss. The law where you live may give you longer, so write to us anyway.

  • never arrives.

Please tell us your order number (it's in the "Thank you" email) and send a photo of the problem, unless it never arrived. Our own promise: write to us within 30 days of delivery (or, if it never came, of the last day of the delivery time shown for your order) and we'll put it right without fuss. If we can't make it

Sometimes our print partner can't make one item (a colour runs out, or a file can't be printed). Then we refund that item in full without you having to ask, send you a kind email, and the rest of your order goes on. If nothing in the order can be made, we refund all of it, shipping too. We never charge you more because the rest of the order became smaller.

  • Refunds go back the way you paid. See when a refund shows on your card.
