Where it's made

Nothing waits on a shelf. When the cancel hour is over, our print partner prints your order at a print shop near you where it can, and posts it to you. A mug and a tee in the same order may be made in different places.

How long it takes

Printing usually takes 2 to 5 working days, and we post every order within 7 working days of payment. If we can't, we email you by that day with a new date, and you can wait or cancel the late part for a full refund. You can cancel the late part any time until it's posted.

If the new date passes too and you haven't told us you'd rather keep waiting, we refund the late part by itself.

You cancel the late part from your order page. Then the post: how long the whole trip usually takes, door to door, is in the table below.

What shipping costs

Shipping is one fee for the whole order, in US dollars. Whether it's free depends on the items' total (the "subtotal", before shipping). Pick where it's going under Ship to on the shop page: your exact shipping and the total show in your basket before you pay.

These are usual times: the post can be slower around holidays. Shipping never goes up after you've paid, even if part of your order is refunded.

Separate parcels

Mugs and tees travel in their own parcels, so they may arrive on different days. You pay one shipping fee for the order (in the last row of the table above, also the fee for each extra mug or tee), not one for each parcel.

Tracking

When a parcel leaves our print partner, we email you an "On its way" message with its tracking link. Your order page shows every parcel and its tracking link too, and says "Delivered" once the carrier tells us.

Customs and import tax

Depending on where you live, tax may be included in the price, added at checkout, or collected at your door. Some places charge import tax or a handling fee on parcels from abroad; then the delivery company may ask you for it when the parcel arrives. It isn't part of our price and isn't paid to us. For some places your basket reminds you of this before you pay; if you're not sure, please check your own country's rules.

Where we deliver

We can't deliver to some places yet. The Ship to list on the shop page shows where we can deliver today. Each order goes to one address, in the place you picked under Ship to.

Address problems

  • Please check your address on the payment page before you pay.
  • If our print partner can't send to the address, we email you ("We need your address") and you can fix it on your order page. It can only go to an address in the place you picked; to send it somewhere else, we refund you and you can order again.
  • If we don't hear from you within 7 days, we refund the order in full.
  • If a parcel comes back to our print partner (for example, the address was wrong or the parcel wasn't collected), we write to you and send it once more for free, to the address you give us. If we don't hear from you within 7 days, we refund those items.

Lost parcels

If a parcel hasn't arrived by the last day of the delivery time shown for your order, or its tracking stops moving, write to us at contact@doodledoggo.app. We send a new one for free or refund those items, whichever you prefer. The other parcels of your order aren't affected. Our own promise: write to us within 30 days of that day and we'll put it right without fuss. The law where you live may give you longer, so write to us anyway.

Cancelling within 1 hour

For 1 hour after you pay, you can cancel the whole order from your order page. Nothing is printed and we refund everything, shipping too. After that hour printing has started, so the order can't be cancelled.

Returns

Everything is printed just for you with your own dog, so we can't take it back if you change your mind. You can cancel the whole order for a full refund within 1 hour of paying, from your order page. If something arrives damaged or misprinted, or never arrives, email us within 30 days of delivery (or of the last day of the delivery time shown for your order) with a photo, and we'll reprint it or refund it, whichever you prefer. This doesn't affect your legal rights.

The 30 days is our own promise: write to us within 30 days and we'll put it right without fuss. The law where you live may give you longer, so write to us anyway.

Damaged or misprinted

  1. Take a photo of the problem (the whole item, and the damage or the misprint up close).
  2. Email it to contact@doodledoggo.app with your order number from the "Thank you" email, within 30 days of delivery if you can (see Returns: the law may give you longer). The same goes for the wrong item, colour or size.
  3. Tell us whether you'd like a free reprint or a refund. A photo is enough: we don't ask you to post anything back.

Refunds

  • We send a refund as soon as it's decided (a cancel in the hour is refunded straight away), back to the card or account you paid with.
  • It usually takes a few days to show on your card, sometimes up to 10 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Refunds are in the currency you paid. If you paid in your own currency on Stripe's page, Stripe pays it back in that currency and works out the amount; for a refund of part of an order, it may differ a little from what you paid for those items.
  • A refund for one item is that item's full price, with its share of any tax you paid on it (in the last row of the table, with its extra-item fee too). When every item has been refunded, the shipping is refunded too.
