Home

Where every day starts and ends.

Home Yard

Your dog's own yard. Play ball, have a cuddle and watch the seasons change.

Cozy House

Naps, meals and cuddles indoors. Decorate the rooms and give every dog a bed of its own.

Garden

Plant seeds, water them and pick what grows. Only dog-safe plants, of course.

Kitchen

Cook simple, dog-safe treats with what you grew in the garden.

The town

Walk your dog all over town. Each walk finds coins and little treasures.

Sunny Park

The first walk. Sniff the flowers, chase the leaves and find little treasures.

Riverside

A path by the water, with ducks who really want your bread.

Maple Woods

Shady paths under the maples. A good nose can dig up something special here.

Seashell Beach

Waves, running in the sand and shells to find along the shore.

Market Street

The shops: food at Kibble Corner, toys, clothes and things for the house.

Town Square

The heart of the town, where the festivals, the stalls and the costume parade happen.

Pupuccino Café

A café where dogs are always welcome. Pull up a chair and relax.

Dog Park

Run and play with other dogs, and say hello to visiting dogs from other players.

Vet Clinic

Gentle check-ups and puppy shots from a very kind vet.

Grooming Salon

A bath, a brush and a fresh new look.

Hilltop Meadow

A windy meadow up the hill, with a view over the whole town.

Lighthouse Pier

A wooden walkway out to the lighthouse, with sea air and seagulls.

Shelter Playroom

Where the shelter dogs wait for a family. Adopt one, or just come and play.

Mt. Fuji Lookout

A hill with a view of Mt. Fuji, which looks different in every season.

Station

Buy a ticket and take the bullet train to Tokyo for the day.

A trip to Tokyo

Take the train from the Station for a day out in Tokyo, and collect a stamp at every stop.

Shibuya Crossing

The famous busy crossing, and Hachikō, the loyal dog, waiting by the station.

Yoyogi Park Dog Run

A big dog run in the park, full of new friends to run with.

Asakusa and Sensō-ji

An old temple street with a giant red lantern and lots of little shops.

Ueno Park

Shady paths, a pond and cherry trees in the middle of the city.

Tokyo Tower

The red and white tower. Look up, then smile for a photo.

Odaiba Seaside

A sandy beach on the bay, with a big bridge in the distance.
