Meet the dogs 犬たち inu-tachi = the dogs

Fourteen wobbly crayon friends, with more on the way. Every one is in the game, ready to be adopted and loved. Each profile has the dog’s name in Japanese katakana, the script Japan uses for words from other languages.

a Mochi puppy

Mochi the Shiba Inu

柴犬 shiba inu · Mochi in katakana: モチ

“I could leave anytime. I choose not to.”

Personality: Proud, independent, dramatic.

  • proudbows to nobody, eats first
  • dramaticgasps at a closed door
  • independentcuddles on their own schedule
  • judgyhas opinions about your snacks

In the playroom: Keeps a little distance, then wanders over on Mochi’s own terms.

Loves
  • salmon pâté
  • plush bone
  • tricks

豆知識 mame-chishiki, a fun fact: Mochi is also the Japanese word for a soft rice cake (餅). The Shiba is one of Japan’s own native dogs.
