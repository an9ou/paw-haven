Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026. A game by an9ou.

In short: Doodle Doggo has no ads and no tracking. If you make an account, we keep your email, a scrambled version of your password and your game save and, only if you switch on a Dog Card, the card and the paws it gets. Nothing else.

What we collect

  • If you play as a guest: your game is saved in your browser, on your device. We never see it. If you are online, a guest copy is also kept with the login service so it isn't lost, with no email or name attached.
  • If you make an account, we store:
    • your email address, so you can log in. We only ever email you when you ask for a 6-digit code to reset your password (Forgot your password? in the game).
    • a hashed password. A hash is a scrambled version of your password that can't be turned back into the password. We can't read your password, and nobody else can either.
    • your game save, so you can play the same game on any device.
    • the dates of your account and your latest save.

We don't collect your name, your age, your location, your contacts or anything else.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No tracking, no analytics and no cookies. The game's fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • No newsletters. The only email is the 6-digit password reset code you ask for.

Dog Cards

With an email account you can share a Dog Card, so your dogs visit other players' Dog Park and Yoyogi dog run. It is off until you switch it on in Settings.

  • What other players see: the two-word card name you pick from a list, your dogs' drawings, breeds, coats and outfits, the card name you pick for each dog (its own name only when that name is on our list), and how many paws the card got.
  • Never shown: your email, your account, or anything you typed. Cards have no free text at all: every part is picked from a fixed list, so there is nothing to read or reply to.
  • Paws: any player can give a card one paw a day. We keep who gave a paw for a few days, only so each player gives one a day.
  • Turning it off deletes the card and its paws as soon as the game is online. Hide this card in the game stops a card visiting you. Deleting your account deletes your card too.

Where it is kept

Account data and saves are stored with Cloudflare, who run the login service. Password emails are sent through Resend, who handle only that one email. Both keep the data only to do that job. You play on itch.io, and the game runs in your browser. itch.io has its own privacy policy for its own site.

Children

Doodle Doggo is a gentle game for everyone. If you are young, please ask a grown-up before making an account. You never need an account: guest play is complete.

Your choices

  • Play as a guest at any time, with no account.
  • Export a save code in Settings whenever you like, to keep your own copy.
  • Delete your account and everything we hold: write to contact@doodledoggo.app from the email address you signed up with, and say you want your account deleted. We delete the account, the password hash, the saves and any Dog Card, and tell you when it is done. This doesn't remove a save code you exported or the guest save on your own device. You can clear those yourself.
  • Ask what we hold about you in the same way.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will put the new date at the top and a short note on the game's page.
