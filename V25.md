# Paw Haven v2.5: Autumn & New Pups (desktop 1280×720 and phones)

Read `PIPELINE.md` (lanes, shared scope, tests), `V24.md` (the last release, same shape as this file), `PHONE.md` (phone minimums) and `CLAUDE.md` (design rules) first. **Where this file disagrees with older docs, this file wins.**

v2.5 adds:

- **Seasons on the real calendar** (Japan, northern hemisphere): spring Mar–May, summer Jun–Aug, autumn Sep–Nov, winter Dec–Feb. The yard, every town place, the garden and the walk scenes get a look for every season. Summer is today's art, byte for byte. Old saves pick the season up from the date.
- **Two festivals:** the **Autumn leaf festival** (1 Oct – 30 Nov) with leaf piles to jump in, Baker Bea's Harvest Stall in the Square (pumpkin and sweet potato treats) and a Leaf Beret + Autumn Scarf outfit; **Halloween** (24–31 Oct) with a costume parade in the Square, a Ghost Sheet and a Pumpkin Suit, and dog-safe treats that each teach one food lesson (no chocolate, no grapes or raisins, no xylitol). Festival things cost coins or nothing. Nothing is ever paid for with money.
- **Four new crayon breeds:** Poodle (black, white, apricot, chocolate), Border Collie (merle included, and the no-double-merle rule covers it), Samoyed, French Bulldog (with a short-snout welfare tip). They adopt, visit, have playdates, make mixes with real mix names, show in the family tree and the ancestry naming, and wear every outfit in every pose and age. **Every existing dog render stays byte-identical.**
- **Ride-alongs from TODO "Shop Day":** shorter phone wording for Gerald's steps 6–8, toasts never over a sheet title on phones, crisper house-card icons at 40 px, a garden watering callback and a "Water the garden" mission, and the Horgi × Horgi pups get their real name.

No new place, no new save version.

## Standing rules (every lane)

- **Nothing bad ever happens.** No fail states, no sickness, no running away. A festival that ends simply packs up. A blocked action gets a short, kind, two-sentence explanation.
- **No selling dogs, ever.** Sparkle stays cosmetic. Festival items are never in the Boutique or Kibble Corner; they are at the stall, for coins, during the festival, and they stay owned afterwards.
- **Real welfare and food safety.** Every new food is dog-safe and says so in two kind sentences. Chocolate, grapes, raisins and xylitol are only ever named in a "never" sentence. The French Bulldog's short snout gets a kind tip, not a penalty.
- **Look.** Detailed pencil-sketchbook world (style G: coloured-pencil washes drifting off two-stroke tapered pencil outlines, light hatching, Caveat labels, washi tape) and **deliberately crude crayon dogs**. No new palettes, no generic icons, no plain web UI. New art must sit next to the old art in a screenshot and look like the same hand drew it. A season must read at a glance in a 390 px phone crop.
- **Words.** Never "breed" as a verb, "stud", "litter price", "sell" (for dogs). The build rejects "nintendo", "pokémon" and "animal crossing". Plain short English, one idea per sentence, no semicolons in player text, no emoji in code, jokes welcome, tips two sentences at most. The `copy` suite checks this.
- **UI.** Every window is a centred popup (`openModal`), a bottom sheet on phones. Motion respects `[data-motion="off"]`.
- **Phones.** Every new screen, card and tap target passes the phone minimums on iPhone 13 (390×844) and Pixel 7 (412×915): 44×44 px taps, 15 px text (13 px captions), no sideways scroll, nothing covers the dog (`#dogHit`). Phone rules sit under `html[data-layout="phone"]` or `isPhone()`.
- **Economy.** Walks stay the main income (about 100 a day). The parade pays 10 coins once a day, leaf piles pay Happiness and Bond, not coins. Stall foods cost 6 to 14, stall clothes 50 to 65.
- **Save.** `S.v` stays 1. Every new field is optional and every reader defaults it. The coordinator adds the defaults to `migrate()` and `freshState()` in `00_core.js` (section 1); lanes default their own fields on `game:ready` as well.
- **Shared scope.** Prefix new top-level names with your lane tag: `fs` FESTIVAL, `br` is taken (breeding), so BREEDS uses `bd`. Art lanes have their own files. Only the cross-lane names in section 2 are unprefixed. Grep `game/src` for every new top-level name before committing.
- **Cross-lane calls** are always guarded: `if (typeof fn === 'function') fn(...)`.
- **Byte-identity.** The commit this spec ships in is tagged **`v25-base`**. Art lanes compare against `v25-base` (`git show v25-base:<path>`), never against `integration/v2.4`. Everything that existed at `v25-base` must render byte-identically unless this file says otherwise (summer season = identical, new keys and names = new).
- **Screenshots.** Art lanes check every new drawing in screenshots against the existing art at desktop 1280×720 and phone 390×844, at every breed, pose, age, season or time where it applies, and iterate until it matches. Save them under `game/shots_v25/<lane>/` and **commit them** on your lane branch (PNG, under 300 KB each, at most 60 per lane; the folder is removed before release). List the paths in your report.
- **Under the test harness** (`navigator.webdriver`) the season is pinned to `summer` and festivals are `off` unless a suite asks otherwise (section 1, "Test pins"), so every older suite sees the art it measured before.
