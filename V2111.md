# V2111: v2.11.1 "Halloween Costumes & Dog Sizes" (owner feedback, 17 Oct 2026)

Status: **building** on `integration/v2.11.1`. Base for the art check: `v2111-base` (branch at `17e513b`, v2.11). Urgent: Halloween 2026 runs until 2 Nov (Japan date), so the costumes ship now.

## 1. What the owner asked for

1. **Shelter text spacing (UI).** The adopt panel text (the "Doodle Doggo Shelter: pick your new best friend" heading, the name line, the description, the "Loves:" lines) is too cramped on phones. More line-height and paragraph spacing; the other shelter and adopt panels get the same check. Phone minimums stay: 44 px taps, 15 px text, no sideways scroll.
2. **German Shepherd neck gap (ART).** The gsd head floats above the body in the standing pose (seen on the Shelter floor). Fix the gsd so the head joins the body in every pose (stand, sit, lie, walk, sleep) and check the other six v2.11 breeds (akita, spitz, pomeranian, afghan, dane, mastiff) for the same gap.
3. **Dog sizes by real size (SIZE).** Reverses the v2.10 "every dog the same size" rule. Three classes, a scene factor only: the dog art never changes.
4. **Three new Halloween 2026 costumes (ART + wiring).** Sold only by the Pumpkin Patch Pop-up, `ed: 2026`, `hwTag()` "2026", inside `HW_WIN`.

## 2. Size classes (section 3 of the request; final table)

| Class | Factor | Breeds | Real-world guide (shoulder height) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **small** | 0.72 | chihuahua, pomeranian, pug, frenchie, dachs, corgi | under about 33 cm |
| **medium** | 0.86 | shiba, spitz, beagle, mutt, poodle, collie, husky | about 33 to 60 cm |
| **big** | 1.00 | golden, greyhound, samoyed, akita, afghan, dane, mastiff, gsd | about 55 cm and up, heavy build |

- **Owner's change (17 Oct, via the Game Manager): first every dog grows 1.25x from the v2.10 size, then the class factor applies.** Final scale = v2.10 scale x 1.25 x class, so big is about 1.25x, medium about 1.08x and small about 0.9x the v2.10 size. Because dogs grow, the walkable zones, the depth scale, the phone home crop and `BL_CROP`, the shelter floor, photo mode, pack walks and Dog Card visitors are re-checked and adjusted where needed (no dog cut off, none covering place buttons, the mailbox, the clipboard or the bowl). The SIZE lane may tune the factors by eye (say so in the report and in section 6).
- Data (owned by the SIZE lane this release): in `game/src/36_personality.js`, `BREED_SIZE = { key: 'small' | 'medium' | 'big' }` (every key in `PawGenes.BREEDS`, unknown keys = 'medium'), `SIZE_K = { small, medium, big }`, `sizeOf(d)` (the class name) and `sizeK(d)` (the factor). Puppies keep their age scaling on top (the factor multiplies, it does not replace).
- Where it applies: the roam scale (37_roam.js, the per-dog scale = `rmScale(y) * sizeK(d)`, and everything that uses it: tap boxes, spot candidates, no-go checks, crops), ball play (38_ball.js), walks and pack followers (`PawWalk.start` `o.pack` in mods/walkrun.js, through a size the game passes, the module never reads breed tables itself), the Shelter floor and meet spot (30_shelter.js `srScale`), photo mode (35_photo.js) and the Dog Card visitors (40_dogcards.js). Props that size a dog to fit (the nap door, a bed, the Mt. Fuji bench, a sofa) may keep their fixed size or take the factor, whichever looks right.
- Phones: the smallest dog keeps a tap target of at least 44 px, and no dog leaves the phone crop.
