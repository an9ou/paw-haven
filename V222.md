# V2.22 "Player levels and dog habits": build contract

Owner's ask (5 Nov 2026): "player levels unlock different places, Bond level unlocks the dog's habits and learning tricks."
Design and the owner's decisions: `docs/design/player-levels.md` (its **Decided** section is final). Branch `claude/player-levels`.
**Big content release.** It ships with v2.20 "Little Helps" and v2.21 "Five New Friends" behind the same `MS2_LIVE` switch, on the owner's launch date.
Big-release rules apply: parts one at a time, at most 2 helpers at once, one suite at a time, three reviews, one translation batch.

Two ladders:
- **You grow:** the player level, 1 to 20. It opens places, walk routes, Tokyo, the garden and kitchen, Fun Fair games and the shop rows that carry a Bond number today.
- **Your dog grows:** Bond 1 to 10, per dog. It keeps tricks, the Bond 2 party, the Bond track gifts and dog spots. New: one habit per Bond level.

## 0. Rules every part keeps

- **Nothing bad ever happens.**
  - XP is never lost, never bought and never paid in coins.
  - No streaks, no expiry and no sad faces.
  - The daily cap is a quiet "more tomorrow", never a stop sign.
- **Nothing open now ever locks again** (section 5). Owned items never show a lock.
- **Coins: none from levels.** No source in section 2 pays coins. Level 10 and level 20 give a title only. Walks stay the main income (section 2.5).
- **Welfare:** habits are true to real dogs, kind and cosmetic. "Does it more" changes poses and roaming only, never stats, Bond, coins or timers. Puppy and walk rules (`walkBlock`) are unchanged.
- **Off means off:** with `lvOn()` false the game is exactly v2.19.x / v2.20-off as now: the same gates, the same texts, the same weights, and no `S.lv` writes except the floor sync (section 5.4).
- **Save:** additive only, in `v222Fields(s, fresh)` and `lvStart(s)`. `S.v` stays 1, nothing is dropped, every old save loads.
- **Words:** English through `tt()` and `tdata()` only. No semicolons, emoji or "breed" as a verb. Use the dog's name instead of pronouns in new lines.
- **Phones first:** 360 x 740 and 390 x 844. Taps are 44 px or more, text is 13 px or more, no sideways scroll and nothing under the phone bar.
- **Art** only through `lvArt(k, ...args)` (`PawArt.LEVELS`), which returns `''` until the art is merged. Every caller has a plain fallback (`doodle('spark')`, `ICON()`).

## 1. The player level: name, HUD, level-up moment

### 1.1 Name
**Proposed: "Town level"** ("Town level 5"). It says what it does (how much of the town is open to you), it is short in every language, and the phone shows only the number.
Alternatives:
- "Neighbour level" (cosier: the town knows you better)
- "Explorer level" (more adventure)

The GM may offer these three in the release questions. The default is "Town level". Chips and short labels say "Level {n}". Sentences say "town level {n}".
There is no unit for XP on screen. Players see "80 to go for level 6".

### 1.5 The level-up moment
- `lvGain` raises `S.lv.lv` and pushes the new levels to `lvQueue`.
- `lvMaybeShow()` runs on the same calm modes as `maybeLevelUp` (yard, market, map, shelter), only while `levelQueue` is empty and `modal.hidden`. It is called at the end of `maybeLevelUp` (after `#luOk` closes) and from the `fw` calm-yard tick.
- So a Bond card always shows first, and the level card follows about 400 ms after it closes.
- Card: `openModal` with class `celebrate lv-lu`:
  - title `lv.lu_title` "Town level {n}!"
  - `lvArt('card')` (fallback: the Bond card's doodle strip with a spark)
  - one of `lv.lu_1..3`
  - "Unlocked:" (reuse `core.lu_unlocked`) and the `lvUnlocksFor(n)` list
  - `lv.lu_next` "Next: {line}"
  - OK `#lvOk`
- Several levels at once give one card (as Bond does) with `core.lu_many`.
- Fresh saves only, on the first level card: `lv.lu_gerald` "Gerald: That star is your town level. Each level opens a bit more of town."
- A reload keeps the card: `S.lv.shown` is the last level whose card was shown. When `S.lv.lv > S.lv.shown`, the card shows on the next calm yard.
- Old saves start with `shown = lv`, so they get no card at the switch (the letter in section 5 tells them).
- Sound: `audioCue('levelup')` plus `SFX.fanfare()`, as for Bond.
- Flavour lines:
  - `lv.lu_1` "The pigeons nod at you now. That is a big deal for pigeons."
  - `lv.lu_2` "Gerald tipped his hat at you. He means it."
  - `lv.lu_3` "Someone in town said your name right. Almost."
- Titles through `titleGive`: level 10 "Friendly Neighbour", level 20 "Town Favourite". They are given once and kept in `S.lv.got`.
- How to play: one lesson, `lv.lesson_title` "Town level" and `lv.lesson_text` "Care, walks and missions grow your town level. Each level opens new places, walks and shop things, and nothing ever locks again." It shows only while `lvOn()`.

## 2. XP

### 2.1 Sources
`lvGain(src, n, o)` is the only writer. Daily sources are counted against the daily cap and their sub-caps. Milestones are not capped, but each counts only once.
Most sources come from one listener `lvOnAct(a)` on the `act` bus. The rest are direct calls (the "Hook" column).

**Daily sources** (the cap is `LV_CAP = 150` XP a Japan day, `jstISO()`, reset at 00:00 JST)

| Source | XP | Sub-cap a Japan day | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Care and play: `feed`, `pet`, `fetch`, `toy`, `bath`, `nap`, `trick`, `scoop`, `photo` acts (one per act, a pack act counts once) | 2 | 15 acts (30 XP) | `lvOnAct` |
| A walk, only the first 5 a Japan day (the full-pay walks, `walkCount()` at the act) | park, town 8 · sunflower, river 10 · hilltop, bamboo 11 · woods, pier 12 · cliffs 13 · beach 14 (`LV_WALK`) | 5 walks | `lvOnAct` (`walk`, `a.area`) |
| Today's mission done | 20 | 1 | `msOnAct` where the stamp is given (`24_missions.js`) |
| A little help done (MS2) | 10 | 2 | where `ms2Life('help')` counts (`24b_missions2.js`) |
| A Dog Days wish done | 10 | 2 | `lvOnAct` (`wish`) |
| A Fun Fair round or town job finished (any `funfair` act, the stall and the photo contest included) | 5 | 4 | `lvOnAct` (`funfair`) |
| A garden harvest (`garden`, `what: 'harvest'`) or a dish cooked (`cook`) | 3 | 5 | `lvOnAct` |
| Friends (only while `frOn()`): a yard visit (`frvisit`), a gift sent (`frGiftSend` after the server says yes) | 5 | 2 | `lvOnAct`, plus one call in `frGiftSend` |

Most a Japan day: 30 + 70 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 20 + 15 + 10 = 205, so the 150 cap matters only for long sessions. A regular player (30 min) earns about 150, a light player (10 min) about 75.
Not sources: `buy`, `wear`, `decorate`, `travel` (except a first visit), `mail`, coins, anything paid. Walks 6 and later give no XP, the same rule as coins.

**Milestones** (not capped, each once)

| Milestone | XP | Once per | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| First visit to a place or a walk route (a town place, a trail, a Tokyo place, the Mt. Fuji Lookout, a shop front; a place and the route with the same id count once) | 15 | id, ever (`S.lv.seen`) | `lvOnAct` (`travel` place, `walk` area) |
| A dog reaches a new Bond level (2 to 10) | 20 | dog id and level (`S.lv.bl[id]` = the highest level counted) | in `addBond`'s level loop |
| The help-wanted board filled (MS2) | 40 | week | where `ms2Life('boards')` counts (`24d_week.js`) |
| A badge tier (MS2). Not when `msTierCheck(quiet)` backfills | 15 | tier | `msTierCheck` |
| A notebook page finished (MS2, new players only) | 10 | page | `msNbOnAct` when a page's last step ticks |

A level-up gives no XP. Under the harness every gain also needs `prefs.lvTest` (section 6).

### 2.4 Rules
- `S.lv.xp` only grows. `lvGain` ignores `n <= 0` and caps a daily gain at what is left of `LV_CAP`. The sub-caps live in `S.lv.src`.
- The day rolls in `lvDay()` (called by `lvGain` and `lvOpen`): when `S.lv.day !== jstISO()`, then `today = 0` and `src = {}`.
- Silent apart from the star's wiggle. No toast for XP. The cap line shows only in the sheet.

### 2.5 Economy check (walks stay the main income)
Levels pay nothing. The only effect on coins is that new players reach the better routes later than today (Bond 10 comes in about 7 days for a regular player, `tools/econ/journey.md`).

| Regular player, best route open (raw coins a full-pay walk) | Day 3 | Day 7 | Day 14 | Day 28 | Day 38+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today (Bond gates) | Maple Woods 60 | Seashell Beach 70 | 70 | 70 | 70 |
| v2.22 (levels) | Town Loop 30 | Hilltop 52 | Maple Woods 60 | Seaside Cliffs 62 | Seashell Beach 70 |

- Even at the start, 5 full walks on Sunny Park pay about 125 raw a day. That is still the biggest source, as the rule wants.
- Week 1 walk coins are about a third lower than today (best route about 39 a walk against 59). This delays the coin glut that the economy review found (keen players own everything by day 8, regular by day 25).
- Old players keep their routes (section 5), so their income is unchanged.

## 5. Old players: migration

### 5.1 Save fields (`v222Fields(s, fresh)`, 51_levels.js; called from `migrate()`, `freshState()` and `lvStart`)
`S.lv = { xp, lv, shown, day, today, src: { care, walk, ms, help, wish, ff, home, fr }, seen: { id: 1 }, bl: { dogId: highest Bond counted }, floor, got: { 10: 1, 20: 1 }, mail: 0 | 1, from: 'fresh' | 'old' }`
- Fresh save (made while lvOn): `xp 0, lv 1, shown 1, floor 0, from 'fresh', seen { yard: 1, house: 1 }`, `bl` = { starter: 1 }.
- Old save: `S.lv` is written by `lvStart(s)` the **first time a save loads with lvOn()**, not before. While the switch is off nothing is written except the floor (5.4).
- Per dog: nothing new. Habits come from Bond and breed, so there is nothing to store and nothing to migrate per dog.

### 5.2 `lvStart(s)` (old save, once, when `!s.lv`)
1. `tb` = the highest Bond in `s.dogs` (at least 1; an empty pack gives 1). Rehomed dogs do not count, as today.
2. `est = 90 x min(careDays, 90) + 15 x |dd.pv keys| + 20 x the sum over s.dogs of (Bond - 1) + 10 x (missions.stamps + 7 x missions.cards)` (each field missing counts 0).
3. `lv = max(lvOf(est), LV_FLOOR[tb])`, `xp = min(max(est, LV_AT[lv]), LV_AT[20])`, `shown = lv`, `floor = tb`, `from 'old'`.
4. `seen` = the `dd.pv` keys plus yard and house. Other places give their 15 on the next visit: a small kind extra.
5. `bl[id]` = each dog's Bond, so Bond levels already reached give no XP.
6. `got` gets the titles for 10 and 20 if `lv` reaches them, given quietly by `titleGive`.
7. One mailbox letter (`mailPush`, from Gerald, `S.lv.mail = 1`), whatever the number of dogs:
   - title `hb.mail_one` "{name}'s habits" for one dog, `hb.mail_many` "Your pack's habits" for two or more
   - text `hb.mail_text` "Gerald has been watching {names} closely, in a friendly way. Every habit so far is written down in Journal > Profile."
   - then `lv.mail_ps` "PS. You have a town level now: {n}. Everything you had open stays open."
8. No level-up card, no toasts, no Bond card. Habits up to each dog's Bond are shown at once, because they follow the Bond.

### 5.3 Nothing open now ever locks
- `LV_FLOOR` (3.1) puts every Bond-b row at level `LV_FLOOR[b]` or lower, and `lvStart` starts at `LV_FLOOR[tb]` or higher.
- `wOpen` also opens any row with `bond <= S.lv.floor`. So a later table edit can never relock it.
- Owned items never show a lock (3.3, `05_market_shops.js`).
- Rehoming the top-Bond dog no longer closes anything: today it would, through `topBond()`.

### 5.4 Edge cases
| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| Several dogs | the floor is the top Bond of any home dog. `est` sums every dog's Bond. One letter |
| A dog rehomed later | levels do not move. The dog's habits leave with it (they come from its record) |
| Guest saves, itch.io, doodledoggo.app `/play/` | the same as any local save. Live only with `MS2_LIVE` |
| claude.ai artifact | frozen at v2.14: never sees v2.22 |

While lvOn is off and `s.lv` exists, `s.lv.floor = max(floor, topBond)`, so Bond earned while off is never locked when it turns back on. While on: `lv = max(lv, LV_FLOOR[floor])` |
| Cloud save | `S.lv` is inside the save JSON, and the Worker stores it as it is. A pick between two saves stays whole-save (newer wins, the older is backed up, `22b_cloud.js`); there is no field merge. A pre-v2.22 cloud save that loads on v2.22 runs `lvStart` like any old save |
