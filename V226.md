## 1. What the player sees

- While you are away, neighbourhood dogs come round to **your yard** and play with what you put out. When you come back, the visitor who came
  most recently is in the yard (one at a time, on the town visitor's spot, `visitorSpot()`), with a name tag and a line:
  "Biscuit came by from Elm Row and tried the Tug Post!" Tap: the visitor's card (name, breed, family, which toy, times visited, the
  thank-you gift if any) with **Pet** (once a day per visitor, +3 Happiness), **Visitors Book** and **Wave bye** (the next visitor, if
  one is waiting, steps in).
- **Visitors Book**: a page per visitor (20). Met: the crayon dog, name, family, first met date, times visited, a paw sticker if it left one,
  a star on the rare ones. Not met: a pencil silhouette and "???"; a rare visitor shows its hint ("Comes on snowy days"). Pressed flowers are
  counted on the first page. Open it from the visitor card, the Care tray card "Visitors" (yard) or the Journal tab "Visitors".
- Never a dog to keep, adopt or buy; visitors always go home. Nothing bad happens: no visitor is missed for good, every one comes back.
  No timers or "only today" wording (rare ones come back every snowy day, every autumn...).

## 2. When visitors come (deterministic)

- Three visit **slots** per JST day (`jstISO`), at JST 10:00, 15:00 and 20:00 plus 0-90 minutes from `hashId(seed|day|k)`.
- How many slots are open that day depends on the yard's **pull** now: each of the 4 new toys out = 2, each older attracting item out
  (Sandbox, Bird Bath, Stepping Stones, Hammock, Garden Bench, Doggy Ramp) = 1. Pull 0 -> 1 slot, 1-2 -> 2 slots, 3+ -> 3 slots.
  So at most **3 visits a day**.
- A slot is a visit when its time falls between `S.visitors.seen` (the last moment the game was open; refreshed every 30 s while the page is
  visible) and now, the gap is at least **20 minutes** (`YV_AWAY`), and it is at most 48 h old. A slot you were present for is simply
  skipped (they come "while you are away"). Checked on `game:ready` and when the page becomes visible again.
- Who comes: every visitor whose condition holds at the slot's time and who has not come that JST day; weight common 1, rare 3, and x2
  for one not met yet; picked with `seeded(hashId(seed|day|k))`. The toy it played with: its favourite if out, else its condition toy,
  else one of the attracting items out (seeded), else none ("had a good sniff round the garden").
- Conditions read the slot's time: season = `ENV.season` override or the slot's month; weather = `ENV.weather` override or the same seeded
  weather as `weatherNow()` for that date and 8-hour period; evening = `ENV.time` dusk/night or a local hour 17-23.

## 4. Thank-you gifts (capped)

- At most **one gift per JST day** (`S.visitors.gday`), on a seeded 40 % of visits. Kinds: a paw **sticker** on that visitor's book page
  (first time only, else a flower), a **Bone-shaped Biscuit** (+1 in the food bag), a pressed **flower** (counted in the book), or rarely
  **10 coins**, at most once in 7 days (`S.visitors.cday`). So gifts add at most 10 coins a week: walks stay the main income.

## 6. Save (additive, `v226Fields` in `migrate()` and `freshState()`)

```
S.visitors = { seed: string, since: ms, seen: ms,          // seed fixed at first load; since = first load; seen = last time open
  met: { [id]: { d: 'YYYY-MM-DD' (first met, local), n: visits, last: ms, stk: 0|1, pet: 'YYYY-MM-DD' } },
  q: [{ id, t, toy, g }] (waiting to be shown, at most 3), log: [{ id, t, toy, g }] (last 20, for "While you were out"),
  gday: 'JST day of the last gift', cday: ms of the last coin gift, flowers: n }
```
Old saves get `seen = since = now` (no flood of visitors on the first load). Toys are ordinary `S.decor` entries (no `S.yardToys`).
Event: `emit('visitors:arrived', { list })` after a catch-up with arrivals, for v2.25's "While you were out" page to list them.
Test gate: `yvOn()` is false under webdriver unless `prefs.yvTest` (like `ddOn`), so older suites never see a visitor.
