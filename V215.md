# V215: v2.15 "Around Town" (the new town map, new places, free roaming)

Status: **design, owner decisions recorded** (MAP design lead, 22 Oct 2026; decisions in section 17) on `design/v2.15-map`. The owner chose "design now, build after": the build starts only after v2.14 (languages, `integration/v2.14`) is released, from that `main`. Nothing in `game/`, `world/`, `dogs/` or `mods/` changes on this branch. Clickable preview: https://claude.ai/artifact/Fy6mzAFggiHUBa6xRGKKJU (source `docs/design/v215/preview/index.html`, screenshots `docs/design/v215/*.png`). As-built notes go in section 18.

## 1. What the owner asked for

1. **Map:** richer, more detailed and lively. Roads and paths natural, organic and winding, never straight, grid-aligned, neat or symmetrical.
2. **Buildings are buttons:** each one is a distinct, clickable element with hover and press states (a lift, a glow, a squash). Every building has its own look: a player knows each one by its design alone, without a label. The four shops (all on Market Street today) are separated and scattered round town.
3. **New places:** a training ground (tricks and agility); several run tracks for the walk runner, **each its own place on the map** that you can visit and run (owner's choice), still offered as routes by the Walk button; a swimming pool area.
4. **Dog movement:** a much bigger roaming area in every place. Today the dogs mostly pace left and right; they should wander freely over the whole walkable ground, in 2D with depth, keeping the v2.10 rules (no-go boxes, `rmPause`, phone crops, size classes).

**Standing rules kept** (CLAUDE.md): nothing bad ever happens (the pool is shallow, with a ramp and life vests, and a kind two-sentence water lesson); the pencil-sketchbook world and the crude crayon dogs; walks stay the main income (about 100 a day); the save migrates additively and old saves load (`market` maps cleanly); the phone layout works (PHONE.md); the Japan scenery stays; seasons, festivals, Halloween, Winter Lights and New Year keep working on the new map and in the new places; every new string goes through the v2.14 tables (`tt`, `tdata`) and is translated into all 10 languages before the release.

## 3. Roads

- **A graph, drawn and walked.** Nodes are junctions and each place's door (the building's base). Edges are Catmull-Rom curves through hand-placed via points, never straight lines. Three types: **road** (wide, cobble-cream with a dashed centre: home → Kibble Corner → the main bridge → the Square → the Café → the Salon → Maple Woods), **lane** (narrower, the side streets), **trail** (dotted footpaths: up the hill, along the riverbank, round the coast, through the forest).
- **Loops, not a tree:** home ↔ Sunflower Lanes ↔ the north bridge ↔ the Shelter ↔ the Square ↔ the main bridge ↔ home; Dog Park ↔ Training Ground ↔ the Salon road; Woods ↔ Bamboo ↔ Sprout Cart ↔ Hilltop; Beach ↔ Pier ↔ Cliffs ↔ Woods. Three river crossings: the main road bridge, the north lane bridge, and the Riverside Trail footbridge (which is the `river` place itself).
- **Data:** world F exports `PawArt.MAP_ROADS = { nodes: { id: [x, y] }, edges: [{ a, b, t, pts }] }` (the same samples the art draws, so the dog follows the drawn road). 31 edges in the preview (`EDGES` in the preview source is the starting table).
- **Travel walk (new):** tapping a building sends the leader's crayon dog (the walking dog art at map scale 0.5) running along the shortest road path from where the pack is to the place (Dijkstra on edge length), about 1.2–1.6 s, **tap to skip**, instant with Motion off, then `travelTo(k)` as today. The pack's current place is where the dog stands when the map opens. Locked places shake and show the Bond lock (no travel), as today's "Nope" line.
- Filler (little houses, trees, fields, the forest, waves) is drawn where no road, river, building or caption is, like today's `free()`.

## 4. Buildings as buttons

Every building is `<g class="mb" data-area="<key>" role="button" tabindex="0" aria-label="<name>">` with four layers the art draws and the CSS animates:

| Layer | What | States |
|---|---|---|
| `.mb-shadow` | soft ink ellipse under the base, outside the lift | shrinks and fades on hover (the building "rises"), grows on press |
| `.mb-lift` | the whole building and its caption, `transform-box: fill-box`, origin at the base | hover / focus: `translateY(-12) scale(1.06)` with a springy ease; press: `translateY(4) scale(.95)` in 70 ms; picked: a small hop |
| `.mb-glow` | a pink radial halo plus a marching dashed crayon ring | fades in on hover, focus and while picked |
| `.mb-hit` | a transparent ellipse over building + caption, static (never lifts, so the hover never flickers) | receives the pointer |

- Locked: hatched grey veil, padlock, a pink "Bond N" chip; pressing shakes it.
- Keyboard: Tab moves between buildings in map order, Enter or Space presses, a dashed pink focus ring. `prefers-reduced-motion` / Motion off: no lift, no ring march, the glow only.
- **Phones:** no hover. `pointerdown` adds the press state; a tap (moved less than 6 px) presses and travels; a long-press (500 ms) shows the name tooltip (PHONE.md rule) without travelling. A drag pans the map as today.
- Tags "new" (pink) and "moved" (gold) stickers on the 8 new and moved places for the first 7 real days after the update (`S.mapNew` set of keys, cleared by visiting), so returning players find them.
- Seasons on every building: `snowcap` roof caps and a cold wash in winter, autumn and spring tree colours, Halloween pumpkins and Winter Lights strings on the Square, the Café, the Boutique and the yard fence (the same `k.hw` / `k.lt` / `k.ny` streams as world A), New Year pine at the Shelter and the Station, and `k.jp` keeps Fuji, the torii and the Japan details.

## 6. New places

### 6.1 Training Ground (`training`, Bond 3, outdoor, 1600 wide)

- **Agility course** (tag `ag`, `44_agility.js`): the leader (or a picked pack dog, `pkWith`) runs a course of five obstacles: weave poles, jump bar, tyre, tunnel, pause table. One big **Go!** button per obstacle with a gentle timing window (wider on phones and for puppies); a late tap still clears the obstacle, just slower. **No fail state:** every run finishes and earns 1 to 3 rosettes.
- **Puppies** run the puppy course (tunnel, table, a bar on the ground): real welfare, young joints don't jump. Seniors (if age shows it) get the same low bar.
- **Rewards:** Bond +10 for each dog's first run of the day; coins 10 / 20 / 30 by rosettes for the first two runs of the day, pack-wide cap **40 a day** (same as ball play's `BL_COIN_DAY`); Energy −10 per run. Best rosettes per dog shown on the scoreboard (`S.ag.best`).
- **Trick stage:** trick training (`15_trick_training.js`) done here counts one extra step per session (the "Training Ground bonus"), no coins.
- **Care note** (first visit, `gold` toast): "Agility is play, so keep runs short and stop while your dog still wants more. Soft ground and low bars keep young and old joints happy."
- Roaming: dogs use the props on their own (weave, jump, tyre, tunnel, table), as in the preview.

### 6.2 Splash Pool (`pool`, Bond 2, outdoor, 1600 wide)

- **Shallow dog pool** with a wide step, a wooden ramp out and a lifeguard chair; no deep end. The first visit gives a free **Doggy Life Vest** (wearable, body slot, `price: 0`, never sold; worn automatically in the water, `S.pool.vest`).
- **Paddle** (once a day per dog): the dog walks the ramp, paddles, climbs out, shakes (+15 Happiness, Cleanliness to at least 60 after the rinse, the `shake` pose and a wet-dog line). Flat-faced breeds (pug, frenchie, and the brachy list in `BREED_PERS`) paddle on the step only: a kind, true detail.
- **Float fetch:** v2.10 ball play with a floating toy (`blStart(toy)` with `o.water`), its coins inside the existing `BL_COIN_DAY` cap.
- **Sprinkler:** a free hop-through for fun (Happiness only).
- **No coins** from the pool itself (keeps the economy).
- **Lesson** (first visit, `gold` toast): "Not every dog loves water, so let them wade in at their own pace. A life vest and a ramp to climb out make every swim a safe one."
- **Winter:** the pool stays open under a striped warm "Winter Dome" tent (art), so no activity disappears for a season.

- **The old routes get the same gate in their places:** Sunny Park, Riverside Trail, Maple Woods, Seashell Beach, Hilltop Meadow, Lighthouse Pier, and the Square for the Town Loop. The map's checkered flag marks them all.
- The **Walk button** keeps the route carousel (`17_walk_route_carousel.js`) from home, with the three new cards appended in Bond order.

## 8. Free roaming, redesigned

### 8.1 Why the dogs pace today

The v2.10 floor is one band per place, x 60–940 and feet y about 410–590 (`RM_ZONES`), because the scenes' ground only starts around y 400: the art puts the horizon low and the props on one line. Big crayon dogs (a big dog is about 190 units wide at the front) in an 880 × 180 strip can only go left or right. The preview's "Today" panel shows the real Dog Park with that strip.

### 8.2 The new ground (art + code)

- **Deep floors.** Every v2.15 scene puts its horizon higher (the floor's back line at about y 330–360 outdoors, about 380 indoors where the floor meets the wall) and places floor props at several depths. The floor polygon is drawn by the art and published with it: `PawArt.FLOOR[name] = { w, back, front, poly: [[x, y]...], props: [{ id, base, box }], spots: [{ id, x, y, tags, act }] }`.
- **Wide places (1600 world units)** for the big outdoor places and every new place: park, dogpark, beach, hilltop, woods, river, pier, training, pool, sunflower, bamboo, cliffs. Deep only (1000 wide) for the yard (its decorations' saved spots are in 1000-wide units), house, square (festival and Halloween props are placed in 1000-wide units), cafe, vet, salon, fujihill and the four storefronts. Tokyo places keep the v2.10 floor in v2.15 (section 17, decision 1).
- **Floor area:** the Dog Park goes from 880 × 180 (158k) to about 1560 × 257 (401k), **2.5×**; a deep-only place about 1.6×.
- **Layering:** floor props are drawn in their own `<g data-fy="<base y>">` groups inside `#floorG`; the roam engine sorts dogs and floor props by feet y (re-appending only when the order changes), so a dog walks behind the tunnel or the bench and in front of it. The backdrop stays behind everything.
- **Depth scale:** per place, linear from `kBack` at the back line to `kFront` at the front: outdoors 0.5 → 1.05, indoors 0.7 → 1.05, times `RM_BASE` and `sizeK(d)` as today (`rmKd`). Speed scales with depth (`0.55 + 0.45 t`), so a far dog moves slower on screen.

### 8.3 The movement model (`43_ground.js`, tag `gr`)

- **Path finding:** a 20-unit grid over the floor polygon minus the hard boxes (feet, 8-unit margin: the live `rmScan()` boxes plus `FLOOR.props` boxes), A* with 8 neighbours, string-pulled, then sampled along a Catmull-Rom curve every 12 units. Cached per place and box set; re-planned when a decoration moves or a path is blocked.
- **Gaits:** stroll 120, trot 220, run 380 units a second (times the depth factor), easing in and out; the dog's facing flips only after 6 units of sideways travel and 250 ms since the last flip (no flicker).
- **Things to do** (picked by weight from `BREED_PERS`: energy, zoomies, follows, attention, `spots` and `trait`; the preview's `LIKE` table is the idea):

| Behaviour | What the dog does | Mostly |
|---|---|---|
| wander | a curving walk through 1–2 random points to a spot, then idle or sit | everyone |
| sniff trail | a zig-zag with sniff stops | beagle, dachs (`sniff`, `dig`) |
| zoomies | a figure-eight loop over open ground at a run, `zoomies` face (v2.13 `exTrig`) | husky, greyhound, high `zoomies` |
| follow | trots behind another dog for a few seconds | high `follows`, mutt |
| play pair | two dogs chase in a circle, a play bow, `happy` | high energy pairs |
| patrol | walks the edge or the gate | aloof, guard traits (shiba, chihuahua) |
| herd | circles the pack's centre | collie (`herd` trait) |
| rest | shade, sun or bed spots: `down`, `sleep` | pug, low energy |
| spot act | the place's own props: bowl (drink), bench (sit up), dig patch, weave, jump, tyre, tunnel, table. Not the pool paddle: that is the player's once-a-day pool act (`45_pool.js`, section 6.2), its water spots carry weight 0 in the roam | per place `spots` |
| come to the front | petted dogs and attention asks (v2.10 rules) | everyone, dachs most |

- **Separation:** soft push between dogs closer than 90 × depth scale, never into a box. Four dogs at most, as today.
- **Camera (wide places):** the view window (1000 desktop, the phone crop ≤ 700) follows the focus (the pack's centre, the leader counted twice, or the petted dog), with a ±150 dead zone, an 0.8 s ease and the ends clamped. Drag to look on desktop and phones; the v2.10 look chips `#rmLookL` / `#rmLookR` point at dogs out of view; ball play keeps following the ball; photo mode freezes the camera where the player put it.
- **Kept as is:** `rmPause(why)` (ball play, photo, modals, training, the agility run), the live no-go scan, the tap rects (`RM_TAP`), petting only the touched dog, `sizeK`, positions not saved, `prefs.rmTest` for autonomous walking under the harness.
- **Hooks into the v2.10 engine** (ROAM 2 lane owns these lines in `37_roam.js` and `38_ball.js`): `rmZoneOf` → `grFloor(place)` when `grOn(place)`; `rmScale` → `grScale(place, y)`; `rmPick` / `rmTo` paths → `grPath(d, x, y, o)`; `BL_FLOOR` rows → `grFloor`. No `grOn` (Tokyo, the harness without `prefs.grTest`) = the v2.10 code path byte for byte.

| Name | What |
|---|---|
| `grOn(place)` | the place has a v2.15 floor: `PawArt.FLOOR[place]` exists, and under the harness `prefs.grTest` |
| `grFloor(place)` | `{ w, back, front, kB, kF, poly, no, spots }` |
| `grScale(place, y)` | the base depth scale at feet y (`rmKd` multiplies the size class) |
| `grPath(d, x, y, o)` | a smooth path for dog d to x, y (`o.gait`, `o.via`, `o.act`) |
| `grCam()` / `grFocus(d)` | the camera window; focus on one dog for a while |
| `__paw.gr` | test hook `{ on, floor, path, cam, sim(place, secs) }` |

## 14. Risks

1. **Art volume** is the biggest cost: 25 map buildings, 8 new scenes, 3 strips, and 15 redrawn scenes. Mitigation: decision 1 (section 17) can cut the wide scenes; every redraw is behind `o.v215`, so a scene that isn't ready falls back to its v2.10 art and band (`grOn` false) and the release still works.
2. **Old art suites** (`v26_scenes`, `v27_scenes`, `v28_scenes`, map hashes) must stay green: everything new is behind `o.v215`; only the game passes it.
3. **Roaming regressions** in systems that use the floor: ball play (`BL_FLOOR`, `BL_CROP`), photo mode, pack care, Dog Card visitors (`dcRoom`), the Halloween pop-up, the bath and the yard nap door. Mitigation: `grOn` false keeps the v2.10 path; `v215_ground` runs every system in every place.
4. **Phone:** 25 buildings make the map denser; camera follow and drag-to-look could fight a tap. Mitigation: 6 px tap threshold (as the map today), camera pauses for 2 s after a drag.
5. **Performance** on older phones: wide scenes are bigger SVGs; A* per dog. Mitigation: path cache, re-plan at most twice a second, floor props are plain groups (no filters), the scene cache key gains `v215`.
6. **i18n load:** about 2,450 new translations must land before the release; long German and Thai captions on the map.
7. **Economy creep:** agility coins plus ball play. Mitigation: caps and the 7-day sim.
8. **Old saves** standing in `market` or in a place whose scene isn't ready: `market` stays valid; unknown keys go home.
