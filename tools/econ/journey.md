## Three player journeys

With today's numbers, coins stop mattering early. A keen player (90 minutes a day) owns everything sold all year by **day 8**, a regular player (30 minutes) by **day 25**, and a light player (10 minutes) by **day 53**. After that, coins pile up with nothing left to buy.

### What each player does in a day (the model)

| Player | Minutes a day | Walks | Other play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 10 | 3 | Care trio once, mailbox, calendar, 2 of 3 missions, one wish, a quick garden check |
| Regular | 30 | 6 | Plus all missions and wishes, a few Fun Fair rounds, ball play, tricks in the Square, the garden, the Saturday stall and photo contest |
| Keen | 90 | 22 | Plus every Fun Fair and toy cap, agility, beach digs, and the game-day bonuses about 4 times |

Everyone spends about 1 coin a minute on kibble plus a treat or two, and buys the cheapest thing they can afford that is not owned yet.

- **Walls:** the only one is the light player saving for the houses: up to 7 days in a row with nothing new bought (days 20 to 53). Nobody waits long for a small item.
- **Coin glut:** keen from day 8, regular from day 25, light from day 53. The Winter Market (3,850) and the yearly festival stalls (800) only delay it by a day or two for regular and keen players.
- **Bond 10 comes fast:** day 2 to 3 for keen, about day 7 for regular, about day 15 for light. The Bond track, meant as a long goal, is a first-week goal for most players.

### The paid shop proposal's effect

| Change | Light | Regular | Keen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Today: everything owned on | day 53 | day 25 | day 8 |
| +20% walk coins, no cap: income | +16% | +14% | +17% |
| +20% walk coins, no cap: everything owned on | day 47 | day 22 | day 7 |
| +20% walk coins, at most +40 a day: income | +6% | +3% | +1% |
| Kind shop items also sold for coins (12 looks at 600, Robo Scoop 4,000, Sprinkler and Water Bowl 2,500 each: 16,200 in all): everything owned on | day 78 | day 36 | day 11 |

The uncapped +20% gives a paying keen player about 800 extra coins a day, when coins already have nothing to buy. It does not change how fun the game is for payers, and it makes free players feel slower. A cap of +40 a day keeps it a small thank-you. The coin prices of the kind shop items are the better lever: they add 16,200 coins of good-feeling goals for everyone.
