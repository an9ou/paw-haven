# v2.29.1 "Balance follow-ups" (contract)

The owner's choices of 13 Nov 2026 on `docs/design/balance-options-2026-11-13.md`. A small update after the 19 Nov launch of v2.26-v2.29. Suite `v2291_balance`. No new save fields, no price changes, walks stay the main income.

- **A. Free swap, home missions only** (`MS_HOME`, `msSwapPool`, `game/src/24_missions.js`): the one free daily swap draws from walk, walk_fresh, walk_dressed, toy, toy_new, bath, wear, photo, buy, buy_food_new, feed_new, treasure, wish, decorate, garden_plant, garden_water, harvest, cafe. With none eligible it falls back to the whole pool. The day's first draw is unchanged. Hooks: `ms.swapPool()`, `ms.home`.
- **B1. Chapter 3** (`57b_story.js`, `sm.c3_board`): a Gerald line under the tasks points at the Town projects board (only while the board is live, `prOn()`).
- **B2. Boutique row** (`05_market_shops.js`, `shop.ks_looks`): a last row "Kind shop looks: {n} coins each" opens the Kind shop on the Looks tab (only with `ksOn()`; coin price from `KS_LOOK_COINS`). Hook `shop.open(k)`.
- **B3. Tomorrow line** (`53_welcome.js`, `wb.t_proj`): while a town project is being saved for, the generic tomorrow lines give way to "The {name} is {pct}% there. Any coins help." The soon-ripe crop, Saturday and Monday lines still come first. `wb.tmrwText(wd)` takes an optional weekday for tests.
- **C. XP cap** (`51_levels.js`, `lv.today_full2`): `LV_CAP` stays 150; at the cap the sheet says "That is all the growing for today. The town projects and the Visitors Book never stop."
- **D. `tools/playtest.js`**: an `aria-disabled="true"` Start button is blocked; the toast is read 2.5 s after pressing Start, and a block is reported as "walk: blocked (...)".
- **News**: `NEWS_V2291` (date 2026-11-20 is a placeholder; the Game Manager sets the real one).
