# V2.20 "Missions v2": design contract

Goal (owner, 4 Nov 2026): more things to do every day, a reason to come back each week, long goals after Bond 10 and a path for days 2-14
of a new player, paid mostly in things that are not coins. Design: `docs/design/missions-v2.md` (its "Decided" section is final).
Branch `claude/missions-v2`. **Big content release:** it goes to `main` only with the owner's go-ahead and launch date (CLAUDE.md).

Owner's choices (4 Nov 2026): 1 mission + 2 little helps a day (helps: Bond, no coins; all 3 give a dog-safe treat); Gerald's help-wanted
board, any 3 of 5, 50 coins, Monday 00:00 JST, every 4 boards an outfit; badges pay cosmetics only; a 14-day notebook for new players;
old saves get backfilled badges but no notebook; no season pass; rewards arrive by themselves (no claim chores).

## 0. Rules that every part keeps
- **Nothing bad ever happens.** Nothing expires with a loss, no streak counter anywhere, a missed day or week just brings a new one, never a sad face.
  No puppy or litter goal anywhere (welfare). Food rewards are dog-safe treats from `FOOD` only.
- **Coins:** today's mission 40 raw + a stamp (unchanged), the board 50 raw a week. Little helps, the pouch, badges, the notebook and growing up pay **0 coins**.
- **Calm:** the top-bar clipboard (`#msBtn`) and the phone "..." entry (`#mmMs`) show a soft crayon dot (`.ms-dot`) when something is newly done or the
  board is ready, never a red number; the existing `n/1` mission count stays as it is. Toasts batch through `toast(text, kind, { group, many })`
  and wait for Gerald and the parade (`msGuideBusy()`). Popups only for the stamp card prize (as today), the notebook day 7 and 14 gifts, the Crayon Medal.
- **Save:** additive in `v220Fields(s, fresh)` (section 1). `S.v` stays 1, nothing is dropped, every old save loads.
- **Words:** English only through `tt()` / `tdata()` (section 4); one translation batch at the end (part H). No semicolons or emoji in player text
  (`copy` suite), never "breed" as a verb, "stud", "sell" for dogs.
- **Phones first:** 360 x 740 and 390 x 844, crayon pills, text never under 13 px, taps 44 px or more, no sideways scroll, nothing under the phone bar.
  Desktop: the same sheet in two columns (design section 5).
- **Under the test harness** every Missions v2 progress (helps, pouch, week, badges, notebook, growing up) needs `prefs.msTest` (`msAllowed()`),
  like today's mission, so older suites keep their coin and item totals. Counters (`life`, `wkAreas`) always count.
- **Art** only through `ms2Art(k, ...args)` (`PawArt.MISSIONS`, section 5) with a plain fallback; no dog art changes.

## 1. Save fields (`v220Fields(s, fresh)`, `game/src/24b_missions2.js`)
Called from `migrate()` (old and cloud saves, `fresh` false), `freshState()` (new saves, `fresh` true) and `msFields()` (every roll). All under `S.missions`.

| Field | Shape | Default (new save) | Old save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `help` | `{ date: jstISO of the list, list: [{ id, n, p, done: jstISO or null, arg? }] (2 rows), sw: '' \| 'm' \| 'h' (the row kind swapped today) }` | `{ date: '', list: [], sw: '' }` | same; rolls on first open |
| `pouch` | `{ day: jstISO of the last pouch, n: its treat name, c: pouches ever }` | `{ day: '', n: '', c: 0 }` | same |
| `life` | `{ ms, help, harvest, cook, bath, trick, fetch, boards, bf }` lifetime counters, `bf: 1` once backfilled | all 0, `bf: 1` | backfilled once (below) |
| `tr` | `{ trackId: 'b' \| 's' \| 'g' \| 'c' }` the highest tier given per badge track (part C) | `{}` | `{}` (C gives earned tiers quietly, one letter) |
| `wk` | `{ w: Monday jstISO of this board, list: [{ id, n, p, done }] (5 notes), got: the w whose board was filled ('' none), card: 0-3 stamps on the neighbour card }` | `{ w: '', list: [], got: '', card: 0 }` | same |
| `wkAreas` | `{ routeKey: jstISO last walked }`, kept to the last 7 Japan days | `{}` | `{}` |
| `nb` | `null` (no notebook) or `{ pages: pages open 0-14, done: { pageNo: [step indexes] }, got: [pageNo gifted] }` | `{ pages: 0, done: {}, got: [] }` when `S.fw.fresh` | `null` (old saves get no notebook) |
| `grow` | `{ dogId: { momentId: jstISO } }` (part F) | `{}` | `{}` |

Backfill of `life` (once, `bf` missing; each value is `max(stored, computed)`, so a second run never lowers it):
`ms` = `stamps + 7 x cards`; `cook` = the dishes in `S.recipes.best` (cooked at least once; `known` also holds the starter recipes); `harvest` = the sum of `S.garden.harvests`; `bath` = `S.dd.wk.bath`;
`trick` = every dog's `tricks[n].shows` summed + `S.dd.wk.trick`; `fetch` = `S.bl.n + S.dd.wk.ball`; `help` and `boards` 0.
Counted by `ms2Count(a)` on the `act` bus from v2.20 on: `ms` (a finished mission), `help` (a finished little help), `harvest` (garden harvest),
`cook`, `bath` (each dog bathed), `trick` (each trick act), `fetch` (fetch and toy acts), `boards` (part D, a filled board).
A part may add sub-fields only additively, with defaults here in `v220Fields`, and lists them in its as-built notes.
