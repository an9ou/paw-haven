# Paw Haven v1.7: four new breeds, ten in total (desktop only)

Client: "please include more dog breeds in the game, such as chihuahua, pug, greyhound, etc. Make it 10 types of dogs in total."

## The new breeds

| key | Default name | Breed | Size | Personality | Joke line | Look (crude H crayon style, one big joke trait) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `chihuahua` | Peanut | Chihuahua | small | Tiny, fearless, dramatic guard dog | "I am the security system." | Apple head, ENORMOUS bat ears, bulgy shiny eyes, a tiny shivery body and twig legs. Default coat: fawn/cream. |
| `pug` | Dumpling | Pug | small | Snorty, cuddly, lazy, food-loving | "snort." | Flat squished face with wrinkle lines, a black mask, big round bug eyes, a tongue tip out, a tight double-curl tail and a round body. Default coat: fawn with a black mask. |
| `greyhound` | Rocket | Greyhound | large | Gentle couch potato who sprints in bursts | "45 mph. Then a 20-hour nap." | VERY long thin legs, a deep chest and tiny waist, a long narrow head, small folded rose ears and a long thin whip tail. Default coat: blue-grey with a white chest. |
| `beagle` | Bagel | Beagle | medium | Nose-led, food-obsessed, bays loudly | "Smelled that from three streets away." | Long floppy ears, a tricolour coat (black saddle, tan, white legs and chest) and a white-tipped tail held up like a flag. Default coat: classic tricolour. |

- All 10 breeds are adoptable as starters at the shelter, each once, with the Boy/Girl choice. The new breeds also join the daily rescue pool.
- Breed size feeds the v2 litter rules: chihuahua and pug are small, beagle is medium, greyhound is large.

## Dogs (art): `PawArt.dog('chihuahua' | 'pug' | 'greyhound' | 'beagle', o)`
- All 29 poses, all outfits (fitted per breed), coat and seed support, odd eyes, facing left, and `dogHead`.
- The 4-leg check covers every frame.
- Keep the same 240×200 canvas with the ground at y=186. The greyhound must fit with its long legs; scale it down where needed, but keep the legs comically long.
- `PawArt.DOGS` gets the 4 new entries. The order is: shiba, corgi, golden, dachs, husky, mutt, chihuahua, pug, greyhound, beagle.
- The 6 existing breeds stay byte-identical.

## Genes: `mods/genes.js`
- Add the 4 breeds to `STARTER_GENES`, `SHADES`, `FREQ` and `BREEDS`, keeping it realistic within the existing loci. Starter genotypes must produce the default looks above.
  - **Chihuahua:** almost any colour. Fawn, cream, black and tan, chocolate, blue, and piebald are all possible. Merle occurs in about 10% of rescues; the double-merle block still applies.
  - **Pug:** fawn (red/cream base with the breed's black mask drawn via `dark`) or solid black. Rare apricot. No merle and no piebald.
  - **Greyhound:** black, blue, fawn, red and white-spotted. Brindle isn't modelled (note this). No merle.
  - **Beagle:** tricolour (black saddle, tan, white; S/sp) as the default, plus lemon, red-and-white, and chocolate tricolour. No merle.
- Phenotype palettes for the new breeds must match their default art colours exactly.
- Extend the Node test so it covers all 10 starters.

## Audio: `PawAudio.bark`, new voices
- **Chihuahua:** a high, sharp, yappy bark, often in rapid bursts. Its `alert` is a 4–5 yap burst.
- **Pug:** a snorty, grunty "rrf" with lots of `huff`, snore and sneeze. Barks are short and muffled.
- **Greyhound:** rarely barks. A soft, low "roo" and `whine`. When it does bark, it's a single quiet woof. It "roos" (howls) when another dog howls.
- **Beagle:** a loud, musical bay ("aroooo") for `alert` and `howl`, plus regular woofs.
- Recognise the breed names "Chihuahua", "Pug", "Greyhound" and "Beagle".
