# Paw Haven v2: Puppy Playdates & Sparkle (desktop only, 1280×720)

Read `PIPELINE.md` first: worktrees, lanes and tests. Also read `/home/claude/paw_haven_bible.md` (design bible), `V13b.md` (breeding rules) and `V2_GENES.md`. **Where this file disagrees with older docs, this file wins.**

## Client direction for v2
- **Dog spots:** "I only want the player to keep up to 4 dogs. It is locked by bonding level: the higher the bond, the more dogs you can keep. Keep the requirements harsh, but make it easy to keep two dogs."
- **Earlier defaults (accepted):**
  - Sparkle is guaranteed by the 24th puppy.
  - Puppies that don't fit go to loving homes in town and come back to visit.
  - A polish pass is included.
- **Standing rules:**
  - Realistic dog logic: always 4 legs, real habits, real breeding rules.
  - Every window is a centred popup.
  - Cozy: no fail states, and nobody ever dies or runs away.
  - No franchise names in game files.

## 1. Dog spots (max 4), unlocked by Bond (HOME lane)

Bond is per dog (`d.bond.level`, 1–10, thresholds `BOND_TH` in 00_core).

| Spot | Unlocks when ALL of these are true | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | From the start | |
| 2 | **Any one dog at Bond 3**, and a house that fits 2 | Easy: about day 2 |
| 3 | **Two dogs at Bond 8**, **21 care days**, and the **Treehouse Den** (or a bigger house) | Harsh: weeks |
| 4 | **Three dogs at Bond 10**, **45 care days**, and the **Royal Castle Kennel** | Very harsh: the long-term goal |

**Houses (`HOUSE_CAP`):**

| House | Fits |
|---|---|
| Cardboard Box | 1 |
| Classic Wooden Doghouse | 2 (changed, so 2 dogs is easy) |
| Cozy Cottage | 2 |
| Snow Igloo | 2 |
| Treehouse Den | 3 |
| Royal Castle Kennel | 4 |

The hard cap is always 4.

**Care days:**
- `S.careDays` counts the distinct real days on which the player fed, petted or played at least once. Mark it inside `dailyCare()` or the feed/pet/play paths, once per day, and store `S.careDayLast` (ISO date).
- Old saves backfill once: `careDays = max(0, ageMonths(S.dogs[0]) - 10)`, capped at 60.

**`dogSlots()`** returns `{ cap, used, free, spots: [{ n, open, reqs: [{ label, done, have, need }] }] }`.
- `cap` is the number of open spots (1–4).
- `used = S.dogs.length`.
- `free = max(0, cap - used)`.
- Also define `slotsFree()`, which returns `free`.

**UI:**
- **Shelter:** the popup header shows a doodle row of 4 paw-print spots: filled for dogs, open for free spots, and padlocked for locked ones. Each locked spot shows its checklist with ✓ and progress, for example "Bond 8 dogs: 1/2", "Care days: 12/21", "Treehouse Den: no".
- **Dog spots card:** the same card opens from the Journal Profile via a "Dog spots" button (SYSTEMS calls `openSpots()`) and from the Shelter.
- **Old saves** with more dogs than open spots keep every dog ("Everyone stays. New friends need a free spot."). They can't add dogs until `free > 0`.
- **Unlock toast:** a gold toast and fanfare the first time a new spot unlocks ("A 3rd dog spot is open! The Treehouse Den has room for one more."). Store `S.spotsSeen`.
- Replace the old "second dog at Bond 5" rule completely. The dev button "Pack" now gives Bond 3, the Classic Wooden Doghouse fitting 2, and a room.
- **Adoption, rescues, keeping puppies and "pick of the litter"** all use `slotsFree()`.
- **Feeding (03_yard and 13_more_dogs feed paths):** a food with `pupHappy` gives that much extra Happiness to dogs under 6 months. Pregnant dogs (`d.preg`) and nursing mums get "Eating for N!" lines.

## 2. Data model (save `v: 1` stays; every new field is optional, so readers must default)

### Dog fields

| Field | Values and meaning |
|---|---|
| `parents` | `null`, or `{ dam: id, sire: id }`. Ids point into `S.tree`. |
| `mix` | `null`, or `{ a: damBreedKey, b: sireBreedKey, head: breedKey, name: 'Horgi' }`. `d.key` is the BODY breed, which drives personality, voice, size and perks. `mix.head` is the breed whose head is drawn. |
| `sparkle` | `true` or `false`. Cosmetic only. |
| `gen` | 0 for starters and rescues, otherwise `max(parent gen) + 1`. |
| `geneTested` | `true` once the vet has run a gene test. The Profile then shows the genotype. |
| `fixed` | Spayed or neutered (already exists). Permanent. |
| `litters`, `lastLitter` | Already exist. |
| `preg` | `null`, or `{ sire, sireKey, sireName, since: ISO, due: ISO, pups: [pupSpec], scanned: false }`. Puppies are rolled at the playdate (deterministic), so the vet scan can count them. |
| `restUntil` | ISO date. A male rests after a playdate. |
| `lastPlaydate` | ISO date. |

### Save-level fields (`S.*`)

**`S.tree: { [id]: rec }`** is the family register: every dog ever involved, including dogs at home, puppies, rehomed dogs and NPC partners.
- `rec = { id, name, key, mix, sex, coat, eyes, sparkle, born, parents: {dam, sire} | null, status: 'home' | 'rehomed' | 'npc' | 'litter', family?, owner? }`
- BREED keeps it in sync: on `dog:added`, at birth, at rehoming, and at NPC playdates. On load it backfills missing records for dogs at home.

**`S.litters: [litter]`** holds litters currently in the nursery (each stays 2 days with mum).
- `litter = { id, mum: dogId, sire: treeId, born: ISO, until: ISO, pups: [pupSpec], named: bool }`
- `pupSpec = { id, name, key, sex, genes, mix, sparkle, born, coat, eyes, parents }`
- A pupSpec can be drawn with `dogSVG(pupSpec, o)` (it has `id`, `key`, `genes`, `born`, `mix` and `sparkle`).
- Nursery pups do NOT use dog spots. They need a spot only when they stay.

**`S.rehomed: [{ id, name, key, mix, sex, coat, eyes, sparkle, born, genes, parents, family, since: ISO, lastVisit }]`** lists puppies living with town families.

**Other save fields:**

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `S.npcLitters: [{ id, npc: treeId, sire: dogId, due: ISO, ready: ISO, pups: [pupSpec], offered: bool }]` | Litters fathered by your male with an NPC female. |
| `S.coatBook: { 'breedKey\|Coat Name': ISO }` | Every coat you have ever had: home dogs, born puppies, rehomed puppies, rescues and starters. Use the body `key` for mixes. |
| `S.sparkleBook: { breedKey: ISO }` | Breeds you have had a Sparkle puppy of. |
| `S.pupsBorn` | Total puppies born. |
| `S.pupsSinceSparkle` | Pity counter. |
| `S.mail: [{ id, kind: 'postcard' \| 'gift' \| 'litter' \| 'news', from, title, text, date: ISO, read: false, dog?: pupSpec, gift?: { coins? item? } }]` | Mailbox messages. |
| `S.careDays`, `S.careDayLast`, `S.spotsSeen` | Dog spots (section 1). |

**Helpers that already exist** (00_core and 10_sex_age_genes): `ageMonths(d)`, `lifeStage(m)` ('puppy' < 6, 'young' < 12, 'adult' < 84, 'senior'), `seasonStatus(d)`, `BREEDING.RULES`, `localISO()`, `bornDaysAgo(n)`, `todayKey()`, `withDog(d, fn)`, `PRd(d)`, `dogById(id)`, `coatInfo(d)`, `addDog(spec, name)`, `dogSVG(d, o)`, `headSVG(d)`, `openModal(title, html, opts)`, `closeModal()`, `toast(msg, kind)`, `nope(msg)`, `markDirty()`, `saveNow()`, `addBond`, `addCoins`, `SFX.*`, `audioCue`, `barkDog(d, kind, opts)`.

## 3. Breeding: Puppy Playdates (BREED lane, realistic, 1 real day = 1 dog month)

**Rules (`BREEDING.RULES`; set `enabled: true`):**
- **Pair:** one male and one female. Same-sex playdates are just fun play ("Best friends! Puppies need a boy and a girl.").
- **Both dogs:**
  - adult (12 months or older)
  - not fixed
  - meters ≥ 50
  - **Bond 6 or higher** (welfare: a well-bonded, healthy dog)
  - not related (`PawGenes.related` and `S.tree` up to first cousins)
  - not merle × merle
- **Female:**
  - in season (`seasonStatus`)
  - not expecting and not nursing
  - under 72 months
  - fewer than 4 litters
  - skips one season after a litter (`lastLitter` + 6 days)
- **Male:**
  - not resting (`restUntil`; rests 2 days)
  - under 96 months
- **Playdate:**
  - A cute popup scene: both dogs play-bow, chase (CSS), then lie down together.
  - Result: puppies on the way with **85%** chance. Otherwise "No puppies this time. They had a lovely day." The pair may try again tomorrow while she's still in season.
  - On success, roll the litter now and store it in `dam.preg`.
- **Litter size:** from the DAM's breed size (`BREEDING.RULES.litters`).
- **Each puppy:**
  - sex 50/50
  - `genes = PawGenes.inherit(dam.genes, sire.genes, rng)`
  - breed: same breed gives a purebred. Different breeds give `mix = PawGenes.mixOf(damKey, sireKey, rng)` → `{ a, b, head, name }`; body `key` = `mix.body`. Mutt with anything gives `key: 'mutt'` and `mix.name` "Mutt mix".
- **Sparkle:**
  - Odds are 1 in 512, ×4 if either parent wears the Sparkle Stone charm at the playdate.
  - A forced Sparkle when `S.pupsSinceSparkle` reaches 23, so the 24th puppy is guaranteed.
  - Roll it at the playdate and reveal it at birth.
- **Pregnancy:** 2 days (`preg.due = since + 2`).
  - The Profile shows "Expecting! Due in N days".
  - Hunger decay is ×1.2.
  - No playdates, and walks are capped at Sunny Park (PLAY enforces this).
  - The art uses `expecting`.
  - A vet check-up shows an "ultrasound" with the puppy count (TOWN sets `preg.scanned`).
- **Birth:**
  - `breedTick()` runs on `game:ready`, `clock:minute` and `yard:enter`. When `today >= preg.due`, a **"Puppies!" popup** opens, the next time the player is in the yard or house.
  - It shows mum with the newborns, each pup's sex, coat and eyes, and a Sparkle reveal with a sparkle sting.
  - The player names each pup. Defaults come from a fun name list, and `Enter` confirms.
  - Pups go into `S.litters` with `until = born + 2 days`. Update `dam.litters++`, `dam.lastLitter`, `S.pupsBorn`, `S.pupsSinceSparkle`, `S.coatBook`, `S.sparkleBook` and `S.tree`.
  - Clear `dam.preg`.
- **Nursery (2 days):**
  - A **nursery basket** (`PawArt.prop('nursery')`) sits in the house and yard scene, with mum lying `down` and the newborn pups curled against her.
  - Click it to open the Nursery popup:
    - the pups (`dogSVG(pup, {pose:'sleep'})`)
    - "Pet softly" (+Happiness for mum, a hearts animation, max 3 per day)
    - a countdown "Ready for new homes in N days"
  - Mum's hunger decay is ×1.3 while nursing. She stays home: no walks (PLAY enforces this).
  - Nursing puppies need no feeding; they nurse.
- **Choosing day** (when `today >= until`): a **"Who stays?" popup** opens.
  - Each pup card shows Stay or Loving home. The player may keep up to `slotsFree()` pups; it shows "Free spots: N".
  - Kept pups use `addDog({ key, sex, genes, born, id }, name)`, then set `parents`, `mix`, `sparkle`, `gen` and `coat`. Their Bond starts at 1.
  - Each other pup gets a town family from `FAMILIES` (about 12 warm names, e.g. "the Tanakas by the bakery", "Old Mr. Reyes at the pier", "the Okafor twins"). It goes into `S.rehomed` and `S.tree` with status 'rehomed', and a goodbye line: "Mochi Jr. trots off with the Tanakas. They promise postcards."
  - Rehomed pups send a postcard within 1–2 days (`mailPush`), and they visit around town (TOWN).
- **NPC playdates (TOWN builds the board; BREED runs the playdate via `openPlaydates({ npc })`):**
  - **Your female × an NPC male:** the litter is yours, exactly as above.
  - **Your male × an NPC female:** her owner keeps the litter. After 4 days (2 pregnancy + 2 nursery), a `kind: 'litter'` letter arrives: "Duke's puppies are here! Daisy's family says thank you." If `slotsFree() > 0`, it offers **"pick of the litter"**: adopt 1 pup, free. Store these in `S.npcLitters`. Their coats count in `S.coatBook`, because they are your dog's puppies.
- **Spay/neuter** (TOWN does the vet UI): free, permanent, with a gentle confirm. `fixed: true`.
- **API (BREED defines):**
  - `canPair(a, b)` returns `{ ok, why }`, where `why` is a friendly reason string.
  - `openPlaydates(opts?)`: a popup to pick the pair from your dogs, plus an optional `{ npc }` partner. It shows each dog's readiness with ✓ or ✗ and a short reason.
    - If both dogs are gene-tested, it shows a **Puppy Predictor**: coat odds from `PawGenes.predict`.
  - `breedTick()`, `openNursery(litterId)`, `openWhoStays(litterId)`, `treePut(rec)`, `coatLog(rec)`.
  - Expose for tests: `window.__paw.breed = { canPair, playdate(damId, sireId, {force}), tickDays(n), birthNow(), chooseNow(), get litters() }`.
    - `tickDays(n)` moves the relevant ISO dates back by n days, so tests never wait.
- **Entry points:**
  - HOME adds a **"Playdates"** card in the Play tray, which calls `openPlaydates()` with a typeof guard.
  - TOWN calls `openPlaydates({ npc })` from the Dog Park board.

## 4. Named mixes and genes (GENES lane, `mods/genes.js` = `window.PawGenes`, additive and pure)
- **`MIXES`:** a table of all 45 pairs of the 10 breeds → `{ name, head }`.
  - Use real fun portmanteaus where they exist (Puggle = beagle × pug, Chiweenie = chihuahua × dachs, Goberian = golden × husky, Horgi = corgi × husky, Chug = chihuahua × pug, Dorgi = corgi × dachs, Shiba-Corgi "Shorgi", Beago, Greagle …) and invent cute ones otherwise.
  - `head` is the breed with the more striking head, chosen per pair, for example a corgi body with a husky head.
  - **Mutt × anything:** "Mutt mix", key `mutt`, `head` = the other breed 50% of the time.
- **`mixOf(damKey, sireKey, rng)`** returns `{ a, b, body, head, name }`.
  - `body` is the dam's or sire's breed, 50/50 by rng.
  - `head` is the table's head breed, or the other parent's breed when the body already is the head breed (so a mix always shows both parents).
- **`predict(genesA, genesB, keyA, keyB)`** returns `[{ coat, eyes, pct }]` sorted by pct. Enumerate every allele combination exactly (4096) and merge by coat name. It must respect the double-merle block (M/M outcomes are re-rolled as non-viable and removed from the odds).
- **`coatCatalog(breedKey)`** returns `[{ coat, rare: bool, how: 'one short plain-words hint' }]`: every coat name `phenotype` can produce for that breed, from the breed's allowed loci (pug: no merle or piebald, and so on). About 4–12 per breed. `rare` covers lilac, cream, merle, double-dilute and similar.
- **`describe(genes)`** returns `{ lines: ['B/b: black coat, carries liver', ...], carriers: ['liver', 'dilute'] }`: plain words for the vet gene-test card.
- **Mix phenotype:** `phenotype(genes, key, seed)` keeps working with the body key. Nothing else changes.
- **Node test** `game/test_v2_genes_node.js`:
  - all 45 mixes resolve
  - `predict` sums to 100%
  - Mochi × Pepper gives about 1/32 lilac where expected, and Biscuit × Frost gives a 1/4 piebald (the V2_GENES teaching examples)
  - `coatCatalog` matches `phenotype` across 20k random genotypes per breed
