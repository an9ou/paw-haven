# V213: v2.13 "Faces & Feelings" (face expressions for all 35 breeds, heads stuck to bodies)

Base: `v213-base` (a branch at `b570005`, v2.12 `main`). Work branch: `integration/v2.13`.

## 1. What the owner asked for (verbatim)

- "Also, u can add more expressions for all dogs, just be creative." All 35 breeds, adults and puppies.
- (v2.12) "Make sure the head is stick to the body". The v2.12 Coordinator found old breeds with the same floating head in a few poses: the collie's howl, the shiba, chihuahua and beagle in happy, speak and howl. Fixed in this release, with a new `check_identical` baseline.

## 2. Rules

- **Layered on, never replacing.** An expression is an overlay on the existing poses. With no expression, every render is byte-identical to `v213-base`, except the neck fixes of section 5 (the only allowed change to old renders, listed there breed by breed and pose by pose).
- Nothing bad ever happens: no pain, sickness or fear faces. Expressions are short and kind. A sneeze is a happy flower-and-bubbles sneeze.
- The deliberately crude crayon style: thick ink, wobbly lines, the same marks the poses already use (`h.line`, `h.shape`, `h.dot`, `h.heart`, `h.text`).
- Outfits, hats, glasses, the Ghost Sheet, the costumes with their own head piece (`TOP2`) and the Heart Sunglasses must look right with every expression on.
- Phone performance holds: only the dog that changes face is drawn again, at most one roam expression starts per second in a scene.

## 3. The art API (Coordinator stub, `dogs/pawart_dogs.js`)

- `PawArt.dog(key, { pose, expr, ... })` and `PawArt.dogHead(key, { expr, ... })`. `expr` is one of `PawArt.EXPR_LIST`. No `expr`, an unknown one, one whose `ok` regex does not match the pose, or a newborn = the old render byte for byte. With an expression the svg gets the class `pa-ex-<name>`.
- `PawArt.exprOk(name, pose)`: true when the expression shows on that pose (the game uses it to pick).
- `EXPR[name]` entries (the block comment above `frame()` has the full signature): `ok` (pose regex), `pose(s, L, k)` (layout change before drawing: a small head tilt, chin up), `pre(s, LX, k)` (face-pass settings), `eye(...)`, `mouth(...)` (draw an eye or the mouth itself, return true), `face(h, s, LX, HT)` (extras on the face), `fx(h, s, L, B, HT)` (marks round the dog on the top layer; `dogHead` skips `fx`).
- The face is drawn again on its own kit (`exSub`), so every other layer keeps its crayon wobble whether the expression is on or off: the dog does not shimmer when a face starts. `pose()` changes do move the head: keep tilts within about 10 degrees and check the neck (section 5).
- The breed's own `faceFn` (chow lids, corso rings, stdpoodle, pitbull grin) runs in the face pass too: an expression that redraws the eyes must still read on those heads.

## 4. The expression system (EX lane, `game/src/41_expressions.js` + `game/css/43_expressions.css`, tag `ex`, test hook `__paw.ex`)

- **No save fields.** Expressions are moments. Photo mode remembers its picker choice in `prefs.phFace`.
- `exOn()`: on in play. Under the harness it needs `prefs.exTest` (old suites see no expressions).
- `exShow(d, name, ms)`: dog `d` shows `name` for `ms` (default per expression, about 2.2 to 3.5 s), then its normal face. One at a time per dog, a short cooldown per dog. Only that dog's art is drawn again.
- `exNow(d, pose)`: the dog's current expression if `PawArt.exprOk(name, pose)`, else null. The surfaces that show expressions pass `expr: exNow(d, pose)` to `dogSVG`; every other caller is unchanged (no stale faces in modals drawn once).
- `exTrig(d, event)`: picks an expression for a play event with the weights below times the dog's personality, then `exShow`. Events: `pet`, `feed`, `treat`, `bath`, `brush`, `ballCatch`, `ballEnd`, `trick`, `dig`, `newPlace`, `roam`, `shelterMeet`, `cardPaw`.
- **Personality.** Weights come from `BREED_PERS[key].trait` (36_personality.js) and a per-breed table `BREED_FACES` in 41_expressions.js (35 rows, each the breed's 2 to 4 favourite faces). The owner's examples must show: a dramatic Shiba (pout, surprised, guilty more often), a sleepy English Mastiff (sleepy, dreamy), a show-off Japanese Spitz (proud, wink, wow). The roam rate follows `energy` and `attention`.
- **Where:**
  - roaming (37_roam.js): the occasional face while a dog stands or sniffs (curious, sleepy, blep, sneeze, zoomies), and every petted dog (`crPetAt`).
  - care: petting, feeding and treats, the bath, brushing.
  - ball play (38_ball.js): a catch, the end of play (a short pout, then fine).
  - tricks: a good trick. Digging: guilty.
  - a new place: the first arrival each Japan day at a Tokyo place or the Mt. Fuji Lookout (wow).
  - photo mode (35_photo.js): a face picker ("Natural" and the fourteen player words) for every dog in the picture. 44 px taps, it scrolls sideways on phones.
  - the Shelter Playroom: a dog you meet.
  - Dog Cards: a visitor you give a paw to shows hearts or wink, on this device only (no new card field, the Worker is unchanged).
  - The site is unchanged in v2.13.
- Edits outside the lane's own files are small hooks in the surfaces above (`13_more_dogs.js` only if needed, `37_roam.js`, `38_ball.js`, `35_photo.js`, `30_shelter.js`, `40_dogcards.js`, the care and trick files). Use `trackAct` / the bus where an event already exists.
