# Paw Haven v1.3 addendum: male and female dogs, plus breeding preparation (realistic logic)

Read with V13.md. The client asked: "Build 1.3, add the garden and kitchen, prepare for the breeding system, please add the male and female, make it follow the reality logic."

v1.3 adds **sex, age and hidden genes** to every dog, plus a **Dog Profile** page. Breeding itself (Puppy Playdates) ships in v2. v1.3 only prepares the data, rules and UI hooks, so v2 can switch it on without migrating saves again.

## Sex
- Every dog has `sex: 'male' | 'female'`.
- **Adoption:** each shelter dog card has a **Boy ♂ / Girl ♀** choice. Copy: "Mochi has a brother and a sister here. Which one is coming home?" The default name stays. The choice is final, and the naming step shows it.
- **Existing saves:** on first load, a one-time doodle card asks "Quick question for the paperwork: is <name> a boy or a girl?" with ♂ / ♀ buttons.
- **Pronouns:** the game copy uses he/him/his or she/her/her from `sex`, everywhere: toasts, bubbles, jokes, results, the Journal and walk lines. Never "it". Use a single helper, for example `P.he`, `P.him`, `P.his`, `P.He`. The toys and walk modules only ever use the dog's name; check them and adapt them if they contain pronouns.
- **UI:** a small pencil ♂ (blue `#5B8FD6`) or ♀ (pink `#E07AA0`) next to the dog's name in the HUD, the Profile and the Wardrobe. The dog art is unchanged: male and female dogs of a breed look alike, which is realistic.

## Age and life stage (time-scaled, real-world proportions)
- **Scale:** 1 real day = 1 dog month. The clock already runs on real device time.
- **`born`:** an ISO date. Adopted shelter dogs start at **10 dog months** (a young dog), so `born` = adoption date minus 10 days. Old saves: set `born` = first-save date minus 10 days, or today minus 10 days if the first-save date is unknown.
- **Life stages:**

  | Stage | Age |
  |---|---|
  | `puppy` | 0–5 months |
  | `young` | 6–11 months |
  | `adult` | 12 months – 7 years |
  | `senior` | 7+ years (84 real days) |

  These match real dog development closely enough. Senior dogs get a cosy "grey muzzle" line in the Profile, with no gameplay penalty.
- The Profile shows the age as "1 year 2 months" and the birthday. On the birthday (monthly in-game "dog-month-days" are too frequent, so use the yearly birthday every 12 real days), the dog gets a Pupcake joke and +10 Happiness.

## Reproductive realism (rules are stored now; breeding is switched on in v2)
Stored as data in `PawBreeding.RULES` (a small constant object in the game, or a tiny module). The Profile can already show status.

**Maturity and season**
- Breeding needs a pair of **one male and one female**. Same-sex pairs can be friends and do playdates, but never produce puppies. The game explains this gently.
- **Maturity:** both dogs must be adults, aged **≥ 12 dog months**. Breeding is not allowed for seniors aged **≥ 8 dog years**.
- **Heat cycle (females):**
  - A season comes about every **6 dog months**, so every 6 real days, starting at 12 months.
  - It lasts **3 real days**.
  - Breeding is only possible during the season.
  - The Profile shows "In season" or "Next season in N days" (seeded per dog, so it is stable).
- **Males** have no cycle. After a playdate they rest **2 real days**.

**Pregnancy and litters**
- **Pregnancy:** real dogs carry about 63 days; scaled, that is **2 real days** ("Expecting!"). The mum needs extra food: +20% hunger decay, and a vet check-up gives a bonus. No playdates while expecting. No heavy walks either: walks are capped at Sunny Park.
- **Litter size by breed size:**

  | Breed size | Breeds | Puppies | Weights |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | small | dachshund, corgi | 1–3 | 40 / 40 / 20 |
  | medium | shiba, mutt | 1–3 | 30 / 50 / 20 |
  | large | golden, husky | 2–3 | 60 / 40 |

  Real litters are larger. They are capped for the cozy game, and the doc notes this as simplified.
- **Puppies:**
  - Each puppy's sex is 50/50.
  - Each puppy inherits one allele per gene from each parent (Mendelian, as in the v2 doc).
  - Puppies stay with mum until **2 dog months** (2 real days) before they can go to new homes. Real puppies go at 8 weeks, which is the same idea.
- **Female recovery and limits:**
  - At least **one full skipped season** before the next litter.
  - Maximum **4 litters per lifetime**.
  - No litters after **6 dog years**.

**Welfare blocks** (the v2 rules, now stored)
- No merle × merle pairings.
- No close relatives: parent, child, full or half sibling, grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, first cousin. To make this checkable, every dog stores `parents: [idA, idB] | null`.
- All care meters must be ≥ 50 and Bond ≥ 6.

**Spay and neuter**
- Available at the vet in v2 as a responsible-owner choice. It sets `fixed: true`, and the dog then never breeds.
- Store `fixed: false` now.

## Data model (additive, migrated in `migrate()`)

```
S.dog += {
  id: 'd_<random>',
  sex: 'male' | 'female',
  born: 'YYYY-MM-DD',
  genes: { B: ['B','b'], D: ['D','D'], E: ['E','e'], S: ['S','sp'], M: ['m','m'], Bl: ['n','n'] },  // hidden; starter defaults from the v2 doc (see below)
  coat: '<plain-language coat name>', eyes: 'brown' | 'amber' | 'blue',
  parents: null,                      // shelter dogs: unknown
  litters: 0,
  lastLitter: null,
  fixed: false,
  seasonSeed: <int>                   // females: offsets the 6-day cycle
}
S.kennel = []                         // future extra dogs (v1.5/v2), same shape; empty in v1.3
```

## Dog Profile page (new Journal tab: "Profile")
- Dog head portrait, name, the ♂/♀ symbol, breed, coat and eye description, age and life stage, birthday, and personality.
- Family tree box: "Parents: unknown (shelter rescue)", with an empty tree doodle.
- Season status (females, adults only): "In season" or "Next season in N days". Males: "Ready for playdates in v2".
- Genes box: "Hidden: a Gene test will be available at the vet in v2", with the 6 gene slots shown as "?".
- Teaser card: "Puppy Playdates are coming in v2".
- Counters: litters (0 of 4 for females), and fixed: no.
