# V2.17.1 "Festive Market": contract and as-built notes

Goal (owner, 26 Oct 2026, urgent for Halloween week): a pop-up market place on the town map that is there only during a festival window, selling
that festival's goods in four kinds of stalls. Built as a general system: one config table of markets. Owner rule (same day): festive items are
sold only at the festival pop-ups, never in the normal shops. Bought things stay forever; missing a market never takes anything away.

Code: `game/src/49_market.js` + `game/css/50_market.css` (tag `fm`, test hook `window.__paw.fm`, `fmOn()`). Small hooks in `00_core.js`
(`sceneArt('fmarket')`, `mpPlaceName('fmarket')`, `v2171Fields`), `42_map215.js` (the tent in the map svg, the travel walk through the Square),
`03_yard.js` (the Pumpkin Lantern row, the Squeaky Ghost's ball tray line), `12_popups...js` (the Witch-Hat Bed row), `05_market_shops.js` and
`48_dogdays.js` (beds with `fest` or `fm` stay out of Barkitecture and the Bond track), `27_halloween.js` (the Square pop-up keeps its own 19 items),
`24_missions.js` (market buys never count as "Market Street shopping"; the Harvest and Winter treat missions also accept a market treat).
Art: world E (`PawArt.prop('fmscene' | 'fmtent' | 'fmlantern', { theme, lit })`, the Squeaky Ghost and Pumpkin Lantern icons), world B (the Witch-Hat Bed
with its front lip). Under the test harness the market needs `prefs.fmTest` and the v2.15 map; `prefs.fmDate` pins the Japan date. Suite: `v2171_market`.

## 1. The config (`FM_MARKETS`)
Each market: `id`, `theme` (scene, tent and sign colours), `name`, `win(year)` -> `['MM-DD', 'MM-DD']` (Japan date, inclusive; a window may cross
New Year), `items` by stall (`decor`, `clothes`, `homes`, `toys`, `treats`; names only: the price, the Bond gate and the kind come from `HM_DECOR`,
`CLOTHES`, `HOUSES`, `BEDS`, `TOYS`, `FOOD`), `todo` (configured, never opens). Edition items (`ed`) are sold only in their own year.
Adding the art agent's 25 Halloween items (branch `art/halloween-market`) is adding their names to the lists once their table rows exist.

When markets overlap (Halloween and Harvest in October) one place holds them both; the one that closes first names the place and the sign.

## 2. The place
- `PLACES.fmarket` exists only while a market is open (`fmSync`, every 5 s and on each scene). A dog standing there when it closes walks to the
  Square with a kind toast; a save reloaded there after it closed starts in the yard (migrate's rule for an unknown place).
- The v2.15 map gets a striped tent at map (1010, 720), beside the Town Square, with the market's name as its caption and an orange "Festival!" tag.
  It is a `.mb` building like the others (button, focus, the travel walk through the Square).
- The scene: three stalls with striped awnings, bunting and lanterns, the sign over the middle stall with the market's name and closing date.
  The stalls are buttons (decor, clothes, toys); "Browse the stalls" opens the sheet.

## 3. The stall sheet
The shop sheet (`.panel.shop`), a tab per stall that has items, a grid that scrolls inside the sheet (up to 12 items a stall), the item's own
price and Bond gate (a locked item says "opens at Bond N"), the purchase window, coins only, "Owned" once bought, "Wear it?" for clothes.

## 4. New items (crude crayon, every fill on its own outline, all dog-safe)
- Pumpkin Lantern (yard decoration, 80, glows after dark), Witch-Hat Bed (bed, 420, Bond 2), Squeaky Ghost (toy, 50, a ball-play toy: 2 coins a return
  inside the ball-play cap). They carry `fest: 'halloween'`, `ed: 2026`, `fm: true`.
- The asked-for costume, bunting and pumpkin house already exist and are on the Halloween stalls: the Pumpkin Patch Suit and the Little Bat Costume,
  the Paper Bat Bunting, the Pumpkin Cottage. No new dog art, so every dog render is unchanged.

now does too. The festival pop-ups that sold them keep selling them (the Square's Harvest Stall, Pumpkin Patch Pop-up and Winter Stall, inside the same
windows), and every festive item is also listed in its market. `v2171_market` checks the three shops list none of them.

## 6. Missions, guide, site
No mission asked for a festive item from a normal shop. "Buy something on Market Street" never counts a market buy (it never counted the pop-ups);
the Harvest Stall and Winter Stall treat missions also accept a market treat, so neither can become impossible. Gerald's lessons and the site
/guide do not name a shop for festive items (the site says "festivals in autumn, winter and the New Year"), so nothing there changed.

## 7. Save (additive, S.v stays 1)
`S.fm = { seen: { marketId_year: ISO }, letters: { marketId_year: ISO }, bought }` (`v2171Fields`). The welcome toast (first visit) and Gerald's
letter (first visit of the day to the yard while a market is open) come once per market per year.
