# v2.23 "Coat colours" (contract; released 7 Nov 2026)

Owner, 7 Nov 2026: "I want that when the users choose their first dog in the game, they can already choose different colour for the dogs.
Different breeds should have different choices of colour, just like how they can be in reality. For example, lab can be white, black and red,
while border collie can be black & white, or 3 mixed colours with different pupil colours."

Suite: `v223_coats`. Save stays `v: 1`; nothing to migrate (a new dog just starts with other genes). Screenshots for the owner first; no release yet.

## 1. What the player sees

- The adoption card (`renderAdopt`, 02_adoption.js) has a colour row under Boy / Girl: one small sitting crayon dog per colour (the breed's
  own art in that coat, cropped to the dog: `cpSit` / `cpCrop`, measured once per breed; a head hid the body colours, review fix 5), the coat name ("Coat: Blue merle"), and, where real dogs have them, eye choices drawn as two eyes (brown or amber, blue, odd)
  with "Eyes: odd". A breed with one colour here (Japanese Spitz, Westie, Maltese, Bernese, Samoyed, Bichon) shows its single coat and no buttons.
- Picking a swatch redraws the meet dog in the playroom (a happy wiggle, `srMeetCoat`) and the picked head in the heads row.
- The pick resets to option 1 whenever the dog changes (the < > buttons and the arrow keys, heads, the playroom board, `srSelectKey`), and so
  does Boy / Girl (`adReset`). Random dog also
  rolls a colour and eyes. Nothing is needed to adopt: option 1 is today's starter.
- **Later starters too**: the Shelter list's starter cards (`shelterCard` kind `starter`, 13_more_dogs.js; a different card from the first
  adoption) get the same row (`shelterCoat[key]`, `shPick`), and `srAdoptFlow` passes the pick to `addDog`. The row says the coat (and eyes), so the
  card's own "coat, eyes" line is only on rescue and resident cards. A Boy / Girl or colour tap
  there now keeps the sheet's scroll position (`shRefresh`).
- Desktop: 40 px swatches inside the card's text area (it scrolls when a long text needs it; the heads row gives way first, as in v2.12).
  Phone: 44 px taps, the row wraps inside the card, the Adopt bar stays at the bottom of the scrolling dock.

## 2. Genetics (10_sex_age_genes.js)

- `STARTER_COATS[key]`: the extra options as `[coat name, the loci that differ from STARTER_GENES]`, tokens such as `Ee`, `bb`, `spsp`, `Mm`.
  Option 1 is always `STARTER_GENES[key]` and `starterPick(key, 0, 0)` returns `null`, so `newDogFields` builds the dog exactly as before
  (`dogs/check_identical.js` and the older suites are untouched).
- Each option is a full genotype, so puppies inherit real genes. Every allele is one the breed's `FREQ` (mods/genes.js) allows; merle is one
  copy (`M/m`) only, so `isDoubleMerle` keeps working at playdates. No paid or sparkle colours: all free.
- Eyes (`STARTER_EYES`): `all` for the husky, the Aussie and the Border Collie (the blue-eye gene `Bl`: husky blue `Bl/Bl` is its starter,
  blue or odd from `Bl/bl` otherwise; a merle coat keeps merle's own rolls with `bl/bl`). The husky's and the Aussie's FREQ has `Bl`; the
  collie's stays `Bl: 0`, so only a starter picked with blue or odd eyes carries it (owner: "border collie can be black & white, or 3 mixed
  colours with different pupil colours"; non-merle blue eyes are real in collies), and random and rescue collies are as before. `merle` for
  the dachshund, Great Dane and mutt (only on a merle coat). Other dogs: brown, or amber on a liver (`b/b`) coat, as `phenotype()` always did.
  No option is a `b/b` stand-in that would give a breed amber eyes it does not have (review fix 2: Pug and Mastiff apricot, Samoyed, Frenchie
  and Bichon cream, Pom white, Great Dane, Cane Corso and Pit Bull brindle, Cane Corso red are gone; `mods/genes.js` gives amber to any `b/b`).
- `phenotype()` rolls the Bl/bl and merle eyes from the dog's id, so `starterIdFor` picks a dog id whose roll gives the chosen eyes; that id
  is the meet dog's and becomes the adopted dog's (`newDogFields(key, sex, born, pick)`, `freshState(key, name, sex, pick)`).
  Yard, photos, walks and litters read the coat from the genes (`coatInfo`), so all of them show it.
- **Dog Cards and Friends** (review fix 1): a card dog is rebuilt with its own id (`dcv:<card>:<i>`), which would roll other eyes and place the
  merle smudges, white patches and spots elsewhere. So a wire dog (`dcDogOf` in 40_dogcards.js, `frCardOf` in 50_friends.js, the Worker's
  `dcDog`) may carry two optional fields: `e`, the eyes as an index into `DC_EYES` (brown, amber, blue, odd; sent only when the id roll
  decides them: `Bl/bl` or a merle that shows), and `p`, the art seed (`hashId` of the dog's id, a 32-bit number, never the id). `dcDog`
  picks the first of `dcv:<card>:<i>`, `dcv:<card>:<i>:1` ... that rolls `e` (`starterIdFor` with a fixed id list, cached; `d.dc` keeps
  the card and index for the expressions redraw) and sets `d.artSeed = p` (`lookOpts` uses it). A card, yard or walk dog without them
  decodes exactly as before. The Worker accepts and stores them only when sent (it refused any extra key before, so it was deployed first).
- Test hook `__paw.cp` (table, pick helpers, the card's state, a dog's stored and drawn coat).

## 6. Tan points and the deep red (owner, 8 Nov 2026; branch `claude/tan-points`)

Four options were added after the release, all starter-only (no FREQ changes, so random and rescue dogs are as before), each a real genotype that puppies inherit:

- **Tan points** (Border Collie Tricolour, Corgi Black-headed tricolour): the A locus the Aussie already has (`TAN`, `at` over `a`) now also counts for the
  collie and the corgi (`TAN_ART` in mods/genes.js). Their presets carry `at/at` (`'atat'`, the corgi with `Ee`). `phenotype()` adds `coat.tan` (a crayon tan,
  lighter on the paler pigments) only when the genes carry `at_` on a dark coat (red and cream hide it, as e/e does), so every collie and corgi without the
  locus, and every other breed, draws byte for byte as before (`node dogs/check_identical.js 60cc2907`: 0 differences). The art (`tanCollie`, `tanCorgi`,
  `tanBrows` in dogs/pawart_dogs.js): the collie gets tan eyebrow dots, tan cheeks and muzzle sides, a tan edge behind the white chest, tan under the tail and
  tan lower legs above white feet; the corgi a black saddle and head with tan cheeks, eyebrow dots, a tan band round the saddle, tan thighs and tan legs with
  white feet. The tan goes down before the white, so it shows between the black and the white. Check: `TAN=1 node dogs/fill_sweep.js`
  compares every tan render with the same coat without (dry and wet, every pose and age, mixes). Names: the collie's `TRI_SOLID` words (Tricolour,
  Chocolate tricolour ...), the corgi's "Black-headed tricolour" (`mod.genes.coat_black_headed_tri`); a merle or piebald keeps its name. The collie keeps
  its brown / blue / odd eyes on the Tricolour. A missing A pair still counts as `at/at` in `inherit` (as for the Aussie), so a tricolour's puppies with a
  black and white collie are tricolour too.
- **Red intensity** (Lab Fox red, Golden Dark golden): a new optional locus `I` (`RED_I`: `I` deep red over `i`, incompletely dominant; a missing pair is
  `i/i`). Only `INTENSE` breeds (lab, golden) on a red (e/e, not dilute) coat read it: I/I the deep shade (`SHADES.<breed>.fox`), I/i the shade in between
  (`fox1`), both named Fox red / Dark golden (`WORDS.<breed>.fox`). `inherit` and `predict` carry it after A (no older draw moves): two fox reds give fox red
  pups, a fox red and a yellow give the in-between shade. The vet's gene card gets one line for it (`mod.genes.line_i_*`), only for genes that carry it.
- **Dog Cards, friend yards and walks** carry both: `A` as since v2.21, `I` as an optional coat locus (`DC_RED`, 40_dogcards.js `dcCoat`; the Worker's
  `DC_COAT`, test_worker.js). A card or yard without them is the same bytes as before; the Worker is deployed before the game (a Worker from before refuses `I`).
- The family book's coat-name fallback (`coatGeneMap`, 07_treasure_journal.js) also tries `A at/at` and `I/I` after every older genotype, so it only gains names.
- Text: 6 new keys (two coat words, the corgi name, three gene lines) in all 12 languages; `tools/i18n_fonts.js zh-Hans --for game` (the other subsets
  already had every character) and `tools/i18n_pack.js`.
