# v2.23 "Coat pick" (contract, not released)

Owner, 7 Nov 2026: "I want that when the users choose their first dog in the game, they can already choose different colour for the dogs.
Different breeds should have different choices of colour, just like how they can be in reality. For example, lab can be white, black and red,
while border collie can be black & white, or 3 mixed colours with different pupil colours."

Suite: `v223_coats`. Save stays `v: 1`; nothing to migrate (a new dog just starts with other genes). Screenshots for the owner first; no release yet.

## 1. What the player sees

- The adoption card (`renderAdopt`, 02_adoption.js) has a colour row under Boy / Girl: one crayon dog head per colour (the breed's own art
  in that coat), the coat name ("Coat: Blue merle"), and, where real dogs have them, eye choices drawn as two eyes (brown or amber, blue, odd)
  with "Eyes: odd". A breed with one real colour (Japanese Spitz, Westie, Maltese, Bernese) shows its single coat and no buttons.
- Picking a swatch redraws the meet dog in the playroom (a happy wiggle, `srMeetCoat`) and the picked head in the heads row.
- The pick resets to option 1 whenever the dog changes (arrows, heads, the playroom board, `srSelectKey`), like Boy / Girl. Random dog also
  rolls a colour and eyes. Nothing is needed to adopt: option 1 is today's starter.
- **Later starters too**: the Shelter list's starter cards (`shelterCard` kind `starter`, 13_more_dogs.js; a different card from the first
  adoption) get the same row (`shelterCoat[key]`, `shPick`), and `srAdoptFlow` passes the pick to `addDog`. A Boy / Girl or colour tap
  there now keeps the sheet's scroll position (`shRefresh`).
- Desktop: 40 px swatches inside the card's text area (it scrolls when a long text needs it; the heads row gives way first, as in v2.12).
  Phone: 44 px taps, the row wraps inside the card, the Adopt bar stays at the bottom of the scrolling dock.

## 2. Genetics (10_sex_age_genes.js)

- `STARTER_COATS[key]`: the extra options as `[coat name, the loci that differ from STARTER_GENES]`, tokens such as `Ee`, `bb`, `spsp`, `Mm`.
  Option 1 is always `STARTER_GENES[key]` and `starterPick(key, 0, 0)` returns `null`, so `newDogFields` builds the dog exactly as before
  (`dogs/check_identical.js` and the older suites are untouched).
- Each option is a full genotype, so puppies inherit real genes. Every allele is one the breed's `FREQ` (mods/genes.js) allows; merle is one
  copy (`M/m`) only, so `isDoubleMerle` keeps working at playdates. No paid or sparkle colours: all free.
- Eyes (`STARTER_EYES`): `all` for the husky and the Aussie (the blue-eye gene `Bl`, which their FREQ has: husky blue `Bl/Bl` is its
  starter, blue or odd from `Bl/bl` otherwise); `merle` for the collie, dachshund, Great Dane and mutt (only on a merle coat: merle itself
  gives the blue or odd eyes, with `bl/bl`; a collie never carries `Bl`). Other dogs: brown, or amber on a liver (`b/b`) coat, as
  `phenotype()` always did.
- `phenotype()` rolls the Bl/bl and merle eyes from the dog's id, so `starterIdFor` picks a dog id whose roll gives the chosen eyes; that id
  is the meet dog's and becomes the adopted dog's (`newDogFields(key, sex, born, pick)`, `freshState(key, name, sex, pick)`).
  Yard, Dog Card, photos, walks and litters read the coat from the genes (`coatInfo`), so all of them show it.
- Test hook `__paw.cp` (table, pick helpers, the card's state, a dog's stored and drawn coat).

## 4. Text

New keys `adopt.coat_line`, `adopt.eyes_line`, `adopt.label_coat_colour`, `adopt.label_eye_colour`, in all 11 languages and yue
(`node tools/i18n_extract.js --en`, then `node tools/i18n_pack.js`). Coat names go through `coatLabel` (every option already had its
translation). The CJK, Korean and Thai game font subsets already held every new character (`tools/i18n_fonts.js <lang> --for game` gives the
same bytes).
