# v2.23 "Coat colours" (contract; released 7 Nov 2026)

Owner, 7 Nov 2026: "I want that when the users choose their first dog in the game, they can already choose different colour for the dogs.
Different breeds should have different choices of colour, just like how they can be in reality. For example, lab can be white, black and red,
while border collie can be black & white, or 3 mixed colours with different pupil colours."

Suite: `v223_coats`. Save stays `v: 1`; nothing to migrate (a new dog just starts with other genes). Screenshots for the owner first; no release yet.

## 1. What the player sees

- The adoption card (`renderAdopt`, 02_adoption.js) has a colour row under Boy / Girl: one small sitting crayon dog per colour (the breed's
  own art in that coat, cropped to the dog: `cpSit` / `cpCrop`, measured once per breed; a head hid the body colours, review fix 5), the coat name ("Coat: Blue merle"), and, where real dogs have them, eye choices drawn as two eyes (brown or amber, blue, odd)
  with "Eyes: odd". A breed with one colour here (Japanese Spitz, Westie, Maltese, Bernese, Samoyed, Bichon) shows its single coat and no buttons.
- Picking a swatch redraws the meet dog in the playroom (a happy wiggle, `srMeetCoat`) and the picked head in the heads row.
- The pick resets to option 1 whenever the dog changes (the < > buttons and the arrow keys, heads, the playroom board, `srSelectKey`), and so
  does Boy / Girl (`adReset`). Random dog also
  rolls a colour and eyes. Nothing is needed to adopt: option 1 is today's starter.
- **Later starters too**: the Shelter list's starter cards (`shelterCard` kind `starter`, 13_more_dogs.js; a different card from the first
  adoption) get the same row (`shelterCoat[key]`, `shPick`), and `srAdoptFlow` passes the pick to `addDog`. The row says the coat (and eyes), so the
  card's own "coat, eyes" line is only on rescue and resident cards. A Boy / Girl or colour tap
  there now keeps the sheet's scroll position (`shRefresh`).
- Desktop: 40 px swatches inside the card's text area (it scrolls when a long text needs it; the heads row gives way first, as in v2.12).
  Phone: 44 px taps, the row wraps inside the card, the Adopt bar stays at the bottom of the scrolling dock.

## 2. Genetics (10_sex_age_genes.js)

- `STARTER_COATS[key]`: the extra options as `[coat name, the loci that differ from STARTER_GENES]`, tokens such as `Ee`, `bb`, `spsp`, `Mm`.
  Option 1 is always `STARTER_GENES[key]` and `starterPick(key, 0, 0)` returns `null`, so `newDogFields` builds the dog exactly as before
  (`dogs/check_identical.js` and the older suites are untouched).
- Each option is a full genotype, so puppies inherit real genes. Every allele is one the breed's `FREQ` (mods/genes.js) allows; merle is one
  copy (`M/m`) only, so `isDoubleMerle` keeps working at playdates. No paid or sparkle colours: all free.
- Eyes (`STARTER_EYES`): `all` for the husky, the Aussie and the Border Collie (the blue-eye gene `Bl`: husky blue `Bl/Bl` is its starter,
  blue or odd from `Bl/bl` otherwise; a merle coat keeps merle's own rolls with `bl/bl`). The husky's and the Aussie's FREQ has `Bl`; the
  collie's stays `Bl: 0`, so only a starter picked with blue or odd eyes carries it (owner: "border collie can be black & white, or 3 mixed
  colours with different pupil colours"; non-merle blue eyes are real in collies), and random and rescue collies are as before. `merle` for
  the dachshund, Great Dane and mutt (only on a merle coat). Other dogs: brown, or amber on a liver (`b/b`) coat, as `phenotype()` always did.
  No option is a `b/b` stand-in that would give a breed amber eyes it does not have (review fix 2: Pug and Mastiff apricot, Samoyed, Frenchie
  and Bichon cream, Pom white, Great Dane, Cane Corso and Pit Bull brindle, Cane Corso red are gone; `mods/genes.js` gives amber to any `b/b`).
- `phenotype()` rolls the Bl/bl and merle eyes from the dog's id, so `starterIdFor` picks a dog id whose roll gives the chosen eyes; that id
  is the meet dog's and becomes the adopted dog's (`newDogFields(key, sex, born, pick)`, `freshState(key, name, sex, pick)`).
  Yard, photos, walks and litters read the coat from the genes (`coatInfo`), so all of them show it.
- **Dog Cards and Friends** (review fix 1): a card dog is rebuilt with its own id (`dcv:<card>:<i>`), which would roll other eyes and place the
  merle smudges, white patches and spots elsewhere. So a wire dog (`dcDogOf` in 40_dogcards.js, `frCardOf` in 50_friends.js, the Worker's
  `dcDog`) may carry two optional fields: `e`, the eyes as an index into `DC_EYES` (brown, amber, blue, odd; sent only when the id roll
  decides them: `Bl/bl` or a merle that shows), and `p`, the art seed (`hashId` of the dog's id, a 32-bit number, never the id). `dcDog`
  picks the first of `dcv:<card>:<i>`, `dcv:<card>:<i>:1` ... that rolls `e` (`starterIdFor` with a fixed id list, cached; `d.dc` keeps
  the card and index for the expressions redraw) and sets `d.artSeed = p` (`lookOpts` uses it). A card, yard or walk dog without them
  decodes exactly as before. The Worker accepts and stores them only when sent (it refused any extra key before, so it was deployed first).
- Test hook `__paw.cp` (table, pick helpers, the card's state, a dog's stored and drawn coat).

## 3. The options (130 in all; option 1 first)

| Breed | Options | Why (real dogs) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shiba | Red · Black and tan · Cream (all with cream urajiro) | Red, black and tan and sesame are the standard colours; cream is real but not a show colour. Sesame needs a locus the model does not have. |
| Corgi | Red and white · Cream and white | Pembroke red and fawn. Black and white is a Cardigan colour (dropped); a Pembroke tricolour would need tan points the corgi art does not have. |
| Golden | Golden · Cream | Goldens run from cream to golden; the dark red is not modelled. |
| Dachshund | Chocolate and tan · Black and tan · Red · Black dapple | The standard colours plus dapple (merle, eyes brown / blue / odd). |
| Husky | Grey and white · Red and white · Copper and white | Grey, red and copper with white are the classic Husky coats; eyes blue / brown / odd. |
| Mutt | Black and white piebald · Red with white markings · Chocolate and white piebald · Black merle with white markings | A mixed dog can be anything; merle gets the eye choice. |
| Chihuahua | Fawn · Cream · Black and tan · Chocolate and tan · Fawn and white piebald | Chihuahuas come in almost every colour; merle left out (banned by some clubs). |
| Pug | Fawn · Black · Silver fawn | FCI Pug colours (apricot left out: the model draws it as b/b, with amber eyes). |
| Greyhound | Blue with white markings · Black · Fawn · Black and white piebald | Common track and show colours; brindle is not modelled. |
| Beagle | Tricolour · Red and white · Lemon and white · Chocolate tricolour | The usual hound colours. |
| Miniature Poodle | Apricot · Black · White · Chocolate · Silver | Five of the solid poodle colours. |
| Border Collie | Black and white · Chocolate and white · Blue merle · Red merle | The owner's list; chocolate is the "red" of Border Collie people; every coat has eyes brown (amber on chocolate) / blue / odd. Tricolour left out: the collie art has no tan points, so it drew the same as black and white. |
| Samoyed | White | White (cream left out: the model draws it as b/b, with amber eyes). |
| French Bulldog | Fawn · Dark fawn · Pied | Fawn, brindle (the model's dark fawn) and pied; cream left out (b/b, amber eyes). |
| Akita | Red · Sesame · White | Japanese Akita colours; brindle is not modelled. |
| Japanese Spitz | White | Always pure white. |
| Pomeranian | Orange · Cream · Black · Chocolate | The common Pom colours (white left out: b/b, amber eyes). |
| Afghan Hound | Red · Cream · Black and tan · Black | Common Afghan colours (silver is rarer). |
| Great Dane | Fawn · Blue · Harlequin · Mantle | AKC colours (black shows in harlequin and mantle); harlequin has the eye choice. Brindle left out (b/b, amber eyes). |
| English Mastiff | Fawn · Brindle | Standard colours (apricot left out: b/b, amber eyes). |
| German Shepherd | Black and tan · Sable · Black · White | Black and tan, sable and black are the standard colours; white is real (White Swiss lines) but not a show colour. |
| Dobermann | Black and rust · Red and rust · Blue and rust · Fawn and rust | The four standard colours. |
| Cane Corso | Black · Grey · Fawn | Standard colours (brindle and red left out: b/b, amber eyes). |
| Pit Bull | Blue · Black · Fawn · Red | Common colours (any colour is allowed; brindle left out: b/b, amber eyes). |
| Miniature Pinscher | Red · Black and rust · Chocolate and rust | The three standard colours. |
| Labrador | Yellow · Black · Chocolate | The three Lab colours (yellow is the "white" / cream Lab). Fox red left out: every Lab genotype already has a shade that saved dogs use, so a darker one would change existing dogs. |
| Alaskan Malamute | Grey and white · Black and white · Sable and white · Red and white · White | The standard colours. |
| Chow Chow | Red · Black · Blue · Cinnamon · Cream | The five standard colours. |

| Miniature Schnauzer | Salt and pepper · Black · Black and silver · White | The three classic colours plus FCI white. |
| Westie | White | Always white. |
| Yorkshire Terrier | Blue and tan · Black and tan · Blue and tan parti | Blue and tan, black and tan (young dogs) and the parti Yorkie (AKC since 2020). |
| Bichon Frise | White | White (cream left out: b/b, amber eyes). |
| Maltese | White | Always white. |
| Shih Tzu | Gold and white · Black and white · Brindle and white · Liver and white · Black | The most common Shih Tzu colours. |
| Dalmatian | Black spotted · Liver spotted | The two standard spot colours. |
| Bernese | Tricolour | Always tricolour. |
| Aussie | Black tricolour · Blue merle tricolour · Red tricolour · Red merle tricolour | The four standard colours; every coat has eyes brown (amber on red) / blue / odd. |

| Jack Russell | White and tan · Tricolour · White and black | The usual colours, always mostly white. |

Left out on purpose: lab fox red (every Lab genotype already has a shade saved dogs use, so a new one would change existing dogs), Shiba
sesame and Akita / Greyhound brindle (no locus for them), the rare dilutes and lilacs of every breed, merle in breeds whose clubs do not accept
it (chihuahua, Pomeranian), the `b/b` stand-ins that gave amber eyes (section 2), Corgi black and white (Cardigan) and tricolour (no tan in the
corgi art; the Aussie's tan locus would need new corgi art), and Collie tricolour (no tan in the collie art).

## 4. Text

New keys `adopt.coat_line`, `adopt.eyes_line`, `adopt.label_coat_colour`, `adopt.label_eye_colour`, in all 11 languages and yue
(`node tools/i18n_extract.js --en`, then `node tools/i18n_pack.js`). Coat names go through `coatLabel` (every option already had its
translation). The CJK, Korean and Thai game font subsets already held every new character (`tools/i18n_fonts.js <lang> --for game` gives the
same bytes).
