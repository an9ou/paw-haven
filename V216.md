# V2.16 "First Week": contract and as-built notes

Goal (data of 25 Oct 2026): 69 % of players never reach Bond 2, 85 % leave within 10 minutes, 5 % come back on another day.
v2.16 makes the first ten minutes guided and rewarding and gives players reasons to come back. Owner-approved plan.

Code: `game/src/47_firstweek.js` + `game/css/48_firstweek.css` (tag `fw`, test hook `window.__paw.fw`), with small hooks in
`00_core.js` (BOND_TH, save fields, the party in `maybeLevelUp`, `barLabels`, `setHudDog`), `25_guide.js` (the guide engine takes a second step list),
`07_treasure_journal.js` (Calendar tab), `22c_phone_shell.js` (Calendar in the "..." menu), `35_photo.js` (the new frames).
Under the test harness everything except the thresholds needs `prefs.fwTest` (the older suites never see it). Suite: `v216_first_week`.

## 1. Bond thresholds (everyone)
- `BOND_TH = [0, 60, 180, 450, 700, 1000, 1400, 1900, 2500, 3200]` (Bond 2 was 100, Bond 3 was 250).
- `fwBondFix` in `migrate()`: a loaded dog whose points pass a threshold levels up quietly (no fanfare, no level-up queue); `migNote` shows one toast
  ("Good news: Bond levels are a little closer now..."). Levels never go down. Dogs at Bond 2+ after the fix are marked in `S.fw.b2` (no party).
- Checked: Paw (Bond 2) and Lie Down (Bond 3) just come sooner; a second dog still needs Bond 3 and a bigger house (300 coins), so the pack is
  still a few days in; playdates need Bond 6 (`welfare.minBond`, unchanged). The dev buttons read BOND_TH, so they follow.

## 2. Save fields (additive, `v216Fields`, S.v stays 1)
`S.fw = { fresh, b2, wel, cal, frames, wish, named, dock, dockNew, morning, shown }` (full comment in `00_core.js`). A save from before v2.16 gets
`fresh: false`, `wel: null`, `dock: 10`. `freshState` sets `fresh: true`, the welcome week and `dock: 1`, and `S.guide.fw = 1` when `fwOn()`.
Per dog: `d.fwTag` (name-tag colour).

## 3. Puppy's first day (new saves)
Five steps in Gerald's card (`FW_STEPS`): pet (ring on the dog), feed (ring on Feed, then on a food inside the tray), play ball (ring on Play,
then on the Tennis Ball card), name tag (the card's button "Make the tag" opens a name-tag sheet: name, five colours; the HUD portrait wears the tag),
a short walk (ring on Walk). Each step gives +4 Bond (raw) with a toast; the last step tops the dog up to Bond 2 if needed. Measured: 55 points
after four steps, Bond 2 on the walk home (about minute 6). Skip and Next always work; the step is saved in `S.guide.step`, so a reload resumes.
For these saves the old 10-step tour does not run (its lessons stay in Journal > How to play, and "Replay the guide" replays the old tour).

## 4. The next unlock line
`#fwNext` inside the HUD Bond meter: desktop, a taped tag hanging under it; phones, a second line inside the meter (one unlock only, the full line
on long-press). Text from `TRICKS`, the Boutique, routes, houses, shop toys and the veggie patch: e.g. "Bond 2: learns Paw + 8 new outfits".

## 5. The Bond 2 party
The first time each dog reaches Bond 2 the level-up card becomes a party: a heading, crayon confetti over the stage (still bits with Motion off),
a free small Boutique item (Bow Tie, else Red Bandana, Sailor Collar, Squeaky Duck, Plush Bone, else 3 Bone-shaped Biscuits), then the dog does Paw.

## 6. Calendars
- Welcome week (new saves): a paw for each real day played, any 7 days, never in a row needed. Day 1 Chicken & Rice Bowl x2 ("a bowl": there is no
  bowl item, this existing food is a bowl), day 3 a toy (Squeaky Duck or the next not owned), day 5 an outfit (Flower Crown or the next), day 7 a
  photo-mode frame with the dog's name (`fwname`).
- Monthly page (everyone): a crayon page per month (colour, doodle, theme line). 3 days 30 coins; 7 days the season's toy; 12 days 50 coins and a
  treat bag (Bone-shaped Biscuit x3, Carrot Sticks x2, Blueberry Bites x2); 18 days the month's photo frame (`fwmYYYYMM`); 24 days the month's
  outfit (Jan Pom-pom Beanie, Feb Knit Scarf, Mar Flower Crown, Apr Polka-dot Raincoat, May Bumblebee Suit, Jun Frog Raincoat, Jul Sailor Collar,
  Aug Heart Sunglasses, Sep Leaf Beret, Oct Autumn Scarf, Nov Cozy Hoodie, Dec Snowflake Snood; a treat bag if owned). Calendar coins stop at 80 a month.
  A new month gives last month's unclaimed gifts automatically (inside the cap) with a kind note. Nothing is ever taken away.
- Rewards are claimed with a tap. The page opens by itself once a day on the first calm moment in the yard (never during the first-day guide,
  a level-up or another popup), and from Journal > Calendar and the phone "..." menu. No new item art: existing items, plus small crayon icons
  drawn in the module (paw stamp, treat bag, frame, calendar, leaf, snowflake, sun, raindrop).

## 7. See you tomorrow
After 10 minutes of play in a session, or on `pagehide`, the dog names one wish for tomorrow (walk, ball, belly rub, snack), once a day. The next
day's morning page shows it in the dog's words; doing it gives +10 Happiness and a little Bond. Not doing it costs nothing.

## 8. The phone bar, option C (owner, 25 Oct 2026; replaced the Bond-1 "lighter dock")
Every phone player (old saves too) gets four equal big buttons: Care (bath icon), Walk, Map, More (crayon dots); the bar is 92 px plus the safe
area (`--barH`), icons 38 px. Care opens a small crayon tray above it with Feed, Play and Bath & nap (the old feed, play and care trays); More opens
Wardrobe and Journal. Tapping outside or the same button again closes a tray; a bar tray rises over an open tray sheet (`#bar.popon`, z 16).
The tray buttons are the same `.act[data-act]` buttons inside `#bar` (`fwPhoneBar`, `fwBarPop`, `FW_POPS` in 47_firstweek.js), so `doAct`,
keyboard shortcuts and the old selectors keep working; desktop and wider layouts keep the seven buttons (`barLabels` redraws on `phone:layout`).
"new!" tags: Wardrobe and Journal on a v2.16 save until first opened (`S.fw.dockNew`), and on More while one waits. In Tokyo (no Walk) the bar
has three columns. Guide rings (`gdTarget`): a button in a closed tray rings its opener first (Care, then Feed or Play, then the item).
Tests: `t.dock(act)` in test_lib.js opens the right tray before a phone test taps Feed, Play, Bath & nap, Wardrobe or Journal.
