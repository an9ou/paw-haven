# V210: v2.10 "Free to Roam" (every dog its own character, ball play everywhere)

Owner's ask (17 Oct 2026, with iPhone screenshots of the yard and the Tennis Ball game), with the choices the owner made in the choice window:

1. **Every dog is its own character.**
   - Every dog is drawn at the same size. There is no big "main character" with small background dogs.
   - The dogs move around freely.
   - Petting a dog affects **only that dog** (owner's choice). This replaces the v2.8.4 rule where a rub pets every ticked dog.
   - The dog you pet comes to you.
   - Each dog behaves by its personality. For example, the dachshund needs more attention.
2. **Ball play.**
   - When you throw the ball, every dog races for it. The fastest usually wins, and sometimes a cheeky dog steals it and runs a lap of keep-away before bringing it back (owner's choice).
   - The free tennis-ball play replaces the timed Fetch challenge.
   - You play **where you are** (owner's choice): the yard, the house, the Dog Park, the beach and so on, in that place's own scene.
   - The view is wider, showing a bigger room or place, especially on phones.

Standing rules apply:
- Nothing bad ever happens, and there are no fail states.
- Walks stay the main income (~100 coins a day), so ball play must not out-earn them.
- Save changes are additive (`migrate()`), and every old save loads.
- On phones: 44 px taps, 15 px text, no sideways scroll.
- The `copy` suite's word rules.
- No itch.io work. No site deploy.
- **Every old dog render must stay byte-identical** (`node dogs/check_identical.js <base ref>`): change sizes by scaling in the scene, never by changing the dog art.

## 1. Free roam (lane ROAM, Opus: a new system)

- **Same size.** In the yard, the house and every town place, every dog present is drawn at the same scale as the active dog today (depth may shrink dogs further back a little, the same for all). The phone crop still shows every dog or gives a clear "look right" hint.
- **Roaming.** Each dog wanders on its own:
  - Every dog idles, walks to spots, sits, lies down, sniffs and naps, using today's idle system (`16_voices_idle.js`) generalised from one dog to every dog present.
  - Spots come from walkable zones per place, which the Coordinator defines.
  - Dogs don't overlap the place buttons, the mailbox, the missions clipboard, the bowl or each other's tap targets.
  - The bed and nap spots still work.
- **The leader** stays in data (`S.activeId`, used by Walk leader, HUD stats, Tricks default), but it is no longer drawn bigger or centred. The HUD head shows whose stats are on screen. Tapping a dog's head in the HUD row selects it for the stats, as today, and does not move the camera to that dog.
- **Petting only that dog.**
  - A rub or tap on any dog pets that dog only: hearts, Happiness and its Bond once a session.
  - The dog walks over to the front of the scene (towards the player) and stays a moment, then wanders off again.
  - The daily care bonus counts on the first good petting of any dog.
  - v2.8.4's "pet the whole pack" rule is removed. Feed, Bath and Nap keep the pack chip row (they act on the ticked dogs).
- **Personality (data per breed, in a table the Coordinator adds to section 5).** Each breed gets a few numbers that steer roaming:
  - **attention:** how often it comes to the player or nudges for pets. Dachshund high.
  - **energy:** how much it walks versus naps.
  - **zoomies:** sudden runs.
  - **follows:** likes to tag along with another dog.
  - **ball:** speed and steal chance, used in section 2.

  Examples:
  - **Dachshund:** high attention. Comes over and does the "pet me" look, with a speech line if ignored for a while. Loves digging spots.
  - **Chihuahua:** guards the gate and barks at passers-by, fearless.
  - **Golden:** loves the ball.
  - **Pug:** lazy, naps a lot.
  - **Greyhound:** naps, then bursts of zoomies.
  - **Border Collie:** herds the other dogs.
  - **Husky:** chatty.
  - **Corgi:** begs at the bowl.
  - **Beagle:** sniffs everything.
  - **Poodle:** sits politely.
  - **Shiba:** keeps its distance, then comes when it decides to.
  - **Mutt:** gentle, follows you.

  Personality lines stay short and kind, in the copy rules' style.
- **Attention needs.** A dog with high attention that hasn't been petted for a while comes to the front and asks: a pose plus a short line. Ignoring it never hurts anything (nothing bad ever happens). It just keeps asking now and then.
- With one dog, the scene works the same way, with that one dog roaming.

## 2. Ball play everywhere (lane BALL)

- **The Play tray:** the timed **Fetch challenge** (`19_fetch.js`, the ring-timing game) is retired from the Play tray. In its place, **Tennis Ball** (and the other throw toys the player owns: Frisbee, Glow Ball, Driftwood Stick) opens the free throw play.
- **Where you are.** The ball game uses the current place's scene (yard, house, Square, Dog Park, Hilltop, Pier, beach, Mt. Fuji Lookout and so on), not a separate small room.
  - The view is wide: on phones the scene shows more of the place (a wider crop, pannable if needed), and on desktop the full place.
  - The Tennis Ball game seen in the owner's screenshot (the drag-and-throw, bounce off the head) is the base: keep its feel, put it in the place's scene, and widen the view.
- **Every dog plays.** Every awake dog present that isn't a nursing mum or a puppy under 3 months races for the ball:
  - Speed comes from the breed's `ball` number and its Energy. The fastest usually wins.
  - **Steal:** sometimes another dog steals the ball from the winner, or a dog grabs it and runs a keep-away lap before bringing it back. The chance depends on the breed (`ball`) and is higher for cheeky breeds.
  - The dog that brings the ball back gets the catch line. Every dog that played gets the play effects (Happiness, Energy cost), and each dog's Bond is counted once a session.
  - The coins stay within today's play budget. Ball play must not become a coin farm: cap the coins per session and per day at about today's Fetch total.
- **The daily missions and Gerald's guide** that mention Fetch now point at ball play with the same counts (`trackAct('fetch', …)` keeps firing so old missions and journal counts work).
- **Phones:**
  - The throw is a drag and release, as now.
  - The dogs and the ball stay inside the visible crop or the camera follows the ball.
  - No toast covers the play area.

### 5.3 Cross-lane API (ROAM implements, BALL uses; names are fixed, stubs are on `integration/v2.10`)

| Name | What |
|---|---|
| `rmOn()` | Roaming runs now: the scene is up (`cur.mode === 'yard'`) and nothing holds it. |
| `rmDogs()` | Every dog drawn in the scene, the leader first. |
| `rmPos(d)` | `{ x, y, face, sc, pose }`: feet in world units, facing, scale, pose now. |
| `rmScale(y)` | The depth scale at feet y, the **same for every dog**. Desktop: today's active-dog size at y 500 (the leader's v2.9 size), a little smaller further back (at least 0.85 of it). ROAM may set the base 0.85–1.0 after screenshots (four dogs must fit), one number for every dog. |
| `rmSet(d, o)` | Place a dog at once: any of `{ x, y, face, pose }`. Cheap enough to call every frame (moves a transform, redraws the art only when pose or facing changes). |
| `rmTo(d, x, y, o)` | Walk there (`o.run` runs, `o.speed` in world units a second), then `o.pose`, `o.face`, `o.done()`. |
| `rmPause(tag)` / `rmResume(tag)` | Hold or release the autonomous roaming. While held, dogs stay where they are and only `rmSet` / `rmTo` move them. BALL uses the tag `'ball'`. |
| `rmZone(place)` | The walkable zone of a place: `{ y0, y1, x0, x1, no: [[x, y, w, h], ...] }` (5.2). |
| `rmEl(d)` | The dog's `<g data-dog>` element in the scene (new; ROAM adds it). |
| `persOf(d)`, `persLine(d)` | 5.1 (Coordinator). |
| `blOn()` | Ball play is on (BALL). ROAM's attention asks, ambient lines, idle and the HUD-row selection keep quiet while it is. |

### 5.4 Ball play (BALL, `38_ball.js`)

- **Entry:** the Play tray's Fetch (challenge) cards go. Tennis Ball, Frisbee, Glow Ball and Driftwood Stick (`FETCH_TOYS` the player owns) get a "Play ball" card that starts ball play in the current scene (mode stays `'yard'`, `blOn()` true, `rmPause('ball')`). The old `PawToys` Tennis Ball / Glow Ball room games leave the tray (`toysPlayable`), the other toys stay. `19_fetch.js` keeps its file with no entry point (old `toyAt_` keys stay).
- **Feel:** port the `mods/toys.js` `ballToy` throw (drag and release, arc, bounce, the head bounce) into the scene's `#ballLayer` (in `yardWorldSVG`, done). Frisbee floats, the stick tumbles, the Glow Ball glows at night.
- **Who plays:** every dog in `rmDogs()` that is awake, at least 3 months old and not a nursing mum.
- **Speed:** `speed = 520 x ball x (0.7 + 0.3 x Energy / 100)` world units a second, x0.8 under 6 months, +-8 % jitter per throw. The fastest usually wins.
- **Steal:** on a throw where two or more dogs play, the dog with the highest `steal` that arrives within 0.6 s of the winner steals it with chance `steal x 0.6`, or the winner runs a keep-away lap with chance `own steal x 0.5`. At most 1 throw in 3 has a steal or lap. Golden (`trait: 'ball'`) never keeps away. Short kind lines in a speech bubble, never a toast.
- **Return:** the dog that brings it back gets the catch line. Each throw brought back: +2 coins (Frisbee +3, Stick +1 extra), **at most 16 coins a session and 40 a day** (`S.bl.coins`, `S.bl.day`, `localISO()`), shown as "Ball play coins: done for today" once spent. Every dog that ran: Happiness and Energy as Fetch did, and its Bond once a session. `trackAct('fetch', ...)` per catch with the same data as Fetch, so missions and the journal still count. `S.bl.n` counts returns.
- **Missions and Gerald's guide** that said Fetch say ball play, with the same counts.
- **Phones:** crop `BL_CROP` 760 wide (`blSky` fills the band above the scene) with the camera following the ball, throw by drag and release, no toast over the play area, a Done button in the dock (44 px).

**ROAM (37_roam.js + 39_roam.css, tag `rm`).**
- Every dog, the leader too, is a `<g class="rmdog" data-dog>` in `#pack`, placed by its feet in world units. The leader's group keeps `#dogPos` / `#dogArt` / `#dogHit` / `#dogFx`, and `dogTo()` moves it through `rmLegacyTo`. Positions are not saved.
- Size: `rmScale(y)` = `RM_BASE` **0.88** at feet y 500 (four dogs fit the yard), `RM_DEPTH` 0.0012 a unit, clamped to 0.86..1.12 of the base. The same for every dog.
- Zones: `RM_ZONES` per place (feet y band; x always 60 to 940), `RM_ZONES_PH` for the phone yard (424–594). No-go boxes come from the live DOM (`rmScan`, the `.hot` props) plus the bowl, the dog house and the Square's fountain basin (`RM_SCENERY`).
- Routing: `rmRoute` walks straight when the line is clear, else over a coarse grid of the zone round the no-go boxes. The feet never enter a box; the tap rect keeps off tappable things where it can (a soft cost).
- Depth rule: a dog standing behind a floor prop (its feet above the prop's base) keeps its body off it (at most a fifth of its width over it), or it would be drawn in front of the prop.
- Overlap rules (`rmOk`, `rmSoft`): nobody overlaps the leader by more than 15% either way (its own tap rect or the leader's); the dogs in front of a dog cover at most 45% of it together; two dogs' tap rects overlap at most 30%. Props up to `RM_HUGE` (70000 square units) keep the 40% / never-the-middle rule (small ones 8%), ordinary decorations at most 10% tap overlap, seasonal props and seasonal decorations (`fest` in `HM_DECOR`, `rmScan` tag `fest`) none; in `rmLeast` touching a seasonal decoration costs more than standing on a pack dog. When nothing is free, `rmCandsAny` loosens in levels (50% apart, leader 30%, then no depth rule), and last `rmLeast` takes the least crowded spot. The feet keep out of every box at every level.
- Hold-safe: while roaming is held (`rmPause`, ball play `blOn()`, photo mode, a modal, training) there is no `rmValidate`, no pet walk to the front and no sofa walk. A dog that wakes while held stands up where it lay. `rmPause` sets each pack dog's `packNext` 2–4 s ahead, so it picks its next move a few seconds after the release.
- Petting: a rub or tap pets only the touched dog. `crPetPack()` is a no-op now (returns `[]`); a pack dog is petted through `crPetAt` (hearts, Happiness up to the soft cap, +3 Bond once a session). `rmPetted` walks it to the front for about 4 s.
- Phone home crop (`rmCropFit`): native width x `RM_WIDEN` 1.3, at most `RM_CROP` 700, and capped so the smallest scene tap target stays 44 px (the nursery basket, `NURSERY_AT.hit`, counts whenever a litter is drawn). That is about **490 units on an iPhone 15**, not the ~700 that 5.2 planned. Phones seed and prefer spots inside the visible crop. The colour above the zoomed-out scene is `rmSky()`.
- Look chips `#rmLookL` / `#rmLookR` (62 x 48 px): the head of the dog out of view plus an arrow (and a count with two or more); a tap pans to it. Hidden during ball play (`html.bl-on`).
- The Mt. Fuji Lookout gets the wide crop too (at the old crop the dogs stood as tall as the torii). Market Street keeps its own.
