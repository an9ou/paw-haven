Read `PIPELINE.md`, `V27.md` (Home & Pack: placement, `S.decor[name].at`, the mailbox Collect button), `V26.md` §9 and `V25.md` §11 (the festival system as built), `PHONE.md` and `CLAUDE.md` first. **Where this file disagrees with older docs, this file wins.** `V27.md` is Home & Pack (shipped as v2.7), so winter is **v2.8** (section 13, decision 1).

- **Snow Days, 1 Dec – end of Feb.** It hardly ever snows in Doodle Doggo Town, so Pip brings a cart of snow from the hills every morning: two **snowdrifts** in the yard to dive into and a **snow heap** for a **snowdog** that looks like your dog (badly). ## Standing rules (every lane)

- **Nothing bad ever happens.** Snow is soft, cold is cosy: no slipping, no thin ice, nobody lost in the snow. A dog without a coat still plays and gets one kind tip. Fortunes are only good ("Small luck" is the lowest). The snowdog never melts on screen.
- **Coins only**, never money. Gifts arrive in letters and pay on **Collect** (v2.7). No selling dogs.
- **Food safety.** Every treat is dog-safe as described, its `tip` a two-sentence lesson. New lessons: macadamia in festive cookies, fatty leftovers and gravy, mochi, real holly. Cocoa, chocolate, macadamia, mochi and holly appear only in a "never" sentence. Decorations are felt and paper, never real holly, mistletoe or poinsettia.
- **Welfare.** Short-coated and small dogs feel the cold (warm wear helps, the tips say so). Paws get wiped after winter walks (road salt).
- **Words.** `copy` suite rules (no semicolons, emoji or browser dialogs in player text, never "breed" as a verb, "stud", "sell" for dogs). Nothing religious in play: Winter Lights, no named holiday. The torii, shrine and jizo are quiet scenery (section 12).
- **Look.** Style G, crude crayon dogs, and a snowdog just as crude. New props look drawn by the same hand as their neighbours.
- **Economy.** Walks stay the main income (~100 a day). Contest 10 coins once a day (1–25 Dec), otoshidama 30 once a year. Drifts, snowdog and fortunes pay Happiness and Bond only. Foods 8–14, clothes 50–65, decorations 80–120.
- **Save.** `S.v` stays 1. New fields only through `v28Fields()` (section 1). Readers default too.
- **Shared scope.** Tags: WINTER `wn` (`.wn-`), NEW YEAR `ny` (`.ny-`), FUJI HILL `jp` (`.jp-`). Taken: `fs hw ho cr sr ms gd bd sh pk mb`, `.pc-`. Grep `game/src` and `game/css` before committing. Cross-lane calls guarded.
- **Byte-identity** against the branch **`v28-base`** (= the v2.7 release on `main`): every old dog, scene, strip, prop, item, icon and bowl render, winter scenes with Lights and New Year off, and the stall's `leaf`/`halloween` kinds. All of this **with `jp` off**: in the game the Japan scenery changes the outdoor art of every season, summer included, on purpose (section 12.1).
- **Harness:** season `summer`, festivals `off`, pack care off, and **the winter flags never turn on by date unless the suite sets `prefs.wnTest`** (v2.5 suites use `fest: 'auto'` with December dates), and the Japan scenery stays off unless it sets `prefs.jpTest`.
- **Phones:** section 9. **Screenshots:** 1280×720 and 390×844, day and night, before and after, next to neighbours, under `game/shots_v28/<lane>/` (< 300 KB each, ≤ 60 per lane, removed before release).

**Lessons from V25 §11 and V26 §9, built in:** spec spots landed on the garden bed and off the top, so drifts, stall, banner and fortune box **reuse as-built spots** (only the snow heap is new). Every name was grepped against items, recipes and breed text (a v1.3 dish clash). The real date broke old suites, so the `wnTest` gate is in core from the start. `checkEnv` knew only two flags: core adds the new ones. Props glared at night: new props take `{ night }`. A prop was cut at the crop edge and bunting sat under a place label: both are pass/fail (section 9). Draw order is fixed (section 7). Placed decorations can land on festival props: `fsKeepOut(place)`.

In the middle ground, a small vermilion torii on the left with a knee-high wooden shrine and its shimenawa behind it, two stone lanterns, a bench facing the view on the right, sakura or momiji by season, a stepping-stone path. The dog stands on the path in the foreground. The root exposes `data-spot-bench="x,y"`.

**Look at Fuji** (place button, 44 px). ## 13. Open decisions (recommendation taken; the Project manager may overrule)

1. **File and version.** `V27.md` is Home & Pack, shipped as v2.7. **Taken:** v2.8 in `V28.md`, base `v28-base` (the v2.7 release).
2. **Include New Year? Taken: yes**, the owner is in Japan: lights down and pine up on 26 Dec, cards on 1 Jan, to 7 Jan (Kantō). Nothing religious **in play**. The torii, the hillside shrine, the stone lanterns and the jizo (section 12) are quiet scenery, as the owner asked.
3. **Dates. Taken:** Snow Days 1 Dec – end of Feb (like the leaf festival's two months), Lights 1–25 Dec, New Year 26 Dec – 7 Jan, JST. Snow only to January would leave February empty.
4. **Editions. Taken:** winter items recur yearly. Only the hood is `ed: 2027`, free in a letter. 2028 needs a Monkey item (TODO).
5. **Snow where it hardly snows. Taken:** Pip's cart, whatever the weather. Snow weather and the snowman stay as they are.
6. **The snowdog resets daily** (the mission repeats), "went up to the hills", never a melting picture.
7. **No toys, no lucky bag** (a lucky bag is a random box for coins).
8. **Gifts:** otoshidama 30 coins, the parcel 3 Warm Carob Cups. Both under a day's walk.
9. **Carob, not "Not-Cocoa":** a child should never remember a dog drinking something called cocoa.
10. **Japan scope. Taken (owner): everywhere.** Fuji on every outdoor skyline in every season, the 10 details, Fuji View Hill. The woods are the only outdoor scene without Fuji (no sky shows through the canopy).
11. **A flag, not a new base. Taken:** `env.jp` keeps every old art test byte-identical with it off. The game shows Japan to everyone, so the summer art changes in play on purpose (12.1).
12. **No hatsumōde visit. Taken:** a shrine visit would be a worship mechanic. The New Year moment is the **first Fuji of the year** instead, which is just as Japanese and fully secular. The Fortune box stays in the Square, as the PM asked.
13. **Fuji hides in rain and snow. Taken:** true to life, and it gives the game a joke instead of a missing mountain.
14. **Fuji View Hill at Bond 2**, like the Dog Park, so new players reach it in a few days.
15. **Size.** With section 12 this file is about 55 KB, over the earlier 40 KB cap. Lanes read only their sections, so it stays one file as the PM asked.
