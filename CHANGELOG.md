| 2026-10-16 | v2.8.2 Account safety | Forgot your password? (under Log in, on the title and in Settings): type the email, and the game always answers "If that email has an account, a link is on its way". The email (from noreply@doodledoggo.app through Resend, with Noodle) links to https://doodledoggo.app/reset, a small page in the game's look that sets the new password and then says "All set! Go back to the game on itch.io and log in." Links work for 30 minutes and once, only the newest works, only their SHA-256 is stored, and a new password logs out every other device. Rate-limited per email (3 an hour) and per IP. An old Supabase account not moved yet gets Supabase's own reset email, and its link moves the account over with the new password. Until the Resend account is set up, the game says resets by email aren't on yet. New tests in `worker`, `cloud` and `account`. |
| 2026-10-16 | v2.8.1 Cloudflare login | Cloud save and accounts moved from Supabase to Cloudflare: the Worker `pawhaven-api` (cloudflare/worker.js) with the D1 database `pawhaven`. The title screen is the same (Log in / Make an account / Play as guest), no emails are ever sent, and Export / Import save codes stay. Passwords are PBKDF2-SHA256 (100k iterations, 16-byte salt), sessions are random 32-byte bearer tokens of which only the SHA-256 is stored, and log in, make an account and guests are rate-limited. |
| 2026-10-16 | v2.8.1 Cloudflare login | Nothing is lost in the move: a device that was logged in carries its login over on its own (same account, same save, no password typed). The save syncs on every change (about 2 s after you play), when the game opens, when you come back to it and when the device is back online. Realtime is gone. |
