# Doodle Doggo

**A cozy dog game, sketchbook edition. Everything here is lovingly sketched. Except the dogs.**

Welcome to a little pencil-drawn town where every fence post, teacup and flower box has been sketched with care. Then someone handed the dogs a crayon. They drew themselves, on purpose, and they are very proud of the result.

Adopt a wonky crayon pup, feed it, bathe it, rub its belly and take it for walks around town. Decorate the yard, fill the house with dog furniture, grow a garden and grow a pack. Nothing bad ever happens here. The worst thing that can happen is a dog walking into a log and pretending it meant to do that.

The ugly dogs are the joke. You will love them anyway.

## Features

- **14 dog types**: shiba, corgi, golden retriever, dachshund, husky, mutt, chihuahua, pug, greyhound, beagle, poodle, border collie, samoyed and french bulldog. Each has its own voice, personality, size and joke.
- **Home and pack**: 16 decorations and 5 pieces of furniture the dogs climb, nap and play on, all movable by drag or tap. Every dog gets its own bed, and Feed, Pet, Bath and Nap care for the whole pack at once.
- **The Shelter Playroom**: a cozy dog-café shelter where dogs looking for a home play, nap and chase tossed toys. Read the Adopt me board, meet each dog and adopt for free.
- **Walks as a runner**: pick a route, then dash, jump and dig your way through the park collecting coins and treasures.
- **Puppy playdates with real coat genetics**: coats and traits are inherited through real-style gene loci, with real welfare rules (age, seasons and rest between litters). Name the puppies, keep them, or find them a loving family for free.
- **Sparkle pups**: a rare shimmering puppy that your journal remembers.
- **Seasons and festivals**: the town follows the real calendar, with leaf piles and a harvest stall in autumn and a Halloween costume parade. For Halloween 2026 Mrs. Plum's Pumpkin Patch Pop-up opens in the Square with 16 things made for 2026 only.
- **Garden and kitchen**: grow veggies, cook dog-safe treats and learn what dogs can and cannot eat.
- **Trick mini-games**: teach tricks with quick timing games.
- **Town places**: a map with a market, shops, a vet, a playboard, a wardrobe and more.
- **Mailbox**: postcards and news from the dogs you have raised.
- **Treasure hunt and journal**: find map pieces, dig things up and fill your Treasure Journal.

## How to play

- **Mouse or touch.** Click or tap the buttons along the bottom: Feed, Play, Care, Walk, Wardrobe, Journal and Map.
- **Pet your dog** by rubbing it with the mouse or your finger.
- **Walks**: press Space or tap Jump to hop, hold to jump further, and Duck to slide under things. D digs, B opens the bag and Esc pauses.
- **Decorate** from the yard or the house: drag a decoration (or tap it, then tap a spot) and press Done.
- Plays on laptops and on phones held upright. On a phone, tap the fullscreen button for the best view.

## Saves

- Your game is saved in your browser, on this device.
- **Export / Import save codes** are in Settings. Export a code now and then to keep a backup or move your game to another device.
- **Make a free account** (an email and your own password, no emails are ever sent) to keep your save in the cloud and play the same game on any device.
- Safari can clear guest saves in embedded games like this one. If you play in Safari, make an account or export a save code.
