Title: Tokyo Trip, 7 new breeds and Halloween costumes (v2.11 + v2.11.1)

Hi everyone! Two updates landed this weekend, so here is everything new in Doodle Doggo.

## 🚄 Tokyo Trip (v2.11)

Once you reach Bond 3, a new **Station** pin appears on the town map. Buy a Shinkansen day ticket and ride to Tokyo with your whole pack, every head at the train window while Mt. Fuji rolls past.

Tokyo has its own map and six places to visit:

- **Shibuya Crossing**: sit with the Hachikō statue and hear his story, then cross the scramble together.
- **Yoyogi Park Dog Run**: ball play and visiting dogs.
- **Asakusa & Sensō-ji**: a photo under the giant lantern, an omikuji fortune (always a good one) and dog-safe Nakamise treats.
- **Ueno Park**: a hanami picnic in spring, a momiji walk in autumn.
- **Tokyo Tower**
- **Odaiba Seaside**: a sunset walk along the shore.

Collect all **seven station stamps** in your Stamp Book to unlock a Tokyo photo frame. There are Tokyo-only souvenirs too: three bandanas, a Lucky Cat decoration and a Shinkansen plush.

## 🐕 Seven new breeds

Say hello to the **Akita, Japanese Spitz, Pomeranian, Afghan Hound, Great Dane, English Mastiff and German Shepherd**. That makes 21 breeds, all drawn just as badly as the rest. 🖍️

## 🃏 Dog Cards: a first step to multiplayer

If you have an account, you can now share a **Dog Card** (it's off until you switch it on). Other players' dogs drop by the Dog Park and the Yoyogi dog run. Tap one to see its card and give it a paw, once a day. There is no typing and no photos, and your email is never shown.

## 🎃 Costumes & Sizes (v2.11.1)

**Three new Halloween costumes** from Mrs. Plum's Pumpkin Patch Pop-up:

- Pumpkin Patch Suit
- Trick-or-Treat Ghost (with a little jack-o'-lantern bucket)
- Little Bat Costume

They fit every breed, puppies too, and they're yours to keep. The pop-up is only open **until 2 November**!

**Dogs now come in sizes.** Every dog is drawn bigger than before, and big dogs (Great Dane, Mastiff, Golden…) now tower over the little ones (Chihuahua, Pug, Pomeranian…). Puppies are smaller again.

**Fixes:** the German Shepherd's head no longer floats above its body, and the Shelter text is easier to read on phones.

Thanks for playing and for every message. Give your doggos a pat from me! 🐾
