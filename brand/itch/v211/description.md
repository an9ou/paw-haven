# Doodle Doggo

**🖍️ Adopt a wobbly crayon pup to love**

A cozy dog game, sketchbook edition. Everything here is lovingly sketched. Except the dogs.

Welcome to a little pencil-drawn town where every fence post, teacup and flower box has been sketched with care. Then someone handed the dogs a crayon. They drew themselves, on purpose, and they are very proud of the result.

Meet Noodle, a very long dachshund who is sure the drawing came out perfectly. Adopt a wobbly crayon pup of your own, feed it, bathe it, rub its belly and take the whole pack for walks. Nothing bad ever happens here. The worst thing that can happen is a dog walking into a log and pretending it meant to do that.

**New in v2.11: the Tokyo Trip.** Catch the Shinkansen from the town Station, ride past Mt. Fuji with every dog at a window, and spend a day in Tokyo. Seven new dog types have arrived too, 21 in all.

**Free to play, right here in your browser, on your phone or your computer. No download, no ads.**

## The Tokyo Trip (new)

- **The Station and the Shinkansen.** Buy a day ticket at the Station on the town map, then ride to Tokyo and back as often as you like that day. Every dog presses its nose to the window.
- **Shibuya and Hachikō.** Cross the scramble with the whole pack, then sit with the Hachikō statue, the most loyal dog in Tokyo.
- **Yoyogi Park dog run.** A big lawn for ball play, with other dogs dropping by.
- **Asakusa.** Walk under the giant red lantern, find a dog-safe treat on the snack street and pick a lucky omikuji fortune for your dog.
- **Tokyo Tower.** A red-and-white tower that glows at night.
- **Ueno Park.** A hanami picnic under cherry blossoms in spring, red leaves in autumn.
- **Odaiba seaside.** A sunset walk on the sand under the Rainbow Bridge.
- **The stamp book.** One stamp for every place, seven in all. Fill the book for a Tokyo photo frame.
- **Souvenirs.** Shibuya, Asakusa and Tokyo Tower bandanas, a Shinkansen plush toy and a lucky cat for the house.

Little kind notes along the way share real dog manners: keep the lead short at busy crossings, check the sign before a dog goes into a temple, and remember that real trains in Japan want dogs in a closed carrier.

## 21 dog types, seven of them new

- **The first 14:** shiba, corgi, golden retriever, dachshund, husky, mutt, chihuahua, pug, greyhound, beagle, poodle, border collie, samoyed and french bulldog.
- **New in v2.11:** **Akita**, **Japanese Spitz**, **Pomeranian**, **Afghan Hound**, **Great Dane**, **English Mastiff** and **German Shepherd**.

Each has its own voice, size, coat colours and a very strong opinion about naps.

## Dogs who do their own thing

- **Free to roam.** Your dogs wander the yard, the house, the Square and every place on the map. Nobody stands in a row.
- **Real personalities.** The Akita sits quietly by the gate. The Pomeranian spins when it is happy. The Japanese Spitz is a show-off. The beagle follows its nose. Every dog type has its own habits and its own spots.
- **Pet the dog you touch.** Rub one dog and only that dog melts.
- **Ball play everywhere.** Drag the ball, let go and watch the chase, in the yard, the park, the Square or on the sand at Odaiba.
- **Pack walks.** Take the whole pack out for a walk: every dog runs along behind the leader.
- **Photo mode.** Tap the camera for a postcard of your pack with a frame and a caption, ready to save.

## Dog Cards (new)

Share a **Dog Card** with a free account, and other players' dogs will visit your dog park and the Yoyogi dog run. Tap a visitor to read its card and give it a paw. It is opt-in, there is no typing and no chatting, and only two picked words ever show as a name. Never your email.

## More to do

- **Puppy playdates with real coat genetics.** Coats and traits are inherited through real-style gene loci, with real welfare rules (age, seasons and rest between litters). Name the puppies, keep them, or find them a loving family for free.
- **Sparkle pups.** A rare shimmering puppy that your journal remembers.
- **Seasons and festivals.** The town follows the real calendar: cherry blossoms in spring, leaf piles and a harvest stall in autumn, the Halloween costume parade, Snow Days and Winter Lights in the Square, and a New Year with a fortune box and New Year cards.
- **The Mt. Fuji Lookout.** Mt. Fuji stands on the skyline all year round, and a quiet place on the map, the Mt. Fuji Lookout, has the best view of it.
- **Home and pack.** Decorate the yard and house, give every dog its own bed, and care for the whole pack at once.
- **The Shelter Playroom.** A cozy dog-café shelter where dogs looking for a home play and nap. Read the Adopt me board, meet them and adopt for free.
- **Walks as a runner.** Dash, jump and dig your way through the park collecting coins and treasures.
- **Garden and kitchen.** Grow veggies and cook dog-safe treats. Nothing toxic to dogs can be planted, cooked or fed.
- **Trick mini-games, daily missions, the mailbox and the treasure journal.**

## How to play

- **Mouse or touch.** Click or tap the buttons along the bottom: Feed, Play, Care, Walk, Wardrobe, Journal and Map.
- **Pet a dog** by rubbing it with the mouse or your finger.
- **Throw the ball:** open Play, pick a ball and drag it across the scene.
- **Walks:** press Space or tap Jump to hop, hold to jump further, and Duck to slide under things. D digs, B opens the bag and Esc pauses.
- **Tokyo:** open the town Map and tap the Station.
- Plays on laptops and on phones held upright. On a phone, tap the fullscreen button for the best view.

## Saving your game

- Your game saves by itself in your browser, on the device you play on.
- **Free accounts.** Log in with an email and your own password to keep your save online and play the same game on any device. If you forget your password, the game emails you a 6-digit code and you choose a new one right there, no links to open.
- **Export / Import save codes** are in Settings. A code is a handy backup, and a way to move a game between devices.
- Safari can clear saves of games played inside a web page like this one. If you play in Safari, log in or export a save code now and then.
