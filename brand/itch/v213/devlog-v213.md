Title: 14 new dogs, 14 new faces, and a home on doodledoggo.app (v2.12 – v2.13.3)

Hi everyone! A lot has happened since the Tokyo Trip, so here is everything new in Doodle Doggo.

## 🐕 Fourteen new friends (v2.12)

The shelter is bigger than ever: **35 breeds** in all. Say hello to:

- **Doberman**: a sleek black and rust guard with soft folded ears
- **Cane Corso**: a big square head and a gentle heart
- **Pit Bull**: a wide grin, a cuddly goofball who needs you
- **Miniature Pinscher**: tiny, fast, in bursts
- **Labrador Retriever**: an otter tail and a nose for snacks
- **Alaskan Malamute**: a fluffy "woo-woo" talker
- **Chow Chow**: a lion ruff, tiny ears and a blue-black tongue
- **Standard Poodle**: tall, leggy, curly and polite
- **Miniature Schnauzer**: a beard, bushy brows, always on guard
- **West Highland White Terrier**: a carrot tail, sniffs everything
- **Yorkshire Terrier**: a silky coat and a topknot bow
- **Bichon Frise**: a cotton puff that spins
- **Maltese**: floor-length white hair, follows you about
- **Shih Tzu**: a flat face, a topknot and a lazy streak

Every new dog has real coat colours, a voice (from the Doberman's deep bark to the Yorkie's tiny yap), a personality, a favourite trick, foods and toys, and a kind little care tip on its adoption card. All drawn just as badly as the rest. 🖍️

There are **371 new mix names** too: Labradoodle, Goldador, Maltipoo, Morkie, Shih-poo, Schnoodle and many more for puppy playdates. The old poodle is now called the **Miniature Poodle**.

## 😊 Faces & Feelings (v2.13)

Every dog, puppies too, now has **14 new faces**: Sleepy, Surprised, Curious, Heart eyes, Proud, Guilty, Achoo, Blep, Zoomies, Pout, Dreamy, Wow, Wink and Yum.

They show when you pet, feed or bathe your dog, when they catch a ball (and pout a little when play ends), nail a trick, dig somewhere they shouldn't (guilty!), or see a Tokyo place for the first time. Each breed has its favourites: a dramatic Shiba pouts, an English Mastiff is sleepy and dreamy, a Japanese Spitz shows off with a wink.

Photo mode has a **face picker**, so you can choose the perfect expression for your postcard.

## 🔧 Fixes (v2.12 – v2.13.3)

- Heads stay on bodies! No more floating heads on the Yorkie, Shiba, Husky, Beagle, Maltese and friends, or on any mix.
- Ball play: the shop buttons and the shops on Market Street no longer get in the way while you throw. The ball lost its shadow too.
- Every window (shops, journal, settings, wardrobe, trick training) is now coloured right up to its crayon line, day and night.

## 🌐 Play on doodledoggo.app

You can now play the game right on our website: **https://doodledoggo.app**. It starts by itself, and there's a full screen button for computers and phones.

New players can read the **How to play** guide at https://doodledoggo.app/guide, and every dog has its profile on the Dogs page.

Your save lives in your browser, so the website and itch.io keep separate saves. Make a free account in the game's Settings to play the same dogs everywhere.
