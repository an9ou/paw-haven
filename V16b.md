# Paw Haven v1.6 addendum: realistic barking and natural idle behaviour

Client request: "add some barking sounds for the dogs, with barking in certain situations based on how dogs actually behave. Also add more default dog gestures that simulate real dog habits, like lying down, sitting down, etc."

## A. Dog voices (audio module, `PawAudio`)
New API (additive):

```
PawAudio.bark(voice, kind, opts?)   // routed through bus.sfx; returns nothing; never throws
// voice: { breed, pitch: 0.85–1.15 (per-dog seeded), size: 'small'|'medium'|'large' }
// kind:  'woof' | 'yip' | 'alert' (2–3 quick barks) | 'demand' (single insistent bark) | 'play' (high excited bark-bark)
//        | 'howl' | 'talk' (husky "awoo-wa-woo") | 'scream' (shiba dramatic yodel, short and funny) | 'whine' | 'growl-play' (soft playful rrr)
//        | 'sneeze' | 'yawn' (squeaky) | 'snore' (soft loop step) | 'pant' (short breathy loop step) | 'huff' (grumbly exhale)
// opts:  { volume: 0..1, distance: 0..1 (farther = quieter + lowpass, for NPC/distant dogs) }
```

- All voices are synthesised: formant-filtered noise bursts plus a pitched glide, with breed formants. No files.
- **Breed character:**

| Breed | Voice |
|---|---|
| Shiba | sharp, and `scream` is a short comedic yodel |
| Corgi | a surprisingly big bark for a small dog |
| Golden | deep, warm "woof" |
| Dachshund | a surprisingly deep, loud bark |
| Husky | rarely barks; mostly `talk` and `howl` |
| Mutt | medium, soft |

- Rescues use their breed voice with seeded pitch.
- Respect the sfx volume and mute. Duck the music slightly (about −3 dB) for barks.
- Never stack more than 2 voices at once.

## B. When dogs bark (game), based on real dog habits
Always context-driven, with cooldowns so it never gets annoying. Each dog has at most 1 bark event per 25 real seconds, unless the player triggers it.

| Situation | What the dog does |
|---|---|
| **Greeting.** The game opens, the walker comes home, or the player returns to the tab after 2+ minutes | happy `play` bark plus `whine`, with a happy pose |
| **Alert.** An NPC dog or person passes in a hangout, the mailbox gift arrives, or Captain Fluff appears in the garden | `alert` barks with a stiff stance (idle with ears up). Shiba: `scream` at Captain Fluff, 1 in 3 times |
| **Contagious.** One of your dogs barks | another dog may answer (30%) after a short delay. A distant NPC dog may answer at the park, Dog Park or square |
| **Demand.** Hunger < 25 or Happiness < 25 | occasional `demand` bark plus `whine`, at most every 60 s |
| **Play.** Fetch throw, toy start, or the play bow during idle | `play` bark. During Rope Tug: `growl-play` |
| **Food.** Feed popup opened, or bowl filling | excited `yip` (Biscuit always) |
| **Night.** Clear night at home | husky `howl` at the moon about once every 3 game-hours |
| **Night, others** | a rare distant neighbourhood dog bark (`distance` 0.8) |
| **Sleep** | soft `snore` steps |
| **Yawning** | `yawn` with the yawn pose |
| **Hot day** | `pant` steps while in the `hot` pose |
| **Sniffing** | occasional `sneeze` |
| **Grumpy.** A bath starts, or the vet | `huff` (Mochi always) |
| **Speak trick and show-off** | the breed's `woof`; Husky gives `talk` |
| **Clicked while asleep** | a sleepy `huff` |

- **Settings:** a "Barking: Normal / Fewer / Off" option, saved in prefs. Fewer doubles the cooldowns and turns off the ambient triggers.

## C. Natural idle behaviour (game + dogs)
Replace the simple idle with an **idle behaviour scheduler** per dog, run when the dog isn't busy. It picks weighted behaviours every 4–9 s and holds each one for a realistic time.

| Behaviour | Pose(s) | Hold | When it's more likely |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stand and look around (ears perk, head turn) | `idle` | 3–6 s | always |
| Sit | `sit` | 5–12 s | always, and by the bowl when hungry |
| Lie down | `down` | 8–20 s | after play or a walk, when Energy < 60, on hot days, indoors |
| Circle then lie down | `walk` + a CSS 360° circle, then `down` | — | before lying down 40% of the time (real dogs circle) |
| Nap | `sleep` | 15–40 s | at night, Energy < 35, in the bed in the house (uses the bed spot), in the dog house in the yard |
| Stretch (play-bow stretch) | `bow` | 1.5 s | after waking or lying down |
| Yawn | `yawn` (new) | 1.2 s | evening, night, after naps |
| Scratch an ear | `scratch` | 2 s | random, more when Cleanliness < 40 |
| Sniff the ground and wander | `sniff` (new), short walks | — | outdoors, more at the park, woods, beach and hilltop |
| Shake off | `shake` | 1 s | after rain exposure, after a bath, after lying down |
| Roll in the grass or sand | `rollover` | 2–3 s | outdoors on grass or sand when happy; at the beach, Cleanliness −2 |
| Tail chase | `walk` + a fast CSS spin | 2 s | young dogs only (life stage puppy or young), when happy |
| Zoomies | `walk`/`jump` plus a fast run across the scene | 2–3 s | Happiness > 85 and Energy > 70, once in a while; after a bath ("post-bath zoomies") |
| Drink water | `eat` at the water bowl | — | after play and on hot days |
| Watch the player | `idle` facing toward the cursor | — | mouse near the dog on desktop |
| Social | sniff another of your dogs, play-bow invite (`bow` + `play` bark), lie down together (`down` side by side) | — | multi-dog homes |

- Personality tweaks:
  - Mochi does more aloof sits and lies facing away.
  - Biscuit sits by the bowl more.
  - Sunny brings toys and wants play.
  - Noodle sniffs and digs more.
  - Frost does more zoomies and talking.
  - Pepper lies close to the player and other dogs.
- Any player action interrupts the idle behaviour instantly. Idle behaviours never block clicks.
- Every behaviour also runs in the phone layout.

## D. Art (dogs)
- **New poses:**
  - `sniff`: nose to the ground, tail out, one front paw slightly lifted, mid-step.
  - `yawn`: sitting, mouth wide open in a yawn, eyes squeezed, tongue curled.
  - `howl`: sitting, head tilted up to the sky, mouth in an "o", eyes closed.
- Same rules as before: 4 legs, H style, outfit and coat support.
