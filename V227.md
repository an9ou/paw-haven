## 1. What the player sees

- A **project board** in the Town Square (a wooden notice board with a paper sheet and a ribbon; fix batch: on the paving in front of the clock tower, below its door, and a "Town projects" button in the Square's row of buttons, `pr.btn`), formerly between the Family Portrait easel and the run
  gate. The paper shows the current project's drawing filling in and a crayon bar. Tap it: the **Town projects** sheet.
- The sheet: Gerald's line ("put in any coins you like, whenever you like; every coin stays in the project"), the current project's drawing
  (the dashed plan, crayoned in with the finished colours from the bottom up to the share paid, a wobbly crayon edge), its name and line, a
  crayon progress bar, "{paid} of {price} coins", the quick buttons **+50, +100, +500, All but 200**, a typed **Any amount** with **Put in**,
  the builds in progress ("ready tomorrow" / "ready in 2 days"), and the list of all four with a chip each: Built, Building, Saving up, Next.
- Four projects, **in order**: Fountain, Bandstand, Sakura Avenue, Lighthouse. Only the one being paid for shows in the town (as a plan, then
  with scaffolding from half paid); later ones are not drawn yet.
- Fully paid: a gold toast, the next project starts at once (paying never waits), and the paid one **builds for 2 Japan days** (the 00:00 JST
  reset, `jstISO()`, as the daily rhythm). It shows with scaffolding; tapping it says when it is ready. Then it is **built for good**: Gerald's
  ribbon-cutting letter arrives once (kept in the mailbox to reread) and the build gets its "something to do".
- When all four are built the sheet says thank you and shows no pay buttons.

## 3. Rules kept

Nothing bad ever happens (no fail state; a build in progress is never undone). No FOMO (projects never leave; the board is always there).
Walks stay the main income (projects only take coins; the small Happiness treats give no coins). Every colour fill stays inside its outline.

## 4. Save (additive, `v227Fields` in `game/src/55_projects.js`)

`S.projects = { i, paid, building: [{ id, ready, paid }], built: [id], letters: [id], treatAt }`
- `i`: index of the project being paid for (0-4; 4 = all paid). `paid`: whole coins in it.
- `building`: paid projects still being built; `ready` = the Japan date (YYYY-MM-DD) it is finished, `paid` = the date it was paid.
- `built`: finished project ids, in order. `letters`: ids whose ribbon-cutting letter was sent (once each; the letter id is
  `pr_ribbon_<id>`, `keep: true`). `treatAt`: game minute of the last Happiness treat.
- `migrate()` and `freshState()` call `v227Fields` (one line each). Bad values are cleaned (index clamped, negative or broken values reset,
  broken building rows dropped). `S.v` stays 1.

## 6. Art (`game/src/55b_projects_art.js`)

Pencil-world props in the world palette and ink (`#5B3D32`), a small seeded wobble per line, each in three stages: `plan` (dashed graphite
outline and two pink planning flags), `half` (the lower part built and coloured, the rest dashed, wooden scaffolding with a ladder; the
lighthouse also a little crane; sakura: young trees tied to stakes) and `done`. Every fill uses the very same path as its outline (the line is
drawn on top), so no fill leaves its outline. The scene layers go through `PawArt.tint` (dusk and night), the lamp glow is a `pa-wb-glow`
group. Kept in the game scope (not a world file) so the build, harness and split need no change; it can move to a world module later.
