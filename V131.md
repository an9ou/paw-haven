# Paw Haven v1.3.1: popups, runner clarity and tutorial, pet beds (laptop only, fast)

This file goes with V13.md and V13b.md. Where they conflict, this file wins.

## 1. Menus become POPUP windows (game)
- **Problem:** the Feed, Play, Care, Walk, Wardrobe, Journal and Map-list trays slide up as wide bottom panels and cover the main screen (see the client's screenshot of the Feed tray).
- **Fix:** every action opens a centred **popup window**.
  - **Look:** a doodle paper card, about 760×460 max, with a washi-tape corner and a pencil border. It has a title, a round × close button, and Esc closes it.
  - **Backdrop:** soft dimmed. The scene stays visible behind it, about 35% dim. Clicking the backdrop closes the popup.
  - **Behaviour:**
    - Only one popup is open at a time.
    - Opening animation is a gentle pop of about 150 ms (reduced-motion: none).
    - The action bar stays visible below the dim, and the active button is highlighted.
  - **Feed:** tap a food card. The popup closes, then the dog walks to the bowl and eats (the existing animation). Remove drag-to-bowl, since a popup covers the bowl.
  - **Play:** the toy cards plus the fetch challenge. Picking one closes the popup and starts the toy or fetch.
  - **Care:** Bath, Nap, Bed (new), Houses, and Go home (when away from home).
  - **Wardrobe and Journal:** become popups too. They can be the largest size, about 980×580. Keep their tabs.
  - **Walk carousel:** keep it as a full-stage screen; it is its own view by design.
  - **Map:** keep the full map scene. The row of place buttons becomes a compact floating chip bar over the map, not a bottom tray.
  - **Shops (Kibble Corner, Boutique, Barkitecture, Sprout Cart):** popups. They can be large.
  - **Small confirms and toasts:** stay as they are.
- **Layout rule:** the scene must be fully visible whenever no popup is open. There are no permanent bottom trays except the action bar.

## 2. Runner: obvious obstacles and a tutorial (mods/walkrun.js)
- **Problem:** "the obstacles are not obvious, please separate them from the background or make them more obvious."
- **Fix: push the background back.**
  - Render the `walkStrip` layers slightly faded and desaturated: CSS `filter: saturate(.65) contrast(.85) brightness(1.05)`, or an equivalent paper-white veil of about 18%. The far layer fades more.
  - The path ground strip stays crisp.
- **Fix: make the obstacles pop.** Each obstacle gets:
  - a bold 3–4px warm-dark outline halo, with a white "sticker" edge around the silhouette
  - a soft drop shadow on the ground
  - a small action badge above it while it approaches: an up arrow for jump, a double up arrow for long jump, a down arrow for duck, in a coloured circle (jump = orange, long jump = red, duck = blue)
  - the hint bubbles from v1.2.1, which stay as they are
- **Fix: collectibles read differently from obstacles.**
  - Coins get a light glow and a gentle bob, and no outline halo.
  - X dig spots get a pulsing dotted ring.
- **Tutorial / intro screen before every walk** (inside the module, before the countdown):
  - The title is the route name, plus a "How to walk" page in sketchbook style.
  - Card 1, Moves: Jump (tap Space / ↑), Long jump (hold Space; clears puddles and mud and keeps you clean), Duck (hold ↓ / S). Each move has a tiny looping doodle of the dog doing it, using its own poses (walk, jump, crouch).
  - Card 2, Obstacles on this route: each obstacle that can appear on this route and in this weather, with its art, name and the action badge (jump, long jump or duck). For example: "Log: jump", "Puddle: long jump", "Branch: duck".
  - Card 3, Treasure: the Nose-o-meter (cold, warm, HOT), sniff spots (clues), dig spots ("X marks a dig spot: press D or tap Dig!"), and digs left (2, or 3 with the Acorn Cap). Only one X hides the treasure.
  - Card 4, Bumps: each bump costs 3 seconds. Coins sit at 3 heights.
  - Buttons: "Let's go!" starts the countdown, and "Next"/"Back" move between cards.
  - A "Skip next time" checkbox. The module reports it through the new optional callback `o.setSkipTutorial(bool)`; the game passes `o.skipTutorial` (a boolean) from its prefs. When it is true, show a compact one-line reminder of the controls during the countdown instead.
  - On the very first walk, the tutorial is always shown.

## 7. Poop and pee (game, art, dogs) with realistic logic
- **When it happens:**
  - Dogs need to go after eating and drinking. A poop comes 30–90 game-minutes after a meal; a pee comes 20–60 game-minutes after drinking water, or every 4–6 game-hours anyway.
  - Use the game clock, which runs at prototype speed, so in practice about once every few real minutes of play. Cap it at 1 active mess per place.
- **Where:**
  - It can happen at any place.
  - Outdoors is normal: no penalty if you clean it up.
  - **Indoors** (`house`) it is an "accident". It only happens when the dog has needed to go for a while and hasn't been outside or on a walk recently, which is realistic potty logic. Taking the dog outside or on a walk resets the need.
- **Animation:**
  - The dog walks to a spot and does a short squat (`squat` pose) for poop. Pee is `leglift` for males, who lift a hind leg, and `squat` for females.
  - A little "…" bubble appears, then a funny line: "Biscuit has made a contribution."
- **Cleaning:**
  - **Poop:** click it to pick it up with a poop bag. Bags are free and unlimited.
  - **Pee:** click it to flush it with the water bottle (`icon('water-spray')`), with a splash and a sparkle.
  - Reward: +2 Bond and +3 coins for cleaning in a public place ("Good citizen!"), +1 Bond at home.
- **If it is not cleaned:**
  - Stink lines and flies appear after a while. The place's Cleanliness drains slowly (−1 every 10 game-minutes per mess), and the dog's Happiness drops a little at home.
  - In public places, an NPC comment appears after a while: "Somebody didn't scoop!"
  - Messes persist per place in the save until cleaned. Max 1 per place keeps it light.
- **Art (world B, G doodle style, tasteful and cute, never gross):**
  - `prop('poop', {fresh: bool})`, viewBox 0 0 60 50: a cute doodle swirl with optional stink lines.
  - `prop('pee')`, viewBox 0 0 100 30: a pale yellow puddle with a shine.
  - `prop('flies')`, viewBox 0 0 60 40: two tiny doodle flies with dotted paths.
  - `icon('poop-bag')`: a little green bag with a paw print.
  - `icon('water-spray')`: a small water bottle with a squirt.
- **Dogs:** new poses `squat` (hind legs bent low, tail up, a concentrating face) and `leglift` (one hind leg raised to the side, standing on 3 with 4 legs still drawn, a casual face). Both use H style, with the 4-leg anatomy check.
- **Journal Profile:** a "Potty stats" line, for example "Scooped: 12. Accidents indoors: 1." Keep it light-hearted.
