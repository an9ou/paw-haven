# Paw Haven v1.2.1, "Places" update: contract (laptop only, fast)

Read this with V12.md, which still applies except where this file overrides it.

## Client feedback being fixed
1. **Ambience too loud.** Music is good, but the weather ambience (rain and so on) is too loud. It must start at a lower default volume.
2. **Rain covers the map.** The rain overlay draws over the town map. The map must show NO weather or time effects at all: no particles, no tint, and the default day art.
3. **Walk and Map overlap, and so do Shop and Market Street.**
   - **Walk:** picking a walk route moves into a route **carousel**, then the runner game opens in its own view.
   - **Map:** used to TAKE THE DOG somewhere to hang out (feed, play, pet). It is no longer the walk picker.
   - **Shop vs Market Street:** both currently lead to the shops. Simplify the buttons so there is one way in.
4. **More places to hang out.** The dog can stay in more places than the yard: inside the house, at the park, by the riverside, and so on.
5. **Runner speed.** The runner's background scrolls too fast to read the instructions. Slow it down. Hitting an obstacle now takes time off the walk clock.
6. **Next update.** Prepare v1.3 (Garden & Kitchen), as a plan only.

## Navigation (final)
- **Action bar:** `Feed · Play · Care · Walk · Wardrobe · Journal · Map`. That is 7 buttons. The **Shop** and **Home** buttons are REMOVED.
- **Shops:** reached only through Map → Market Street, where you tap a shopfront. The dog comes along.
- **Location chip:** the HUD shows a pencil pin plus the current place name, next to the clock and weather chip.

- **Travel.**
  - Map: click a place. A short "on the way" doodle card plays (about 1.2 s) and the dog trots across. Then the place becomes the dog's current location, and it is saved.
  - Cost: travel costs −2 Energy.
  - The first visit to each place each day gives +5 Happiness.
  - Locked places get a "nope" kazoo plus the Bond requirement.
- **Actions at every place:** Feed (a portable bowl at that place's bowl spot), Play (toys and fetch), Pet (rub), Wardrobe, Journal.
- **Care (Bath, Nap)** works only at `yard` and `house`. Anywhere else, the Care tray says "Bath and naps happen at home" and offers a "Go home" button (travel to the yard).
- **Weather and time** apply to every outdoor place: the scene variant plus particles.
  - `house` gets NO particles. Rain shows on its windows only, through the scene art.
  - `map` gets NO particles, no tint and the default art.
- **Music:** `PawAudio.setContext({place: <place id>, area, time, weather})`. For park, river, woods and beach as hangouts, pass `place` as the place id. Walks still use `place: 'walk'`.

## Walk flow
- **Route carousel.** The Walk button opens a full-stage carousel of route cards: park, river, woods, beach. Each card shows:
  - a preview window cut from `PawArt.walkStrip(area, {time, weather})`
  - the route name, length and difficulty (1–4 paw prints)
  - how many treasures there are still unfound, and the map-piece status
  - the weather and time it will run in
  - a lock with its Bond level, if locked

  Navigate with ← → keys or the arrow buttons, or drag. "Start walk" opens the runner.
- **Runner view.** The runner runs in its own full view: the action bar is hidden, and the HUD is reduced to the dog name and coins.
- **After the walk.** The results card shows, then the player returns to the place where the dog was before the walk.

## Runner changes (mods/walkrun.js)
- **Slower world speed:** about 30% slower base speeds. Park ramps 190→225, river 195→235, woods 200→240, beach 210→250, in the module's own units. Re-tune the obstacle spacing so the gaps in seconds stay comfortable.
  - The jump and duck physics stay as they are, so jumps now cover more ground.
  - Check that a held long jump still clears a puddle comfortably at the new speed. Make the puddles a bit narrower, or the jump floatier, if needed.
- **Readable instructions:**
  - Hint bubbles are larger (at least 22px text) and appear earlier (at least 2 s before the obstacle), and they stay attached to the obstacle.
  - The first time each obstacle type appears in a walk, play a gentle slow-motion (×0.5 for about 1 s) when the hint shows.
  - The controls legend at the bottom is easy to read: at least 16px text, on a paper chip.
- **Hit penalty:** hitting an obstacle takes **−3 seconds** off the remaining walk time.
  - It shows a red pencil "−3s" popup near the timer, flashes the timer bar, and makes a bonk.
  - There is no further time penalty during the invulnerability blink.
  - If the time reaches 0, the walk ends normally (onEnd).
  - Report it as `r.timeLost` (seconds). Keep everything else in `r` unchanged.

## Audio changes (mods/pawaudio.js)
- **Default volumes:** master 0.8, music 0.5, sfx 0.8, **ambience 0.25** (was 0.4).
- **Quieter weather beds:** lower the rain bed by about 5 dB and the wind and snow bed by about 3 dB relative to before.
- **New places:** `house` gets cozy indoor music, with the outside ambience muffled (rain on the window at night). `park`, `river`, `woods` and `beach` used as hangouts get the yard-style music, with that area's ambience (birds, stream, wind and leaves, waves).
- **Game prefs:** if the saved prefs still hold the old default ambience (0.5) and the player never moved that slider, migrate it to 0.25.
